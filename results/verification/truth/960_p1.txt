A Regular Meeting of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday, October 4,
2021, at 6:00 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street Potsdam, NY.

PRESENT: Mayor Tischler
Trustees McKenna, Jacobs-Wilke & Lee

ALSO PRESENT: Lori Queor, Village Clerk/Registrar
Cara Adams, Treasurer
Jim Corbett, DPW Superintendent
Fred Hanss, Planning Director
Mark Murray, Police Chief

Mayor Tischler opened the meeting at 6:03 P.M and pointed out the location of the exits on the
north and east walls of the board room. He advised that the board room has a capacity for 109
people.

The Mayor then took the roll and stated that all members were present except for Trustee Warr.

The Mayor then stated that the following sets of minutes had been submitted for Board
acceptance:

September 20, 2021 Regular Meeting 6:00 p.m.
September 20, 2021 Executive Session 6:30 p.m.

Motion was made by Trustee Lee and Seconded by Trustee Jacobs-Wilke to accept the minutes
as submitted. All were in Favor — None Opposed — Motion Carried.

Mayor’s comments: Covid is still prevalent. Wear a mask, social distance, avoid large
gatherings and get the vaccine if you are able to.

Trustee’s Comments:
Trustee Warr — Not present.

Trustee McKenna — Echo Mayor’s comments. Consider getting vaccine if you can. Alex and I
will be starting back up the Police Advisory Committee as soon as we can.

Trustee Jacobs-Wilke — Indigenous People Day is coming up and we are thankful for our ability
to be on these lands. National Walk/Bike to school day is coming up and it is wonderful that the
Village of Potsdam can ensure the safest routes to school possible for walkers and bikers.
Trustee Lee — None

Administrator’s Comments: Not present.

DPW Superintendent’s Report: Superintendent Corbett provided a written report which is on file
with the Village Clerk. He then went over his report with the board.
