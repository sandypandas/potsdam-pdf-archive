A Regular Meeting of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday, June 19,
2017 at 7:00 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, New York.

                      PRESENT:               Mayor Tischler
                                             Trustees Lee & Sheehan

                      ALSO PRESENT:          Lori S. Queor, Village Clerk/Treasurer
                                             Greg Thompson, Administrator
                                             Fred Hanss, Planning Director
                                             Mark Murray, Police Chief
                                             Mimi VanDeusen, Museum Director
                                             Matt Seymour, Police Officer

Mayor Tischler opened the meeting at 7:00 P.M., and stated that all members were present with
the exception of Trustee Warr and Trustee Garner.

Mayor Tischler then stated that minutes could not be accepted at this time due to a lack of board
members who were present at the meetings.

Mayor’s comments: Mayor Tischler stated that he will be doing a proclamation at the Summer
Festival on July 15, 2017 proclaiming that day “Sergi’s Day” in celebration of Serg’s 50 years in
the community.

Trustee’s Comments: Trustee Lee and Trustee Sheehan both stated that they had no comments
at this time.

Administrator’s Comments: Administrator Thompson stated that he wanted to welcome
new/returning board member Abby Lee back to the board. He then reported on the following:

-      Hydro Dams – NYGATS – Clarkson University would like to take over and the biggest
       issue is if we turn it over to them we would no longer be the primary. Will forward the
       request to Village Attorney, Andy Silver, for his legal review and recommendation.
-      East Dam – working group hasn’t met recently. Waiting on the results of a grant
       application before we move forward.
-      AWOS – grant for $244,000 received will be moving forward on the replacement.
-      National Grid rates going up – met with Rich Burns of National Grid, impact to the
       Village will be about a 10% increase.
-      Twinstate Technologies – IT switch over has been smooth. Got quotes for a contract.
       Recommend we go with the $5,000 prepaid option. Board agreed.
-      Water Tank – waiting on the 3rd party inspection by Atlantic Testing Laboratories.
-      WWTP upgrades – waiting on final approvals from OCR – we are a bit delayed.
-      Crosstown Canal – met with Marcellus Construction who is the contactor and they will
       be on site within two weeks and should take 3-4 weeks to complete.
