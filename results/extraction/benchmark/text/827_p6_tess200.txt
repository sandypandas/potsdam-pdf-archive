Trustee Lee Moved and Trustee Jacobs-Wilke Seconded a resolution as follows: WHEREAS, the Declaration of Independence defined the United States of America as a democracy based on the unalienable rights of life, liberty, and the pursuit of happiness, and government by the consent of the people; and the 14th Amendment instilled equality of the races into the U.S. Constitution; and WHEREAS, from slavery to Jim Crow laws to the modern criminal justice system, Black people in this country have been brutalized and dehumanized for centuries; and WHEREAS, the struggles of Black people have been highlighted most recently by alarming findings that Black Americans are dying from COVID-19 at a much higher rate compared to white Americans due to systemic, structural racism; and WHEREAS, in the 21st Century, Black people in America continue to live in fear of losing their lives at the hands of law enforcement or members of white supremacist groups; and WHEREAS, on May 25, 2020, 46-year-old father of two, George Floyd, pleaded for his life while handcuffed and on the ground, while a police officer pushed his knee into George Floyd's throat for over nine minutes, ultimately killing George Floyd; and WHEREAS, racism and death continue to plague our country, despite many pleas for change; and WHEREAS, on June 1, 2020, nearly one thousand community members peacefully marched through the Village of Potsdam in protest of these acts; and WHEREAS, on June 10, 2020, hundreds more community members peacefully marched through the Village of Potsdam, joined by Mayor Tischler, Chief Murray and members of the Village Board; and WHEREAS, "Equal Justice Under the Law" is a phrase engraved on a pediment in front of the United States Supreme Court, the Village Board believes all law enforcement officials should work toward making this a reality; NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that the Potsdam Village Board condemns the killing of George Floyd, and condemns any and all acts of racism and discrimination; and LET US RESOLVE, individually, to search our own hearts and to decide, in a moral crisis - which in truth is always what we should do, what we can do, and determine what we will do; and BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Potsdam Village Board aims to make the Village of Potsdam an even more welcoming, inclusive, and safe community to all, regardless of race or ethnicity.
