AGENDA - REGULAR MEETING 3 VILLAGE OF POTSDAM BOARD OF TRUSTEES JANUARY 9, 2017 - 7:00 P.M. Trustee Moved and Trustee Seconded a resolution approving the following Capital Assets Policy: Village of Potsdam Capital Assets Policy It is the responsibility of the Trustees and Administration of the Village of Potsdam to maintain and replace the Capital Assets of the Village in order to best expend monies put forth by the tax payers of the Village of Potsdam. Capital Assets are those fixed assets with significant useful lives. The Village of Potsdam is committed to maintaining and improving all of its capital assets. This commitment magnifies the importance of a long term plan to prioritize and justify the necessary expenditures, develop a time table for proposed repairs/replacements, and to strategize payment methods/opportunities. The Multi-year Capital Assets Plan will be developed by a Committee composed of the Mayor and Village Trustees, Village Administrator, Treasurer, and Department Heads. Public meetings/hearings will be held for the input of the general public. The Multi-year Capital Assets Plan will be for a period of no less than 3 years and no more than 5 years. Said plan is to be evaluated and updated on a one year cycle. The Multi-year Capital Assets Plan will be developed according to a six step plan. Step one shall be the development of policy and objectives. Step two will consist of creating an inventory of all assets belonging to the Village of Potsdam and conditions of such assets. During step three, it shall become the responsibility of the Board and Staff, with public input, to prioritize projects and all expenditures related to each. Step four will involve assessing budgetary impacts related to each project respectively. Step five shall be a time for discussing and strategizing capital acquisition. The final step, step six, is the actual implementation of the Capital Assets Plan along- side the yearly budget. It shall be the objective of this plan to provide the Village of Potsdam with a long term plan for the improvement and replacement of assets while providing guidance during budget preparation. It is the belief of the Trustees of the Village of Potsdam that the implementation of such a plan will assist in our continued goals of correcting current and preventing future financial stress.
