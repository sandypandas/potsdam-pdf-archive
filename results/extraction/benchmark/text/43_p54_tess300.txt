Driveway Location 1. Opposed driveways are not allowed unless a signal is provided at that location. 2. Cross road driveways creating left-turn overlaps shall be soaced a minimum of 60 feet apart, measured along the edge of right-of-way. 3. A driveway may not unduly constrain the location or safe and functional use of driveways at abutting properties. Driveway locations can significantly detract from access to abutting or opposed driveways. These impacts are often manifest by lines of vehicles waiting to turn left into a business and by long delays in entering or leaving businesses. Inappropriate driveway locations are also implicated in high accident locations; generally because they increase the number of conflict points in a relatively small area. The techniques outlined above and the recommended spacing contained in Figure 4-3 address the most common problems. Driveway Design 1. Driveways shall be designed to provide for the safe and efficient movement of traffic between the roadway and the site, and to eliminate the potential for the queuing of vehicles along the roadway due to congestion in or at the driveway.
