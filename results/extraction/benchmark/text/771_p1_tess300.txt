The Regular Meeting of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday, November 4, 2019 at 6:00 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, New York. PRESENT: Mayor Tischler Trustees Warr, McKenna & Goliber ALSO PRESENT: - Lori S. Queor, Village Clerk/Treasurer Jim Corbett, DPW Superintendent Mark Murray, Police Chief Fred Hanss, Planning Director Trey Smutz, Recreation Director Mimi VanDeusen, Museum Director Mayor Tischler opened the meeting at 6:00 P.M., pointed out the exits on the North and East Walls and advised that the board room has the capacity for 109 people. The Mayor then stated that all members were present with the exception of Trustee Lee. The Mayor then stated that the following sets of minutes had been submitted for Board acceptance: October 21, 2019 Regular Meeting 6:00 p.m. Motion was made by Trustee Goliber and Seconded by Trustee McKenna to accept the minutes as submitted. All were in favor - none opposed - motion carried. Mayor's comments: Thank you to the DPW, Fire Dept. and Police Dept. for all their hard work this week with the weather event. Thank you to Cindy Goliber for her work as a Trustee, this is the last meeting she and I will both be at and I appreciate all you have done. Trustee's Comments: Trustee Warr - Big congrats on the DRI award, looking forward to seeing what the money is used for. Trustee McKenna - Echo congrats to everyone on the DRI award and thank you to Cindy. Trustee Goliber - One of the proudest days in Potsdam ever getting the 10 million dollar DRI. Congrats to everyone! Administrator's Comments: Administrator Thompson was not present at the meeting. Recreation Director's Report: Director Trey Smutz provided a written report which is on file with the Village Clerk. He then went over the report with the board. Planning Director's Report: Director Fred Hanss provided a written report which is on file with the Village Clerk. He then went over the report with the board.
