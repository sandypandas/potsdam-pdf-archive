A Regular Meeting of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Tuesday, September 7, 2021, at 6:00 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street Potsdam, NY. PRESENT: Mayor Tischler Trustees Warr, McKenna, Jacobs-Wilke & Lee ALSO PRESENT: Lori Queor, Village Clerk/Registrar Cara Adams, Treasurer Greg Thompson, Administrator Jim Corbett, DPW Superintendent Fred Hanss, Planning Director Trey Smutz, Recreation Director Mark Murray, Police Chief Mayor Tischler opened the meeting at 6:54 P.M and pointed out the location of the exits on the north and east walls of the board room. He advised that the board room has a capacity for 109 people. The Mayor then took the roll and stated that all members were present. The Mayor then stated that the following sets of minutes had been submitted for Board acceptance: August 11, 2021 Special Meeting 3:30 p.m. August 11, 2021 Executive Session 3:32 p.m. August 16, 2021 Regular Meeting 4:30 p.m. Motion was made by Trustee Lee and Seconded by Trustee Jacobs-Wilke to accept the minutes as submitted. All were in Favor - None Opposed - Motion Carried. Mayor's comments: Covid is coming back stronger than ever. Wear masks, social distance and get vaccinated. Trustee's Comments: Trustee Warr- None Trustee McKenna - Thank you to everyone who made Porch Fest a huge success again this year. Would like to use this opportunity to ask Brent from St. Lawrence Health some questions that were asked in the Public Hearing so that we can get answers to those questions. Has there been any discussion on a multi-story parking garage? Brent stated that there is no plan due to costs, it would be very expensive to construct and maintain.
