Finance b dge The Village of Potsdam budget, including the police department budget, is available online at vi.potsdam.ny.us. Each year, public sessions for the budget are held and the public is invited to comment on the fiscal priorities of the municipality, including for the Potsdam Police Department. The vast majority of the police budget goes to salaries of the employees, including Police Chief, Lieutenant, Sergeants, patrol officers, civilian dispatchers, Special Patrol Officer assigned to the Potsdam Central School District, and crossing guard. The Police building lease, police vehicles and equipment are the next biggest budget items. Chief of Police Mark Murray has limited discretionary spending for specialized training or equipment.
