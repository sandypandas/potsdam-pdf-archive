A Public Hearing of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday, May 15, 2023 at 4:15 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, NY. PRESENT: Mayor Tischler Trustees Warr, Tirion, Jacobs-Wilke, Lee ALSO PRESENT: Georgine Scott, Village Clerk/Registrar in Training Lori Queor, Village Clerk/Registrar Gregory Thompson, Village Administrator Isabelle Gates-Schult, Treasurer Trey Smutz, Recreation Director Lisa Newby, Code Enforcement Officer Fred Hanss, Planning Director Mark Murray, Chief of Police Mayor Tischler opened the hearing at 4:15 P.M. He stated that this public hearing is to receive public comments on a proposed local law to amend Chapter 173, Water, Article Il, Water Rents, Section 173-13 of the Village of Potsdam Municipal Code. He took the roll stating that all members were present. Village Clerk in Training, Georgine Scott, then read the Legal Notice as published. The Mayor then asked for public comments. No public comments were heard. Hearing no public comments, the Mayor declared the hearing closed at 4:19 P.M. Respectfully submitted, Georgine Scott Village Clerk
