Trustee Warr Moved and Trustee Sheehan Seconded a resolution as follows: LOCAL LAW #8-2017 Section 1. Purpose. The Purpose of this Local Law is to Amend Chapter 180, Zoning, Article III, Section 180-6 Zoning Map of the Village of Potsdam Municipal Code. Section 2. Amendment. The zoning map shall be amended to change the zone designation from School (SCH) to Business District (B-1) for the property located at 119 Maple Street - Tax Map #64.066-4-11.1, Potsdam, NY. Section 3. Enactment. This Local Law shall become effective immediately upon the notification of the filing of same with the Secretary of State.
