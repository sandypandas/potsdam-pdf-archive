§125-5. Prohibited Acts. The deposit, accumulation, display and/or outdoor storage of junk, junk appliances, junk furniture, junk mobile homes, junk motor vehicles, garbage collectively hereinafter ("Junk") regardless of quantity, is hereby prohibited within sight of persons traveling the public highways or within sight of neighboring property/properties and/or business concerns. The provisions of this law shall also be applicable to conditions existing at the time of enactment. §125-6. Exclusions. The provisions of §125-5 ("Prohibited Acts") shall not apply to the storage or placement on the premises of the following material: 1. Cut and/or chopped wood intended for consumption in a wood burning stove, furnace or fireplace located in a building on the property. 2. Lawn, yard and/or garden implements. 3. Lawn and patio furniture. 4. Farm machinery upon property zoned for agricultural use. 5. Operable garden and yard machinery and apparatus used on the premises. 6. Standing fences. 7. Hoses and sprinklers used for watering lawns and/or gardens. 8. Storage or placement and accumulation of materials and/or vehicles in connection with a commercial operation duly conducted on the premises where such storage, placement and accumulation is expressly permitted and/or licensed by the laws of the Village of Potsdam. 9. Construction materials and equipment used for the construction or renovation of a building on the premises for which a building permit has been issued. 10. Unlicensed vehicle(s) owned or registered to a member of the U.S. Armed Forces while on deployment. §125-7. Administration and Enforcement. A. Enforcement. This law may be enforced by the Village Code Enforcement Officer, the Village police or by any individual duly appointed by the Village Board.
