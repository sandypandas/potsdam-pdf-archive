A Public Hearing of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday, July 20,
2020 at 4:15 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, NY.
PRESENT:

Deputy Mayor Warr
Trustees McKenna & Jacobs-Wilke
Mayor Tischler via Zoom video

ALSO PRESENT:

Lori S. Queor, Clerk/Treasurer
Greg Thompson, Administrator

Deputy Mayor Warr opened the hearing at 4:19 p.m. He stated that this public hearing is to
receive public comments on a proposed Local Law to allow chickens in within the Village of
Potsdam limits. He took the roll stating that all members were present except for Trustee Lee
and that Mayor Tischler was participating via Zoom video conferencing.
Village Clerk, Lori Queor, then read the Legal Notice as published.
Public Comments:
Rose Revezzi of 7 Barclay Street spoke in favor of allowing chickens in the Village of Potsdam.
She suggested the village not make the rules too strict.
Susan Powers of 17 Spring Street spoke in favor of allowing chickens in the Village of Potsdam.
She provided the Village Clerk with a written copy of her statement which is on file with the
clerk.
Hearing no further public comments, the Deputy Mayor then closed the hearing at 4:29 P.M.

Respectfully submitted,

Lori S. Queor
Village Clerk
