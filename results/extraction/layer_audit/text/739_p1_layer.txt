A Public Hearing of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday, April 15,
2019 at 5:48 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, New York.
PRESENT:

Mayor Tischler
Trustees Warr, McKenna, Lee & Goliber

ALSO PRESENT:

Lori S. Queor, Clerk/Treasurer
Jim Corbett, DPW Superintendent
Trey Smutz, Recreation Director

Mayor Tischler opened the hearing at 5:48 p.m. He stated that this public hearing is to receive
public comments on a proposed Local Law to amend Chapter 153, Taxation Article IX Tax Cap
Override. He took the roll stating that all members were present.
Village Clerk, Lori Queor, then read the Legal Notice as published.
Public Comments: None
Hearing no public comments, the Mayor then closed the hearing at 5:49 P.M.

Respectfully submitted,

Lori S. Queor
Village Clerk
