Trustee Goliber Moved and Trustee McKenna Seconded a resolution approving
the taxi rates for inside the Village of Potsdam limits of Potsdam Madstop Taxi for the
period of May 1, 2018 through April 30, 2019 as follows:

$7.00 for 1 passenger

$1.00 for each additional passenger

$4.00 for each additional stop - $1.00 per minute after 5 minutes

$10.00 for 1 passenger for 12 p.m. to 6 a.m.

$1.00 for each additional passenger for 12 p.m. to 6 a.m.

$8.00 for SUNY Potsdam, Medical vouchers

$100.00 per hour for moving boxes, totes, bines, etc. minimum of $50.00

Ayes 4 (Mayor Tischler) Nays 0
