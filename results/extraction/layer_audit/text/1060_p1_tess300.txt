A Public Hearing of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Tuesday, September
19, 2022 at 5:50 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, NY.

PRESENT: Mayor Tischler
Trustees Warr, Tirion, Jacobs-Wilke & Lee

ALSO PRESENT: Lori S. Queor, Village Clerk
Isabelle Gates-Shult, Treasurer
Fred Hanss, Planning Director

Mayor Tischler opened the hearing at 6:05 P.M. He stated that this public hearing is to

receive public comments on a proposed application to be submitted on behalf of Trezza Realty,
LLC for funding through the Restore New York Communities Initiative for the Scanlon Building
Redevelopment Project.

He took the roll stating that all members were present.

Village Clerk, Lori Queor, then read the Legal Notice as published.

The Mayor then asked for public comments. Brooks Washburn, Architect for the project
addressed the board and gave an overview of the project. Larry Hazen, property owner stated it
is a beautiful building with tremendous potential and at this point they are not sure who will be
occupying the building. Planning Director Fred Hanss stated that public comments will be taken

in writing to the Village Clerk up until September 30" at 2:00 p.m.

Hearing no further public comments, the Mayor declared the hearing closed at 6:14 P.M.

Respectfully submitted,

Lori S. Queor
Village Clerk
