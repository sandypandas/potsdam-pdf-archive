A Public Hearing of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday, June 17,
2019 at 4:20 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, New York.

PRESENT: Mayor Tischler
Trustees Warr, Lee & Goliber

ALSO PRESENT: Lori S. Queor, Clerk/Treasurer
Jim Corbett, DPW Superintendent
Greg Thompson, Administrator
Lisa Newby, Code Officer

Mayor Tischler opened the hearing at 4:20 p.m. He stated that this public hearing is to receive
public comments on a proposed Local Law to amend Chapter 180 Zoning, to change the zone
designation from B-1to SCH for the property located at 42 Maple Street. He took the roll
stating that all members were present with the exception of Trustee McKenna.

Village Clerk, Lori Queor, then read the Legal Notice as published.

Public Comments: Trustee Warr asked if they can do what they want there now why they need a
zone change that if they leave the site will have to be changed back. Administrator Thompson
stated that because some things they would do there would require a special use permit of the
zone is not changed.

Ian Hazen, Director of Facilities stated that the property is contiguous to their current SCH zone,
the property is a better fit than their previous plan to build on the former Robar property, and it is
next to the Stewarts where they fuel up.

Trustee Goliber asked if the property does not meet their needs in the future would they be
buying more property and taking it off the tax rolls? Mr. Hazen stated that this facility will serve
their needs for many years and that the will be vacating the Old Snell building downtown that
will go back on the tax rolls. He stated it is basically a swap.

Hearing no further public comments, the Mayor then closed the hearing at 4:26 P.M.

Respectfully submitted,

Lori S. Queor
Village Clerk
