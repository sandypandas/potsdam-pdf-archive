An Executive Session of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday, January
6, 2020 at 6:55 p.m., Civic Center, Park Street, Potsdam, New York.

PRESENT: Mayor Tischler
Trustees Warr, McKenna, Jacobs-Wilke & Lee
Administrator Thompson
The Board of Trustees, upon a resolution offered by Trustee Lee and seconded by Trustee
Jacobs-Wilke went into Executive Session for the discussion of the employment history of
particular persons at 6:55 p.m.

Discussion of same was had; no formal vote or action was taken in Execution Session.

Upon a resolution offered by Trustee Lee and seconded by Trustee Warr the Executive Session
was adjourned at 7:43 p.m.

The undersigned was not in attendance at the Executive Session. Information is provided by
Mayor Tischler.

Respectfully submitted,

Lori S. Queor
