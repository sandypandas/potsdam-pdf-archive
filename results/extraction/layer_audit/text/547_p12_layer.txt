(Trustee Warr)

Trustee Warr Moved and Trustee Hopke Seconded a resolution formally adopting a
Section 3 Policy Plan required to document the Village of Potsdam's intent to ensure that
low/moderate income residents of the Village of Potsdam and firms headquartered with
in St. Lawrence County, NY have access to training, employment and contract
opportunities resulting from projects funded in whole or in part by the US Dept. of
Housing and Urban Development, including Small Cities Community Development
Block Grant and the HOME Investment Partnership Program. Be it further resolved that
a copy of this plan be included in any and all applicable notice to bidders, bid documents
and as addendum to covered contracts.
