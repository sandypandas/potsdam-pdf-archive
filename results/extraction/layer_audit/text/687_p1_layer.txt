The Annual Meeting of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday,
December 3, 2018 at 6:00 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, New
York.
PRESENT:

Mayor Tischler
Trustees Warr, McKenna, Lee & Goliber

ALSO PRESENT:

Greg Thompson, Administrator
Lori Queor, Village Clerk/Treasurer
Jim Corbett, DPW Superintendent
Lisa Newby, Code Officer
Mark Murray, Police Chief
Fred Hanss, Planning Director
Trey Smutz, Recreation Director

Mayor Tischler opened the meeting at 6:00 P.M., pointed out the exits on the North and East
Walls and advised that the board room has the capacity for 109 people.
The Mayor then stated that all members were present.
The Mayor then stated that the following sets of minutes had been submitted for Board
acceptance:

Motion was made by Trustee Goliber and Seconded by Trustee Lee to accept the minutes as
submitted. All were in favor - none opposed - motion carried.
Mayor's comments: Lights are on downtown, thank you to the DPW. Have a very safe and
happy holiday season.
Trustee's Comments: Trustee Warr stated that he had no comments. Trustee McKenna echoed
the Mayor's comments and stated that the annual lighting of the Plumb tree went well. Trustee
Lee stated that the museum did a good job with their Christmas exhibit. Trustee Goliber stated
that this is her first meeting since she was elected to the one year term and she is looking forward
to working for and with everyone. Mayor Tischler congratulated Trustee Goliber.
Administrator's Comments: Administrator Thompson provided a written report to the board
which is on file with the Village Clerk. He then went over the report with the board.
