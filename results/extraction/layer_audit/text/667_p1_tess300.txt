A Joint Special Meeting of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees and the Town of Potsdam
Council was held on Wednesday, October 23, 2018 at 6:00 P.M., in the Town Offices, Elm
Street, Potsdam, New York.

PRESENT: Mayor Reinhold Tischler
Trustees McKenna & Lee

ALSO PRESENT: Lori S. Queor, Village Clerk/Treasurer
Greg Thompson, Village Administrator
Trey Smutz, Recreation Director
Ann Carvill, Town Supervisor
Rose Rivezzi, Town Councilperson
James Grant, Town Councilperson
Sarah Lister, Town Councilperson
Judy Rich, Town Councilperson
Cindy Goliber, Town Clerk

Mayor Tischler opened the meeting at 6:00 P.M., and took the roll and stated that all members
were present with the exception of Trustee Warr. Trustee Goliber stated that she is at the
meeting but as the Town Clerk not as a Trustee.
NEW BUSINESS:
Discussion of the recreation budget for the Town Calendar Year of 2019 and the Village's Fiscal
Year June 1, 2019 to May 31, 2020 was had by both boards.

Trustee McKenna Moved and Trustee Lee Seconded a resolution to adjourn the Special

Meeting of The Village Board of Trustees held on October 23, 2018 at 6:36 p.m.

Ayes 3 (Mayor Tischler ) Nays 0
(Trustee McKenna & Lee)

Respectfully submitted,
