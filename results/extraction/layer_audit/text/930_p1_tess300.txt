A Special Meeting of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday, July 12,
2021 at 2:30 p.m., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, New York.

PRESENT: Mayor Reinhold Tischler
Trustees Warr, McKenna, Jacobs-Wilke & Lee

ALSO PRESENT: Lori S. Queor, Village Clerk/Registrar
Greg Thompson, Village Administrator

Mayor Tischler opened the meeting at 2:32 p.m., and took the roll and stated that all members
were present.

NEW BUSINESS:

Trustee Lee Moved and Trustee Jacobs-Wilke Seconded a resolution to go into Executive
Session for the discussion of the employment history of particular persons at 2:33 p.m.

Ayes 5 (Mayor Tischler) Nays 0
(Trustee Warr, McKenna, Jacobs-Wilke
& Lee)

Trustee Warr Moved and Trustee Jacobs-Wilke Seconded a resolution to adjourn the
Executive Session at 3:42 p.m.

Ayes 5 (Mayor Tischler) Nays 0
(Trustee Warr, McKenna, Jacobs-Wilke
& Lee)

Trustee Warr Moved and Trustee Lee Seconded a resolution to adjourn the Special
Meeting of July 12, 2021 at 3:43 p.m.

Ayes 5 (Mayor Tischler) Nays 0

(Trustee Warr, McKenna, Jacobs-Wilke
& Lee)

Respectfully submitted,

Lori S. Queor
Village Clerk
