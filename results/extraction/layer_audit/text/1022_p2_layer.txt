Public Comment: Ed Cool, Chief of the Potsdam Volunteer Fire Company addressed the board
regarding calls that the Parishville Rescue Squad has been taking. He stated that because they
are a Fire District, they get all their funding from the taxpayers and cannot bill insurances. He
stated there is a law trying to be passed that would allow them to bill insurances but they have
been trying to get the law passed for the last 20 years or so. He further stated that they are
getting slack from the taxpayers because out of the 719 calls they went on this year, 277 were to
Potsdam. Mr. Cool said that he has stated to the taxpayers that they are in the County Mutual
Aid Program and providing services to Potsdam is mutual aid. He then went on to state that he is
not trying to complain but would like the Board to see if there is any way they could help out.
He also informed the board that he is going to the next Town of Potsdam Board meeting to see if
there is anything they can do to help also.
Tom Demo, Town Councilman for Parishville then address the board. He stated that he would
ask the Village Board to look into and maybe there is something the board can help them with.
He stated that they would be willing to sit down with the Village, Town and Rescue Squad to see
if there is something that can be done.
Mayor Tischler stated that he would be willing to meet and have a discussion.
