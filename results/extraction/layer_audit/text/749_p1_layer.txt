A Public Hearing of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday, July 15,
2019 at 4:15 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, New York.
PRESENT:

Deputy Mayor Warr
Trustees Lee & Goliber

ALSO PRESENT:

Lori S. Queor, Clerk/Treasurer
Jim Corbett, DPW Superintendent
Greg Thompson, Administrator
Lisa Newby, Code Officer
Trey Smutz, Recreation Director

Deputy Mayor Warr opened the hearing at 4:15 p.m. He stated that this public hearing is to
receive public comments on a proposed Local Law to amend Chapter 35 Personnel to amend
Article I, Section 35-6 Vacations Item #D to add the Museum Curator/Director and remove the
Chief Water/Hydro Plant Operator and Economic Developer.
Village Clerk, Lori Queor, then read the Legal Notice as published.
Public Comments: None
Hearing no public comments, the Deputy Mayor then closed the hearing at 4:17 p.m.

Respectfully submitted,

Lori S. Queor
Village Clerk
