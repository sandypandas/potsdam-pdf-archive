Trustee Goliber Moved and Trustee Lee Seconded a resolution to enter into a Legal
Services Agreement with Barclay-Damon to provide legal services in connection with the
application submitted to the St. Lawrence County Industrial Development Agency for a
Payment-In-Lieu-Of -Taxes (PILOT ) Agreement submitted by the Vecino Group for the
gut renovation of Old Snell Hall and conversion to a state and federally assisted lowincome housing project. Further, that the Village Board of Trustees requires that as a
condition of consideration of the Vecino Group's application for a PILOT Agreement,
that the Vecino Group pay for any and all legal services provided to the Village of
Potsdam by the law firm of Barclay-Damon; and Further Resolved that the Vecino Group
place funds in escrow with one of the local taxing jurisdictions against which Barclay Damon may submit invoices for services rendered.
