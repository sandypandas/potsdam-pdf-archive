A Special Meeting of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Friday, October 19,
2018 at 4:15 p.m., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, New York.

PRESENT: Mayor Reinhold Tischler
Trustees Warr, McKenna, Lee & Goliber

ALSO PRESENT: Greg Thompson, Village Administrator
Lori S. Queor, Village Clerk/Treasurer

Mayor Tischler opened the meeting at 4:15 p.m., and took the roll and stated that all members
were present.

He then asked Planning Director Fred Hanss to give the board a brief explanation of the East
Dam Hydro Project and how the resolution below came about. Mr. Hanss provided a brief
explanation to the board.

NEW BUSINESS:

Trustee Lee Moved and Trustee Goliber Seconded a resolution authorizing the Village of
Potsdam to enter into an engineering services agreement with Hatch Associates
Consulting Inc. to provide engineering services to the Village of Potsdam for the East
Dam Hydro-Electric Generator Plant Renovation Project, for the Base Bid and Alternate
Costs 1, 2, 3 and 4; not to include Alternative 4.4.11 Tailrace Improvements and 4.6
Interconnection; and, further authorizing the Mayor or in his absence the Deputy Mayor
to execute any and all contract documents with Hatch Associates.

Ayes 5 (Mayor Tischler) Nays 0
(Trustee Warr, McKenna, Lee
& Goliber)

Trustee Goliber Moved and Trustee Lee Seconded a resolution to adjourn the Special
Meeting of October 19, 2018 at 4:29 p.m.
Ayes 5 (Mayor Tischler) Nays 0

(Trustee Warr, McKenna, Lee
& Goliber)

Respectfully submitted,

Lori S. Queor
Village Clerk
