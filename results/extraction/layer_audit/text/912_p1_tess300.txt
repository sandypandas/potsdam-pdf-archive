A Public Hearing of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday, April 19,
2021 at 5:45 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, NY.

PRESENT: Mayor Tischler
Trustees Warr, McKenna, Jacobs-Wilke & Lee

ALSO PRESENT: Greg Thompson, Administrator
Mayor Tischler opened the hearing at 5:45 p.m. He stated that this public hearing is to receive
public comments on a proposed local law to amend Chapter 80 and Chapter 180 to allow
chickens in the Village of Potsdam. He took the roll stating that all members were present.
The Administrator then read the Legal Notice as published.
Public Comments:
Susan Powers of 17 Spring Street spoke in favor of the law.
Rose Revezzi of 7 Barclay Street spoke in favor of the law.

Alex French spoke in favor of the law.

The Mayor then closed the hearing at 5:52 P.M.

Respectfully submitted,

Lori S. Queor
Village Clerk
