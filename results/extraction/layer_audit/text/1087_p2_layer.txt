Katie Deuel of 5 1⁄2 Pleasant Street spoke regarding the flooding in her neighborhood. She stated
that she has contacted Public Works, Greg and Lisa and has not gotten much of a response. She
further stated that she pays taxes and is looking for the board to help them as the flooding has
gotten worse over the last 2-3 years.
Heather Wheeler of 10 Waverly Street (corner of Waverly and Broad Streets) spoke regarding
the flooding in her neighborhood. She stated that the flood of 202 was due to a huge rainfall,
that the parking lots upstream are probably not helping the issue, and that there are sink holes all
through the Waverly and Pleasant Streets neighborhoods.
Tracey Wanamaker of 7 Pleasant Street spoke regarding the flooding in her neighborhood. She
stated that 11 years ago when the study was done on the crosstown canal and some cleaning of
the canal was done the flooding got better for a few years. She stated that the last 2-3 years it has
gotten worse again.
Kevin Smith of 12 Waverly Street spoke regarding the flooding in his neighborhood. He stated
the flood in 2010 was major, the flooding improved for awhile but that the last 2-3 years it is
increasing again. He stated that 3-4 months out of the year they have flooding.
Allison Carney of 84 Leroy Street spoke as one of the Board of Directors of the Potsdam
Chamber of Commerce. She stated that their concerns are regarding the abandonment of Liberty
Street and the Raymond Street extension. She stated that some of their concerns are parking,
flow of the vehicles, snow removal, keeping the freight entrances and keeping traffic 2 way and
not one way only.
Larry Hazen of 17 Hillcrest Drive spoke regarding the Liberty Street abandonment. He stated
that he is opposed to the abandonment of Liberty Street. He stated that it will negatively impact
his business.
Deputy Mayor informed Allison and Larry that the some of those issues are being worked on and
once we get more final information we will share that information with the Chamber and the
property/business owners in that area.
