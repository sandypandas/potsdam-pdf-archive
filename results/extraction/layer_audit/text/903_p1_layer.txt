A Public Hearing of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday, April 5,
2021 at 5:45 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, NY.
PRESENT:

Mayor Tischler

ALSO PRESENT:

Lori S. Queor, Village Clerk/Treasurer

Mayor Tischler opened the hearing at 5:46 P.M. He stated that this public hearing is to receive
public comments regarding the tentative Village Budget for Fiscal Year 2021-2022. He took the
roll stating that all members were present except Trustee Lee.
Village Clerk, Lori Queor, then read the Legal Notice as published.
The Mayor then asked for public comments.
There were no public comments.
