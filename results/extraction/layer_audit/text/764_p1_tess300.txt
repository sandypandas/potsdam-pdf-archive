An Executive Session of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday, August
19, 2019 at 5:15 p.m., Civic Center, Park Street, Potsdam, New York.

PRESENT: Mayor Tischler
Trustees Warr, McKenna, Lee & Goliber

The Board of Trustees, upon a resolution offered by Trustee Goliber and seconded by Trustee
Lee went into Executive Session for the discussion of the employment history of particular
persons at 5:15 p.m.

Discussion of same was had; no formal vote or action was taken in Execution Session.

Upon a resolution offered by Trustee Warr and seconded by Trustee McKenna the Executive
Session was adjourned at 6:25 p.m.

The undersigned was not in attendance at the Executive Session. Information is provided by
Mayor Tischler.

Respectfully submitted,

Lori S. Queor
Village Clerk
