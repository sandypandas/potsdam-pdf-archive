New York State Homes and Community Renewal has released a Request for Applications for its Small
Rental Development Initiative. The program can provide grant assistance to not-for-profit organizations
and partnerships between not-for-profit organizations and private real estate developers for the construction
or renovation of affordable multi-family rental housing. Follow this link to the Homes and Community
Renewal website for the Request for Applications, application workbook, model pro-forma package, vendor
responsibility questionnaire and applicant certifications https://hcr.ny.gov/srdi. Applications will be
accepted until 4:00 PM on April 17, 2023.
