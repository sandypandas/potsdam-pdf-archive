A Public Hearing of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday,
April 3, 2023 at 5:50 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, NY.
PRESENT:

Mayor Tischler
Trustees Warr, Tirion, Jacobs-Wilke

ALSO PRESENT:

Lori S. Queor, Village Clerk
Georgine Scott, Village Clerk in Training
Gregory Thompson, Administrator
Isabelle Gates-Shult, Treasurer
Mark Murray, Police Chief

Mayor Tischler opened the hearing at 5:47 P.M. He stated that this public hearing is to
receive public comments on a proposed Local Law to amend Chapter 153, Taxation Article IX
Tax Cap Override.
He took the roll stating that all members were present apart from Trustee Lee who was excused.
Village Clerk in Training, Georgine Scott, then read the Legal Notice as published.
The Mayor then asked for public comments.
No public comments were heard.
Hearing no public comments, the Mayor declared the hearing closed at 5:49 P.M.

Respectfully submitted,

Georgine Scott
Village Clerk in Training
