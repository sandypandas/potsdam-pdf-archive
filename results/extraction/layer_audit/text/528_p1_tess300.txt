A Public Hearing of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday, January 23,
2017 at 6:45 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, New York.

PRESENT: Mayor Tischler
Trustees Warr, Hopke & Sheehan

ALSO PRESENT: Maureen Gaffney, Deputy Clerk
Greg Thompson, Village Administrator
Fred Hanss, Planning Director
Mayor Tischler opened the hearing at 6:45 P.M. He stated that this public hearing is to receive
public comments on a proposed local law to amend Chapter 180, Zoning of the Village of
Potsdam Municipal Code.
Deputy Village Clerk, Maureen Gaffney, then read the Legal Notice as published.
The Mayor then asked for public comments.
Fred Hanss explained the changes to chapter 180 to the board.

Hearing no further public comments, the Mayor declared the hearing closed at 6:49 P.M.

The undersigned was not in attendance at this Public Hearing. Information was provided to her
by Deputy Clerk Gaffney.

Respectfully submitted,

Lori S. Queor
Village Clerk
