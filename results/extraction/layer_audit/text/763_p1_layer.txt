Motion was made by Trustee Goliber and Seconded by Trustee Lee to accept the minutes as
submitted. All were in favor - none opposed - motion carried.
Mayor's comments: Mayor Tischler stated that a lot of paving has been done and thank you to
the DPW crew for a great job.
Trustee's Comments: Trustee Warr had no comments. Trustee McKenna reminded everyone of
the Ives Park Concert Series which is starting. Trustee Lee had no comments. Trustee Goliber
stated that it is nice to see the police and fire personnel getting recognized recently.
Administrator's Comments: Administrator Thompson reported on the West and East Dam
Hydro projects, the airport grant, and the new fuel system at the airport and thanked Jim and
Dustin for their help installing that.
Recreation Director's Report: Recreation Director Trey Smutz provided the board with a written
report which is on file with the Village Clerk. He then went over the report with the board.
