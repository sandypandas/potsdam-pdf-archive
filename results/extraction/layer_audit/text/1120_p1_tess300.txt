A Public Hearing of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday,
April 3, 2023, at 5:45 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, NY.

PRESENT: Mayor Tischler

Trustees Warr, Tirion, Jacobs-Wilke

ALSO PRESENT: Lori S. Queor, Village Clerk
Georgine Scott, Village Clerk in Training
Greg Thompson, Administrator
Isabelle Gates-Shult, Treasurer
Mark Murray, Police Chief

Mayor Tischler opened the hearing at 5:45 P.M. He stated that this public hearing is to

receive public comments regarding the tentative Village budget for Fiscal Year 2023-2024.

He took the roll stating that all members were present apart from Trustee Lee who was excused.
Village Clerk in Training, Georgine Scott, then read the Legal Notice as published.

The Mayor then asked for public comments.

Daniel Ben-Avraham, 8 Circle Drive, Potsdam commented that Massena Municipality has
budget workshops available for the public to attend to better understand their proposed budget

and he suggest that the Village of Potsdam consider doing the same.

Hearing no further public comments, the Mayor declared the hearing closed at 5:48 P.M.

Respectfully submitted,

Georgine Scott
Village Clerk in Training
