A Regular Meeting of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday, January
23, 2023, at 6:00 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, New York.
PRESENT:

Mayor Tischler
Trustees Warr, Tirion, & Lee

ALSO PRESENT:

Lori S. Queor, Village Clerk/Registrar
Georgine Scott, Deputy Clerk/Registrar
Mark Murray, Police Chief
Trey Smutz, Recreation Director
Greg Thompson, Village Administrator

Mayor Tischler opened the meeting at 6:00 P.M., pointed out the exits on the North and East
Walls and advised that the board room has the capacity for 109 people.
The Mayor then stated that all members were present with the exception of Trustee JacobsWilke.
The Mayor then stated that the following sets of minutes had been submitted for Board
acceptance:
January 3, 2023

Regular Meeting

6:00 p.m.

Motion was made by Trustee Lee and Seconded by Trustee Tirion to accept the minutes as
submitted. All Were in Favor - None Opposed - Motion Carried.
Mayor's comments: We have had a few storms and another one is supposed to happen
Wednesday/Thursday this week with 3-5 and then 5-8 inches of snow and the DPW will do the
best they can. Covid is still out there so be careful, be safe and be smart.
Trustee's Comments:
Trustee Warr - World University Games were awesome, I went to several contests and they
were all great. Volunteers all got a lot of great swag and it was great seeing all the teams
walking around town in the jackets.
Trustee Tirion - World University Games were very exciting, team USA won silver in men's
hockey and I am glad the Zamboni got fixed in time and was up and running. Spoke with Eric
York about the new website last week and he indicated he should be done the end of February
and will make a beta version available the two weeks Clarkson is on spring break. He will take
comments and incorporate them and then will go live.
Trustee Lee - FISU Games we great and it was wonderful seeing the students everywhere.
Everyone was personable and friendly and the businesses downtown were busy which was good.
Administrator's Comments: Administrator Greg Thompson provided the board with a written
report that is on file with the Village Clerk. He then went over his report with the Board.
