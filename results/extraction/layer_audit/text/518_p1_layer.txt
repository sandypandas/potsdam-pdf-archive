A Special Meeting of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday, June 12,
2017 at 8:00 a.m., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, New York.
PRESENT:

Mayor Reinhold Tischler
Trustees Warr & Lee

ALSO PRESENT:

Greg Thompson, Village Administrator
Lori S. Queor, Village Clerk/Treasurer
Fred Hanss, Planning Director

Mayor Tischler opened the meeting at 8:00 a.m., and took the roll and stated that all members
were present with the exception of Trustees Sheehan & Garner.
NEW BUSINESS:

Trustee Warr Moved and Trustee Lee Seconded a resolution authorizing and directing the
Village of Potsdam to apply for $10 million in grant funding through the 2017 Downtown
Revitalization Initiative Program and further authorizing the Mayor or in his absence the
Deputy Mayor to execute the grant application and supporting certifications and any
resulting grant agreement.
Ayes 3

(Mayor Tischler)
(Trustee Warr & Lee)

Nays 0

Trustee Warr Moved and Trustee Lee Seconded a resolution to adjourn the Special
Meeting of June 12, 2017 at 8:02 a.m.
Ayes 3

(Mayor Tischler)
(Trustee Warr & Lee)

Nays 0

Respectfully submitted,

Lori S. Queor
Village Clerk
