An Executive Session of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday, May
17, 2021 at 5:09 p.m., Civic Center, Park Street, Potsdam, New York.

PRESENT: Mayor Tischler
Trustees Warr, McKenna & Jacobs-Wilke
Administrator Thompson
The Board of Trustees, upon a resolution offered by Trustee Jacobs-Wilke and seconded by
Trustee McKenna went into Executive Session for the discussion of the employment history of
particular persons at 5:09 p.m.

Discussion of same was had; no formal vote or action was taken in Execution Session.

Upon a resolution offered by Trustee Warr and seconded by Trustee Jacobs-Wilke the Executive
Session was adjourned at 5:39 p.m.

The undersigned was not in attendance at the Executive Session. Information is provided by
Mayor Tischler.

Respectfully submitted,

Lori S. Queor
Village Clerk
