A Public Hearing of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday, October 1,
2018, at 5:50 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, New York.
PRESENT:

Mayor Tischler
Trustees Warr, McKenna & Lee

ALSO PRESENT:

Greg Thompson, Village Administrator
Lori S. Queor, Village Clerk/Treasurer

Mayor Tischler opened the hearing at 5:50 p.m. He stated that this public hearing is to receive
public comments on a proposed local law to amend Chapter 168 Vehicles and Traffic.
Village Clerk/Treasurer, Lori Queor, then read the Legal Notice as published.
Public Comments: Alexandra Jacobs-Wilke from the North Country Children's Museum Board
spoke in favor of the change as the museum will be open on Wednesday's and having the
Raymond Street lot closed on Wednesday would make it hard for people to get in to the museum.
Hearing no further public comments, the Mayor then closed the hearing at 5:54 P.M.

Respectfully submitted,

Lori S. Queor
Village Clerk
