A Public Hearing of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday, April 4,
2022 at 5:45 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, NY.

PRESENT: Mayor Tischler
Trustees Warr, Tirion, Jacobs-Wilke & Lee

ALSO PRESENT: Lori S. Queor, Village Clerk
Cara Adams, Village Treasurer
Greg Thompson, Administrator
Mark Murray, Police Chief
Lisa Newby, Code Officer
Trey Smutz, Recreation Director

Mayor Tischler opened the hearing at 5:49 P.M. He stated that this public hearing is to receive
public comments regarding the tentative Village Budget for Fiscal Year 2022-2023. He took the
roll stating that all members were present.

Village Clerk, Lori Queor, then read the Legal Notice as published.

The Mayor then asked for public comments.

Michael Edgar from Maple Street asked if there were additional funds added to the budget for
another DPW employee who could be used to address the sidewalk issues he has brought up.
Administrator Thompson stated that we have freed up some funds due to not replacing the DPW
Superintendent who retired this year so it is possible we could hire another employee who might

work 4 at the DPW and ¥% at the arena.

Hearing no further public comments, the Mayor declared the hearing closed at 5:52 P.M.

Respectfully submitted,

Lori S. Queor
Village Clerk
