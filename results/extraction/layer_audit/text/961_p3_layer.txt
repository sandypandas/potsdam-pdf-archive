RESOLUTION TO ABANDON A PORTION OF COTTAGE STREET AS A
MUNICIPAL STREET AND TO CONVEY SAME TO CANTON POTSDAM
HOSPITAL
WHEREAS, the Village Board of the Village of Potsdam is the owner in fee of a
municipal street named Cottage Street located in the Village of Potsdam, New York; and
WHEREAS, the Village has received a request from Canton Potsdam Hospital that the
Village abandon the portion of Cottage Street between Waverly Street and Leroy Street
and convey said portion of Cottage Street to Canton Potsdam Hospital to help facilitate
its continued development and upgrading of its hospital campus for the betterment of the
Hospital and the community; and
WHEREAS, the Village Board has determined that the portion of Cottage Street between
Waverly Street and Leroy Street is useless as a public thoroughfare and is no longer
needed as a public street; and
WHEREAS, the Village conducted a public hearing held before the Potsdam Village
Board of Trustees at the Potsdam Civic Center, 2 Park Street, Potsdam, New York 13676,
the 5th day of October, 2020 at 5:30 p.m., to consider the abandonment of the portion of
Cottage Street between, Waverly Street and Leroy Street, and the neighboring
landowners and the general public was invited by public notice to attend; and
WHEREAS, the Village conducted a SEQRA review pursuant to the New York State
Environmental Conservation law, deeming the action as an unlisted action, completed
and reviewed a short Environmental Assessment Form, finding that the abandonment of
the portion of Cottage Street between Waverly Street and Leroy Street would cause no
potential for significant environmental impacts, and issued a negative declaration for
purposes of SEQRA, and
