Interest session on Complete Streets
April 12, 2021

The Village of Potsdam invites all interested residents to join in an interest session as the
municipality looks to form a Complete Streets Working Group to help guide infrastructure
development.

Whether your favored mode of transportation is by foot, bike, wheelchair, car, or with a stroller
in tow-your insights matter. Our streets, sidewalks and trails are for everyone. As we work to
make the Village of Potsdam safer and more accessible to all, we hope to engage residents in
this important work.

Join us to find out more about how to get involved:
