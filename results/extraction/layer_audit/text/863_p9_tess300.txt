Available Industrial Sites

Potsdam Damon Field Airport, Corner of Outer Elm Street (State Highway 11B) and Hatch Road.

Owned by the Village of Potsdam. General aviation airport with room for air transportation business
development sites. Zoned for light industrial and aviation businesses.

Single 3,705 x 60 feet runway with a parallel taxiway, and AWOS.
Av-Gas and Jet-A fueling facilities.
Pilot lounge, tie-downs and T-hangars are available on-site.

Home to Air Methods air ambulance service and LIT Aviation, daily air freight deliveries to the regional
UPS distribution Center on Hatch Road.

Situated in the Federal Opportunity Zone, Census Tract 4909.
