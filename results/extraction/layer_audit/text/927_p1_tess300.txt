A Public Hearing of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday, June 21,
2021 at 4:25 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, NY.

PRESENT: Mayor Tischler
Trustees Warr & McKenna

Trustee Lee via Zoom

ALSO PRESENT: Lori S. Queor, Village Clerk/Treasurer
Greg Thompson, Administrator

Mayor Tischler opened the hearing at 4:26 p.m. He stated that this public hearing is to receive
public comments on the East Dam Hydro Electric Generating Plant Renovation Project OCR
Grant #947PR34-17. He took the roll stating that all members were present except for Trustee
Jacobs-Wilke.

The Village Clerk then read the Legal Notice as published.

There were no public comments.

Hearing no public comments, the Mayor then closed the hearing at 4:27 P.M.

Respectfully submitted,

Lori S. Queor
