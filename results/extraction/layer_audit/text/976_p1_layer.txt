A Public Input Session of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday,
November 15, 2021 at 5:30 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, NY.

PRESENT:

Mayor Tischler
Trustees Warr, McKenna, Jacobs-Wilke & Lee

ALSO PRESENT:

Cara Adams, Village Treasurer
Greg Thompson, Administrator
Jim Corbett, DPW Superintendent

Mayor Tischler opened the input session at 5:37 p.m. He stated that this public input session is
to receive public comments on the possible establishment of licensed cannabis retail
establishments and/or on-site consumption locations in the Village of Potsdam, under the NYS
Marijuana Regulation and Taxation Act.
He took the roll stating that all members were present.

Public Comments:
Anne McLaughlin from Pierrepont who owns property in the Village of Potsdam spoke in favor
of both the retail establishments and on-site consumption locations.
Christa Kelson from 91 Main Street spoke by phone. She stated that she teaches courses on
cannabis at SUNY Potsdam and spoke in favor.
Email from Rose Revezzi in favor.

Trustee McKenna Moved and Trustee Jacobs-Wilke seconded a resolution to close the public
input session at 5:54 p.m.
Ayes 5

(Mayor Tischler)
(Trustee Warr, McKenna, Lee &
Jacobs-Wilke)

Nays 0

Respectfully submitted,

Lori S. Queor
Village Clerk
