AGENDA - REGULAR MEETING 5
VILLAGE OF POTSDAM BOARD OF TRUSTEES
MONDAY - OCTOBER 16, 2017 - 7:00 P.M.

Whereas, we believe that even if emissions were dramatically reduced today,
communities would still be required to adapt to the effects of climate change for decades
to come,

Now, Therefore, The Village of Potsdam Resolves that: To reduce greenhouse gas
emissions and adapt to a changing climate, that the Village Board of Trustees adopts the
New York State Climate Smart Communities Pledge, which is comprised by the
following ten elements:

1. Pledge to be a Climate Smart Community.

2. Set goals, inventory emissions, plan for climate action.

3. Decrease community energy use.

4. Increase community use of renewable energy.

5. Realize the benefits of recycling and other climate smart solid waste management

practices.

6. Reduce greenhouse emissions through the use of climate smart land-use tools.

7. Enhance community resilience and prepare for the effects of climate change.

8. Support the development of a green innovation economy.

9. Inform and inspire the public.

10. | Commit to an evolving process of climate action.
