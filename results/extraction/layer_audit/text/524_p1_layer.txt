A Public Hearing of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Tuesday, April 25,
2017 at 6:50 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, New York.
PRESENT:

Mayor Tischler
Trustees Warr, Hopke & Sheehan

ALSO PRESENT:

Maureen Gaffney, Deputy Village Clerk
Greg Thompson, Village Administrator
Fred Hanss, Planning Director

Mayor Tischler opened the hearing at 6:52 P.M. He stated that this public hearing is to receive
public comments on a proposed Local Law to amend Chapter 168, Vehicles and Traffic. The
Mayor took the roll stating that all members were present except for Trustee Garner.
Deputy Village Clerk, Maureen Gaffney, then read the Legal Notice as published.
The Mayor then asked for public comments.
Hearing no public comments, the Mayor declared the hearing closed at 6:53 P.M.
The undersigned was not in attendance at this hearing and information was provided to her by
Deputy Clerk Maureen Gaffney.
Respectfully submitted,

Lori S. Queor
Village Clerk
