A Public Hearing of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday, June 17,
2019 at 4:10 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, New York.

PRESENT: Mayor Tischler
Trustees Warr, Lee & Goliber

ALSO PRESENT: Lori S. Queor, Clerk/Treasurer
Jim Corbett, DPW Superintendent

Greg Thompson, Administrator
Lisa Newby, Code Officer

Mayor Tischler opened the hearing at 4:10 p.m. He stated that this public hearing is to receive
public comments on a proposed Local Law to amend Chapter 180 Zoning, to change the zone
designation from R-1 to R-3 for the property located at 74 Lawrence Avenue. He took the roll
stating that all members were present with the exception of Trustee McKenna

Village Clerk, Lori Queor, then read the Legal Notice as published.

Public Comments: None

Hearing no public comments, the Mayor then closed the hearing at 4:12 P.M.

Respectfully submitted,

Lori S. Queor
Village Clerk
