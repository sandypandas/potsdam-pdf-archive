A Public Hearing of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Thursday, June 29,
2017 at 3:30 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, New York.

PRESENT: Mayor Tischler
Trustees Lee & Sheehan

ALSO PRESENT: Lori S. Queor, Village Clerk/Treasurer

Greg Thompson, Village Administrator
Fred Hanss, Planning Director

Mayor Tischler opened the hearing at 3:30 P.M. He stated that this public hearing is to receive
public comments on the Village of Potsdam's 2017 proposal for $750,000 in funding through the
CDBG for the East Dam Hydro Plant renovation project.

Village Clerk, Lori Queor, then read the Legal Notice as published.

The Mayor then asked for public comments.

Fred Hanss stated that we have used the CDBG program before for a wide variety of projects.

He stated that he and the Administrator went to Albany in April to the Office of Community
Renewal and the hydro project they were told is an eligible project. He further stated that NYPA

is working on an engineering report which will be the basis of the application.

Hearing no further public comments, the Mayor declared the hearing closed at 3:36 P.M.

Respectfully submitted,

Lori S. Queor
Village Clerk
