A Public Hearing of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Thursday, June 29,
2017 at 3:40 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, New York.
PRESENT:

Mayor Tischler
Trustees Lee & Sheehan

ALSO PRESENT:

Lori S. Queor, Village Clerk/Treasurer
Greg Thompson, Village Administrator
Fred Hanss, Planning Director

Mayor Tischler opened the hearing at 3:40 P.M. He stated that this public hearing is to receive
public comments on the Village of Potsdam's 2017 proposal for funding through the NY Main
Street Program.
Village Clerk, Lori Queor, then read the Legal Notice as published.
The Mayor then asked for public comments.
Fred Hanss stated that we have used the NYS Main Street Program in the past for the Arlington
and J.R. Weston Buildings. He stated that they are applying for $200,000 which would renovate
4 buildings. Trustee Sheehan asked Mr. Hanss to make sure that the property owners in the area
know.
Hearing no further public comments, the Mayor declared the hearing closed at 3:52 P.M.

Respectfully submitted,

Lori S. Queor
Village Clerk
