A Public Hearing of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Tuesday, September
5, 2017 at 6:30 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, New York.

PRESENT: Mayor Tischler
Trustees Warr, Lee & Sheehan

ALSO PRESENT: Lori S. Queor, Village Clerk/Treasurer
Greg Thompson, Village Administrator

Mayor Tischler opened the hearing at 6:30 P.M. He stated that this public hearing is to receive
public comments on a proposed Local Law to amend Chapter 168 Vehicles and Traffic to make
the Waverly Street/Broad Street intersection a four way stop.

Village Clerk, Lori Queor, then read the Legal Notice as published.

The Mayor then asked for public comments.

Hearing no public comments, the Mayor declared the hearing closed at 6:32 P.M.

Respectfully submitted,

Lori S. Queor
Village Clerk
