A Public Hearing of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday, March 21,
2022 at 5:45 P.M.
PRESENT:

Deputy Mayor Warr
Trustees Tirion, Jacobs-Wilke & Lee

ALSO PRESENT:

Village Clerk, Lori S. Queor

Deputy Mayor Warr opened the hearing at 5:47 p.m. He stated that this public hearing is to
receive public comments on a proposed local law to amend Chapter 180 Zoning. He took the
roll stating that all members were present except Mayor Tischler.
The Village Clerk then read the Legal Notice as published.
Public Comments:
Debbie Gordon spoke in favor of the local law.
Dave Mooney from NCOC spoke in favor of the local law. He provided the Village Clerk with a
petition signed by 100 people who are in favor of the local law. The petition is on file with the
Village Clerk.
Jim Gillespie from Stewart's spoke in support of the local law.
Lynn Smith, co-owner of Foxy Roxy's spoke in favor of the local law.
Roxanne Smith spoke in favor of the local law.
Hearing no further public comments, the Deputy Mayor then closed the hearing at 5:54 P.M.

Respectfully submitted,

Lori S. Queor
Village Clerk
