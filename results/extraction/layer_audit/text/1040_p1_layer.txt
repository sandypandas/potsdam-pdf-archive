The Village of Potsdam proposes an overall goal of 8.9 percent for its Disadvantaged Business Enterprise
(DBE) program for FAA funded projects for federal fiscal years 2023, 2024 and 2025. The proposed goal
and its rationale are available for inspection during normal business hours at the Village of Potsdam
Offices, Civic Center - 2 Park Street, Potsdam, NY 13676. Comments on the goal will be accepted by the
Village of Potsdam, P.O. Box 5168, Potsdam, NY 13676 and the FAA Office of Civil Rights, FAA Southern
Regional Office, 1701 Columbia Ave., College Park, GA 30337.
