A Regular Meeting of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday, October 5,
2020 at 6:00 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, NY.

PRESENT: Mayor Tischler
Trustees Warr, McKenna, Jacobs-Wilke & Lee

ALSO PRESENT: - Lori Queor, Clerk/Treasurer
Fred Hanss, Planning Director
Greg Thompson, Administrator
Jim Corbett, DPW Superintendent
Lisa Newby, Code Officer

Mayor Tischler opened the meeting at 6:03 P.M and pointed out the exits on the North and East
walls of the Board Room. The Mayor then stated that all members were present.

The Mayor then stated that the following sets of minutes had been submitted for Board
acceptance:

September 21, 2020 Regular Meeting 6:00 p.m.
September 21, 2020 Executive Session 6:32 p.m.

Motion was made by Trustee Jacobs-Wilke and Seconded by Trustee McKenna to accept the
minutes as submitted. All were in Favor - None Opposed - Motion Carried.

Mayor's comments: Trick or Treating will be up to the individuals.
Trustee's Comments:
Trustee Warr - None

Trustee McKenna- Will post CDC guidelines on trick or treating on the Village's Facebook
page. Would like to see a resolution added to the agenda tonight to request the Planning Board
review the hospital issue and provide a recommendation. Wish the President and his
administration all the best with the Covid 19 illness. I condemn white supremacy and I hope all
elected officials agree. I condemn violence, hatred and white supremacy.

Trustee Jacobs-Wilke - concurred with Trustee McKenna. Indigenous People's Day coming up
and thankful for our indigenous brothers and sisters especially those who are near us on
Akwesasne. Complete streets working group needs to meet soon.

Trustee Lee - Thank you to trustee McKenna and Jacobs-Wilke for their passion on important
issues.

Administrator's Comments: Administrator Thompson provided the board with a written report
which is on file with the Village Clerk. He then went over his report with the Board. He stated
that letters for the policing committee will likely go out tomorrow. They are a little behind
because they just found out that the D.A. has to be present and the Police Chief has been
extremely busy recently with court cases.
