A Public Hearing of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Tuesday, February
21, 2017 at 6:45 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, New York.
PRESENT:

Mayor Tischler
Trustees Warr, Hopke & Sheehan

ALSO PRESENT:

Lori S. Queor, Village Clerk/Treasurer
Greg Thompson, Village Administrator
Fred Hanss, Planning Director
Mark Murray, Police Chief

Mayor Tischler opened the hearing at 6:46 P.M. He stated that this public hearing is to receive
public comments on a proposed local law to amend Chapter 18, Elections of the Village of
Potsdam Municipal Code.
Village Clerk, Lori Queor, then read the Legal Notice as published.
The Mayor then asked for public comments.
Rose Rivezzi addressed the board. She stated that she had mixed thoughts on term limits. She
stated that she felt educating the public regarding elections, terms, etc. would be a better way to
bring more people to the table.
Village Clerk Lori Queor then read letters from Tom French who was opposed to term limits and
Lou Ann Lange who was in favor of term limits into the records. Copies of these letters are on
file with the Village Clerk.

Hearing no further public comments, the Mayor declared the hearing closed at 6:55 P.M.

Respectfully submitted,

Lori S. Queor
Village Clerk
