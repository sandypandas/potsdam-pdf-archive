An Executive Session of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday, May
15, 2017 at 8:48 p.m., Civic Center, Park Street, Potsdam, New York.

PRESENT: Mayor Tischler
Trustees Warr, Hopke & Sheehan
Administrator Greg Thompson
The Board of Trustees, upon a resolution offered by Trustee Sheehan and seconded by Trustee
Warr went into Executive Session for the discussion of the employment history of particular
persons at 8:48 p.m.

Discussion of same was had; no formal vote or action was taken in Execution Session.

Upon a resolution offered by Trustee Warr and seconded by Trustee Hopke the Executive
Session was adjourned at 9:09 p.m.

The undersigned was not in attendance at the Executive Session. Information is provided by
Administrator Thompson.
