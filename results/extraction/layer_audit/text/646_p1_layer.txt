A Regular Meeting of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Tuesday,
September 4, 2018 at 6:36 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, New
York.
PRESENT:

Mayor Tischler
Trustees Warr, McKenna, Lee & Goliber

ALSO PRESENT:

Greg Thompson, Administrator
Lori Queor, Village Clerk/Treasurer
Mark Murray, Police Chief

Mimi VanDeusen, Museum Director
Matt Seymour, Police Officer

Mayor Tischler opened the meeting at 6:36 P.M., pointed out the exits on the North and East
Walls and advised that the board room has the capacity for 109 people.
The Mayor then stated that all members were present. Trustee McKenna left the meeting at 6:50
p.m.
The Mayor then stated that the following sets of minutes had been submitted for Board
acceptance:

Motion was made by Trustee Goliber and Seconded by Trustee Lee to accept the minutes as
submitted. All were in favor - none opposed - motion carried.
Mayor's comments: The Mayor stated that he appreciates everyone's comments on the fluoride
issue and that there will be a resolution on the September 17, 2018 agenda for a decision on this
issue.
Trustee's Comments: Trustee Warr had no comments. Trustee McKenna apologized for being
late to the public hearing and stated that she will have to leave early for another engagement.
Trustee Lee thanked everyone for all the info they have provided. Trustee Goliber also thanked
everyone for the information they have provided.
Administrator's Comments: Administrator Thompson provided a written report to the board
which is on file with the Village Clerk. He then went over the report with the board.
