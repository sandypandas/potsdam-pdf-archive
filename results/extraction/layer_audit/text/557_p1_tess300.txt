An Executive Session of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday, March
6, 2017 at 7:28 p.m., Civic Center, Park Street, Potsdam, New York.

PRESENT: Mayor Tischler
Trustees Warr, Hopke & Sheehan

The Board of Trustees, upon a resolution offered by Trustee Warr and seconded by Trustee
Sheehan went into Executive Session for the discussion of the employment history of particular
persons at 7:28 p.m.

Discussion of same was had; no formal vote or action was taken in Execution Session.

Upon a resolution offered by Trustee Warr and seconded by Trustee Sheehan the Executive
Session was adjourned at 7:55 p.m.

The undersigned was not in attendance at the Executive Session. Information is provided by
Mayor Tischler.

Respectfully submitted,

Lori S. Queor
Village Clerk
