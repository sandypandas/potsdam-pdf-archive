Trustee Moved and Trustee Seconded a resolution to submit an
application for funding through the NYS Community Development Block Grant Program
(CDBG) through the 2017 Consolidated Funding Application/Priority Project funding
cycle. The Village of Potsdam seeks a grant in the amount of $750,000.00 under the
CDBG Program's Public Infrastructure funding category to be used with $1,118,386 in
funding provided through the NYS and Municipal Facilities Program and the NYPA
Energy Efficiency Program. Project funding will be used for the express purpose of
undertaking the substantial rehabilitation of the Village of Potsdam's East Dam Hydro
Electric Generating Plant. Further be it resolved that the Mayor of the Village of
Potsdam or in his absence, the Deputy Mayor is hereby authorized and directed to
execute the application documents, any required certifications and any grant agreement
that may result from said application.

Ayes Nays
