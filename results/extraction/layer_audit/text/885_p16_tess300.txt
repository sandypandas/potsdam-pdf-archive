Overall Satisfaction with Village of Potsdam Police Department

When asked how satisfied they were with their overall experiences with the Village of Potsdam
Police Department, a majority of respondents (62.3%) reported that they were either satisfied
(20.8%) or very satisfied (41.5%). 18.9% of respondents reported that they were either
dissatisfied (8.6%) or very dissatisfied (10.3%), while 20.3% reported that they were neither
satisfied nor dissatisfied.
