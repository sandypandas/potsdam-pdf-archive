A Public Hearing of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday, August 21,
2017 at 6:50 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, New York.
PRESENT:

Mayor Tischler
Trustees Warr, Lee & Sheehan

ALSO PRESENT:

Lori S. Queor, Village Clerk/Treasurer
Greg Thompson, Village Administrator
Fred Hanss, Planning Director

Mayor Tischler opened the hearing at 6:50 P.M. He stated that this public hearing is to receive
public comments on a proposed Local Law to amend Chapter 180 Zoning Article III, Section
180-6 Zoning Map to change the zone designation from SCH to B-1 for the property at 119
Maple Street.
Village Clerk, Lori Queor, then read the Legal Notice as published.
The Mayor then asked for public comments.
Fred Hanss stated that the property is surrounded by B-1, the Planning Board reviewed the
request and fount it a desirable change that is consistent with the Comprehensive Plan.
George Digiovanna from Clarkson University stated that they are in talks with a private
developer to develop the property and it would then bring in tax revenue for the village.
Hearing no further public comments, the Mayor declared the hearing closed at 6:53 P.M.

Respectfully submitted,

Lori S. Queor
Village Clerk
