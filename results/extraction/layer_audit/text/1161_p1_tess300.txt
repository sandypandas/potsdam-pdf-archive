A Special Meeting of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday,
August 3, 2023 at 1:00 p.m., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, New York.

PRESENT: Mayor Reinhold Tischler
Trustees Lee & Jacobs-Wilke

ALSO PRESENT: Greg Thompson, Administrator
Georgine Scott, Village Clerk

Mayor Tischler opened the meeting at 1:00 p.m., took the roll and stated that all members
were present apart from Trustee Warr and Tirion, who were excused.

NEW BUSINESS:

Trustee Lee Moved and Trustee Jacobs-Wilke Seconded a resolution

to vote on the matter of the ratification of the Memorandum of Agreement (MOA)
between the Village of Potsdam and the Village of Potsdam CSEA Unit, CSEA,
Local 1000 AFSCME, ALF-CIO for the period of June 1, 2023 to May 31, 2027.

Ayes 3 (Mayor Tischler) Nays 0
(Trustee Lee, Jacobs-Wilke)

Trustee Lee Moved and Trustee Jacobs-Wilke Seconded a resolution authorizing the submission
of an application to the U.S. Department of Transportation's Rural and Tribal Assistance Pilot
Program in the amount of $320,000 to prepare a technical study for the development of enhanced
pedestrian and bicycle facilities to include: preliminary engineering, preparation of draft state and
federal environmental reviews, a construction requirements analysis, cost estimation, cost/benefit
analysis and public outreach; and, further, authorizing the Mayor or in his absence, the Deputy
Mayor to execute the application, any required certifications and any grant agreement resulting
from said application.

Note: There is no local share of project costs for this grant program.

Ayes 3 (Mayor Tischler) Nays 0
(Trustee Lee, Jacobs-Wilke)
