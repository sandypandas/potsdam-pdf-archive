A Public Hearing of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday, May 15,
2017 at 6:40 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, New York.
PRESENT:

Mayor Tischler
Trustees Warr, Hopke & Sheehan

ALSO PRESENT:

Lori S. Queor, Village Clerk/Treasurer
Greg Thompson, Village Administrator

Mayor Tischler opened the hearing at 7:15 P.M. He stated that this public hearing is to receive
public comments on a proposed local law to amend Chapter 173 Water Article II Water Rents of
the Potsdam Village Code. He took the roll stating that all members were present except for
Trustee Garner.
Village Clerk, Lori Queor, then read the Legal Notice as published.
The Mayor then asked for public comments.
The water billing was discussed at the public hearing prior to this public hearing concerning
sewer rates so there was no new comments.
Hearing no further public comments, the Mayor declared the hearing closed at 7:16 P.M.

Respectfully submitted,

Lori S. Queor
Village Clerk
