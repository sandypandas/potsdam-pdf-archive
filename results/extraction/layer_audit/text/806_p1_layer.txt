A Public Hearing of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday, April 6,
2020 at 5:45 P.M., via Zoom Meeting due to the Coronavirus Pandemic.
PRESENT:

Mayor Tischler
Trustees McKenna, Jacobs-Wilke & Lee

ALSO PRESENT:

Lori S. Queor, Village Clerk/Treasurer
Fred Hanss, Planning Director
Greg Thompson, Administrator

Mayor Tischler opened the hearing at 5:49 P.M. He stated that this public hearing is to receive
public comments regarding the tentative Village Budget for Fiscal Year 2020-2021. He took the
roll stating that all members were present except Trustee Warr.
Village Clerk, Lori Queor, then read the Legal Notice as published.
The Mayor then asked for public comments.
There were no public comments and Clerk Queor stated that she had received no comments via
email, regular mail or telephone call.
Hearing no public comments, the Mayor declared the hearing closed at 5:50 P.M.

Respectfully submitted,

Lori S. Queor
Village Clerk
