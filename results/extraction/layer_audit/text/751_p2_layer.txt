Museum Director's Report: Director Mimi VanDeusen stated that she was not prepared to give a

dig at Meadow East. Ms. VanDeusen stated that they have found some small things that match
the era but they are not finding as many artifacts as they had hoped to. Trustee Lee asked about
the Sandstone Festival. Ms. VanDeusen stated that unless they could partner with someone else
they cannot do it themselves as it is a lot to do. Trustee McKenna asked what the Sandstone
Festival was. Ms. VanDeusen stated it was a tour of the 23 historic buildings plus information
on the quarries, etc.
