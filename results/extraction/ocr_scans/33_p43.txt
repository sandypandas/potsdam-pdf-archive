Village of Potsdam, New York, 2012-2022 Comprehensive Plan

Environmental Remediation

In the 200 years since Potsdam’s settlement, the community’s natural environment has been changed in
profound. ways — the most damaging is the contamination of lands within the Village by coal tars,
petroleum, solvents and other hazardous chemicals. Significant areas of land zoned for commercial
development (B-1 and B-1 Central Area Overly) have been found to be contaminated by hazardous waste
disposed of over the last two centuries. These areas would be appropriate for commercial in-fill
development or adaptive re-use, perhaps as open space, if the environmental contamination was
remediated.

Recommendations
ER-1 The Planning and Development Office and the Village Planning Board should identify state
and/or federally listed Brownfield sites within the Village of Potsdam that are suitable for in-fill re-

development or open space.

ER-2 The Planning and Development Office should identify sources of state and/or federal financing
- including loans, grants and tax credits that can be used for Brownfield remediation and re-use.

ER-3 The Planning and Development Office should seek state and/or federal funding to be used for the
purposes of marketing former Brownfield sites for adaptive re-use.
