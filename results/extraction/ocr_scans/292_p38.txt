PETITION FOR THE ANNEXATION OF TERRITORY FROM THE TOWN OF
POTSDAM, COUNTY OF ST. LAWRENCE, STATE OF NEW YORK TO THE
VILLAGE OF POTSDAM, COUNTY OF ST. LAWRENCE, STATE OF NEW YORK

TO THE TOWN BOARD OF THE TOWN OF POTSDAM, ST. LAWRENCE COUNTY, NEW
YORK; ©

TO THE VILLAGE BOARD OF THE VILLAGE OF POTSDAM, ST. LAWRENCE
COUNTY, NEW YORK:

Pursuant to General Municipal Law Article 17, the Petitioner, Clarkson University, a
New York University chartered by the Board of Regents of the State of New York, with an
address at 8 Clarkson Avenue, Potsdam, New York 13676 ("Petitioner"), Petitions for the
annexation of territory from the Town of Potsdam, New York ("Town") to the Village of
Potsdam ("Village").

1. . The Petitioner proposes and petitions that the Town Board of the Town permit
and allow to be annexed to the Village the territory more particularly described As 152 Maple
Street, tax map number 64.065-5-1. |

2. The Petitioner proposes and petitions that the Village Board of the Village permit
and allow to be annexed into the Village, the property recently.acquired from Mr. James Engels

at 152 Maple Street.

3.  . The Petitioner herein is the only person or entity occupying the territory described

above.

4, The Petitioner herein is the owner of all of the assessed valuation of the real

property in said territory assessed upon the last preceding town assessment roll of the town.
