C.. Environmental

Major environmental resources and constraints were identified in conservatively defined study
areas where potential impacts due to the new roadway alternative could occur. Existing
environmental resources and constraints are described below and include noise-sensitive land
uses, wetlands, environmental justice (EJ) areas, resources governed under 23 Code of Federal
Regulations part 774 (Section 4(f) resources), and lands or facilities acquired with Land and
Water Conservation Act funds (Section 6(f) resources).

1. Noise-Sensitive Land Use

Noise sensitive receptors in the vicinity of Route 11 were identified based on guidance in the
NYSDOT Environmental Procedures Manual. \dentified receptors included residential land
uses; public and community facilities (including educational facilities, health facilities, cultural
facilities, and religious-facilities); and recreational and entertainment facilities (including
theaters, auditoriums, playgrounds, athletic fields, and outdoor sports facilities; recreational
facilities such as nature trails and bike paths; state-owned forest lands; and public parks). Land
use categories comprising noise-sensitive receptors are shown in Figure 4, “Land Use” and
Figure 5, “Public Parks/Recreation Resources.”

2. Wetlands

National Wetland Inventory (NWI) freshwater wetlands and New York State Department of
Environmental Conservation (NYSDEC) wetlands are mapped in Figure 6, “NWI and NYSDEC
Wetlands,” which indicates the presence of large NWI and NYSDEC wetlands along major

portions of the study area.

Wetlands measuring 12.4 acres (5 hectares) or larger are protected under the New York State
Freshwater Wetlands Act, and are under the jurisdiction of NYSDEC. Smaller wetlands may
also be protected if they are considered of unusual local importance. Around every wetland is an
“adjacent area” of 100 feet that is also regulated to provide protection for the wetland. The U.S.
Army Corps of Engineers (USACE) also protects wetlands, irrespective of size, under Section
404 of the Clean Water Act.
