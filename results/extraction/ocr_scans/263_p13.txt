A Special Meeting of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Thursday, August
27, 2015 at 7:30 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, New York.

PRESENT: Mayor Steve Yurgartis
Trustees Warr, Hopke, & Garner

ALSO PRESENT: Everett Basford, Administrator
Fred Hanss, Planning Director

Mayor Yurgartis opened the meeting at 7:30 P.M. and took the roll stating that all members were
present with the exception of Trustee Tischler.

NEW BUSINESS:

Trustee Garner Moved and Trustee Warr Seconded a resolution to amend the Owner-
Engineer Agreement for the Water Pollution Control Facility and Collection System
Improvement Project contract with Barton and Loguidice in accordance with Exhibit K-1
Scope of Services. Further resolved that the Mayor or in his absence the Deputy Mayor
authorized to execute the amendment.

Funds for the additional cost not to exceed $6,000 to come from Sewer Fund
Contingency.

Ayes 4 (Mayor Yurgartis) Nays 0
(Trustee Warr, Hopke & Garner

Trustee Garner Moved and Trustee Warr Seconded a resolution accepting the Grant Offer
for Airport Improvement Program (AIP) Project No. 3-36-0099-031-2015 from the
Federal Aviation Administration for the Potsdam Airport Damon Field Runway
Rehabilitation Construction as submitted in grant application. Further authorizing Everett
Basford, Village Administrator as the sponsor’s authorized representative, to execute said
grant agreement.

Ayes 4 (Mayor Yurgartis) Nays 0
(Trustee Warr, Hopke & Garner
