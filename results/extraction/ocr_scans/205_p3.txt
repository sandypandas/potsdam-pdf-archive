Trustee Moved and Trustee Seconded a resolution as follows:

Whereas, the Village of Potsdam has secured a grant through the New York State
Environmental Facilities Corporation for the preparation of a Wastewater Treatment Planning
Study, Planning Grant # 38948 in the amount of $30,000; and,

Whereas, the Village of Potsdam pledged to provide matching cash in the amount of $8,900
to pay for the local share of cost of developing the Wastewater Treatment Planning Study;

Now Therefore, Be It Resolved That: the Village of Potsdam authorizes and appropriates
22 percent of the local match as required by the Engineering Planning Grant Program for the
Village of Potsdam Wastewater Treatment Plant Study project. Under the Engineering
Planning Grant Program, this local share must be at least 20 percent of total project cost. The
maximum local share appropriated subject to any changes agreed to by the Mayor shall not
exceed $8,900 based upon a total estimated maximum project cost of $38,900. The Mayor
may increase this local match through the use of in-kind services without further approval
from the Village of Potsdam’s Board of Trustees.

Ayes Nays
