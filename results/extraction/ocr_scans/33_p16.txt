The Zoning Board of Appeals ~ Acts upon appeals brought by applicants for area and use
variances; and, is responsible for interpreting the Village’s zoning laws.

The Airport Committee — Advises the Village Board of Trustees on the development, operations
and management of the Potsdam Damon Field Airport.

Joint Town/Village Recreation Committee — Appointed by the Town and Village Boards to
oversee funding, functions and activities of the Recreation Department and advises the Village
Board of Trustees on recreational issues.

The Potsdam Housing Authority Board — Oversees and directs the funding and property
management of publicly and privately owned workforce and elderly housing in the Village of
Potsdam.

The Potsdam Museum Board — Oversees and directs the funding, functions and operations of the
Potsdam Public Museum.

The Comprehensive Planning Committee — Tasked with the development of a draft 10 year
strategic plan for the Village of Potsdam.

The Local Waterfront Revitalization Advisory Committee — Advises the Village Board of
Trustees on waterfront revitalization issues and assist the Village in the planning and
implementation of revitalization activities.

The Downtown Gardening Committee — Is responsible for the planting of flowers to beautify
downtown areas of the Village in concert with the Potsdam Chamber of Commerce.
