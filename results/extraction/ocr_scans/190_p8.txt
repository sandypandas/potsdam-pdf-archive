AGENDA - REGULAR MEETING 6
VILLAGE OF POTSDAM BOARD OF TRUSTEES
JANUARY 5, 2015 — 7:00 P.M.

Now Therefore, Be It Resolved That: The Village Board of Trustees of the Village of
Potsdam, duly convened does hereby:

Resolve, that the Village Board of Trustees of the Village of Potsdam hereby approves the
above subject project; and, it is hereby further:

Resolved, that the Village Board of Trustees of the Village of Potsdam hereby authorizes
the Mayor or in his absence the Deputy Mayor of the Village of Potsdam to pay in the
first instance 100 percent of the Federal and non-Federal share of the cost of Preliminary
and Final Design costs and Construction/Construction Support/Construction Inspection
costs for work for the Project or portions thereof; and is further:

Resolved, that the sum of $503,000.00 is hereby appropriated from the Potsdam Growth
Fund and made available to cover the cost of participation in the above phase of the
Project; and it is further:

Resolved, that in the event the full Federal and non-Federal share costs of the project
exceeds the amount appropriated above, the Village Board of Trustees of the Village of
Potsdam shall convene as soon as possible to appropriate said excess amount immediately
upon notification of the Mayor thereof; and is further:

Resolved, that the Mayor of the Village of Potsdam or in his absence, the Deputy Mayor
of the Village of Potsdam be and is hereby authorized to execute all necessary
Agreements, certifications or reimbursement requests for Federal aid on behalf of the
Village of Potsdam with the New York State Department of Transportation in connection
with the advancement or approval of the Project and providing for the administration of
the Project and the municipality’s first instance funding of project costs and permanent
funding of the local share Federal aid and State-aid eligible project costs and all Project
costs within appropriation thereof that are not eligible; and it is further:

Resolved, that a certified copy of this resolution be filed with the New York State
Commissioner of Transportation by attaching it to any necessary Agreement in

connection with the Project; and it is further:

Resolved, that this Resolution shall take effect immediately.
