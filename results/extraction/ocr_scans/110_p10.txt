Trustee Moved and Trustee Seconded a resolution as follows:

Whereas, the Village is desirous of submitting a grant application to the NYS Archives
Local Government Records Management Improvement Fund (LGRMIFP) for
implementation of a Geographic Information System (GIS) for water and wastewater
records, and

Whereas, other communities in St. Lawrence County have expressed interest in
partnering with the Village for a shared services application for water and wastewater
records implementation using GIS, and

Whereas, the Village has the necessary resources to assist partnering communities by
sponsoring a shared services LGRMIF application and act a lead applicant and fiscal
agent for project implementation if grant funding is awarded, and

Whereas, pursuant to LGRMIF guidelines, it is necessary that the lead agency: formally
appoint a Records Management Officer (RMO), and formally adopt the appropriate
records retention and disposition schedule.
