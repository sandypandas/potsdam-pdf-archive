An Executive Session of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday, July 6,
2015 at 3:40 P.M., Civic Center, Park Street, Potsdam, New York.

PRESENT: Mayor Yurgartis
Trustees Warr & Hopke
Village Administrator Everett Basford

Hopke went into Executive Session for the discussion of contracts at 3:40 p.m.

Discussion of same was had; no formal vote or action was taken in Execution Session.

Session was adjourned at 3:57 P.M.

Village Administrator Everett Basford.
