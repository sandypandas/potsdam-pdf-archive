gallon less than tank truck load delivered.

Slack Chemical - Fluosilicic Acid at $5.99 per gallon delivered, 30 gallon drums.

Holland Company - Poly Aluminum Chloride at $2.32 per gallon tank truck load & $3.23
per gallon less than tank truck load delivered.

Slack Chemical - Liquid Polymer Sta Floc 8837 at $1.43 per pound or Sta Floc 270 at
$1.85 per pound.

Ayes Nays

Trustee Moved and Trustee Seconded a resolution accepting,
with regret, the resignation of Police Officer Peter LaSala from the Village of Potsdam
Police Department effective March 16, 2020.

Ayes Nays

Trustee Moved and Trustee Seconded a resolution agreeing to
fully support the application submitted by the North Country Skateboarders for the
development and construction of a skatepark and outdoor art gallery proposed for
location at the southern potion of Fall Island Park. The Village Board authorizes the
North Country Skateboarders to develop the brick paved mall section of the park subject
to the following conditions:

iF That the North Country Skateboarders secure grant funding through the
Downtown Revitalization Initiative and other grants and/or donations to finance
the development and construction of the skatepark.
