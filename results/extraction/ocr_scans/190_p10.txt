The Regular Meeting of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday,
December 15, 2014 at 7:00 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, New
York.

PRESENT: Mayor Yurgartis
Trustees Warr, Hopke & Tischler

ALSO PRESENT: — Lori 8. Queor, Clerk/Treasurer
David Fenton, Administrator
Everett Basford, Administrator
Bob Henninger, WTP/WWTP Chief Operator
Kevin Bates, Police Chief
Greg Thompson, Code Officer
Fred Hanns, Planning Director

Mayor Yurgartis opened the meeting at 7:00 P.M., and stated that all members were present
except for Trustee Garner.

Mayor Yurgartis then stated that the following sets of minutes had been submitted for Board
approval:

December 1, 2014 Public Hearing 6:40 p.m.
December 1, 2014 Public Hearing 6:50 p.m.
December 1, 2014 Annual Meeting 7:00 p.m.
December 1, 2014 Executive Session 7:18 p.m.

A Motion was made by Trustee Tischler and Seconded by Trustee Warr to approve the minutes as
submitted. All were in favor, none opposed, motion carried.

Mayor’s comments: Mayor Yurgartis stated that there was an accident last week involving two
of the Village’s snow plows. He stated that it occurred Wednesday night during a time when it
was snowing and that it is difficult to drive such wide vehicles especially with the plows under
any condition. He stated that the most serious damage was to our new truck and that it will be
covered by our village insurance.

Trustees’ comments: Trustee Warr stated that he wanted to thank those that are putting money in
the parking meters during this time when there is free parking. Trustee Hopke stated that the
Mayor has appointed herself and Trustee Warr to the Recreation Committee and that the first
meeting is January 14". She then stated that in Fred’s written report it says that his department is
reviewing the Village Code to update it and that she feels we should look at the entire code, not
just the zoning chapters and update the entire code. Trustee Tischler wished everyone a safe,
happy and healthy holiday season.

Administrator’s comments: Administrator Dave Fenton stated that revising the Village Code will
be a great job for someone. He stated that if the weather cooperates, the hangar renovation
should wrap up this week and be completely enclosed. Administrator Everettt Basford stated
that doing a code codification is nothing new to him and would take some time and effort. He
stated that he has learned a lot this past week.
