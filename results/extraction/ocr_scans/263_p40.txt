SERVICES AGREEMENT

This SERVICES AGREEMENT (this “Agreement”) is entered into as of September 1, 2015 (the
“Effective Date”), by and between Air Methods Corporation, a Delaware corporation (“AMC”), whose
place of business is 7301 South Peoria Street, Englewood, Colorado 80112, and The Village of Potsdam,
New York, a municipal government, having its principal place of business at 2 Park Street, Potsdam, NY
13676 (the “Village”) (with “AMC” and the “Village” constituting the “Parties” and each, “Party”).

Recitals
A. The Village has equipment available to provide snow removal services.
B. AMC desires to engage the Village to provide on-call snow removal services at the
premises it leases from the Village.
Agreement

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual representations, promises and agreements
contained herein, the adequacy and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, AMC and the Village
hereby agree as follows:

1. Term of Agreement; Termination. AMC hereby engages the Village to provide on-call
snow removal services, subject to the terms and conditions hereof, for the period commencing as of the
Effective Date and ending on August 31, 2016 (“Term”). Thereafter, the parties, upon their mutual
agreement, will have the option to renew this Agreement for two (2) additional one (1) year terms
(“Renewal Term”). Either Party may terminate this Agreement, without cause, upon thirty (30) days prior
written notice to the other Party.

2. Services. During the Term, the Village shall perform on-call snow removal services for
AMC. The Village agrees to provide snow removal services and maintain its crew in-service in a manner
that allows AMC to respond to emergent flight requests associated with its business as a provider of
emergency medical air ambulance services. Such on-call snow removal services are anticipated to
include, but not be limited to, the Village of Potsdam Highway Department providing, at the request of
AMC, snow removal services to clear snow as needed and for AMC to safely access, egress and ingress,
and take off and land its fixed wing aircraft. The Agreement contemplates approximately fourteen (14)
annual call-ins for snow removal. .

The Parties acknowledge that the Village is neither obligated nor capable of removing all
snow and ice to a “bare surface” and slippery conditions may continue to exist even after the performance
of plowing services. The Village assumes no liability for this naturally occurring condition. AMC
acknowledges that weather conditions may change rapidly and without notice and that the Village
assumes no liability for such changes in conditions. AMC shall maintain its own responsibility to monitor
and inspect the premises.

3. Compensation. AMC shall pay the Village an amount of Five Hundred Twenty Five
Dollars ($525.00) each month during the Term, prorated for any partial month during the Term (the
“Service Fee”).

4, Entire Agreement. This Agreement sets forth the entire agreement and understanding of
the parties hereto with respect to the matters covered hereby and supersedes any prior agreement or
understanding whether oral or written between the parties with respect to the matters covered hereby.
