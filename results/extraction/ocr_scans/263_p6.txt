- Met with Rose Rivezzi of the Town Board regarding the recreation budget for next year
and regarding the $50,000 grant the Town received that the Village needs to accept
because we owned the Arena. The $50,000 is reimbursable funds meaning we have to
spend our money first then apply for reimbursement so the discussions were having the
Town advance the $50,000 seeing as they normally give us their share of the recreation
money at the end of their budget year in December. This is a timing issue and Rose is
here to speak to the issue.

Rose Rivezzi from the Town stated that she reiterates what Everett said. She stated that the
Town Board said yes to advancing the $50,000 and basically they are just paying us for the
recreation program sooner so there is no danger that the will end up $50,000 short. They are just
releasing the $50,000 sooner than they have in the past. Ms. Rivezzi encourage the Board to
authorize Mr. Basford to contact Senator Griffo’s office with the go ahead and authorize Fred
Hanss to prepare the paperwork that needs to be done.
