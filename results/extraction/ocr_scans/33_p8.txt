Ninety-two percent of the Village’s population in 2010 was native born. Of the Village’s residents that
were foreign born, 3 percent have become naturalized citizens of the United States and 5 percent are
citizens of foreign nations. This is roughly comparable the same statistics gathered in 2000.

The recruitment of students by SUNY Potsdam and Clarkson University has declined by 15.4 percent
from the Fall Semester of 2000 to the Spring Semester of 2012, This has less to do with the programs
offered by local universities and more with declining birth rates and net out-migration from New York
State based on a projection of high school graduates prepared by the State University of New York’s
Office of Higher Education Research and Information Systems’. Given the fact that the recruiting of
highly qualified students will continue to be competitive, the Village should work closely with the
universities to coordinate community development activities that will complement their recruiting efforts.
