A Regular Meeting of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday, March 1,
2021 at 6:00 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street Potsdam, NY. The meeting also
included Zoom video conferencing and streamed live to the Village’s Facebook Page due to the
COVID-19 19 Pandemic.

PRESENT: Mayor Tischler
Trustees Warr, McKenna & Jacobs-Wilke

ALSO PRESENT: Lori Queor, Clerk/Treasurer
Fred Hanss, Planning Director
Jim Corbett, DPW Superintendent

VIA ZOOM: Erin Cheney, Museum Director
Greg Thompson, Administrator

Mayor Tischler opened the meeting at 6:04 P.M and stated that all members were present with
the exception of Trustee Lee.

The Mayor then stated that the following sets of minutes had been submitted for Board
acceptance:

February 22, 2021 Regular Meeting 6:00 p.m.

Motion was made by Trustee Jacobs- Wilke and Seconded by Trustee McKenna to accept the
minutes as submitted. All were in Favor — None Opposed — Motion Carried.

Mayor’s comments: Reminder again about COVID-19 and wearing your mask, social
distancing, wash your hands and avoid large gatherings. Be careful, be smart and be safe.
Thank you to Mr. Corbett and Dustin Bradley, Jerry Dibble and Scott Thomas for coming in this
weekend to take care of a roof leak. Job well done and thank you!

Trustee’s Comments:

Trustee Warr— None

Trustee McKenna— Remember from last year that every year when the snow thaws the Water
Treatment Plant has to keep up with the melting snow and will have to add extra chlorine to the
water to keep it safe. You can filter your water or leave it out for 24 hours and that will help. It

is normal, happens every year and if you have questions you can ask the Administrator.

Trustee Jacobs-Wilke — Women’s History Month, I will do a resolution for the next agenda
recognizing it.

Trustee Lee — Not at the meeting.

Administrator's Comments: Administrator Thompson provided a written report which is on file
with the Village Clerk. He then went over his report with the board.
