A Joint Public Hearing of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees and the Town of Potsdam
Council was held on Thursday, November 6, 2014 at 6:15 P.M., in the Potsdam Town Offices
Board Room, Elm Street, Potsdam, New York.

PRESENT: Mayor Yurgartis
Trustees Tischler & Hopke

ALSO PRESENT: David Fenton, Village Administrator
Lori S. Queor, Village Clerk/Treasurer

Marie Regan, Town Supervisor
Rose Revezzi, Town Councilwoman
Judy Rich, Town Councilwoman
Jim Grant, Town Councilman
Rollin Beattie, Town Councilman
Cindy Goliber, Town Clerk

Mayor Yurgartis opened the hearing at 6:15 P.M.

This public hearing is to receive any objections which may be presented against a petition to
annex territory into the Village of Potsdam consisting of approximately 23.33 acres of land
owned by Clarkson University and approximately 157.7 acres of land owned by the Village of

Potsdam.

Legal Notice of the Public Hearing was published in the Courier Observer newspaper on October
17, 2014.

The Mayor then asked for public comments.
There was no public comment.

The Mayor thanked the Town Board for their cooperation and stated that the airport is good for
all the residents of Potsdam.

The Mayor declared the hearing closed at 6:16 P.M.

Respectfully submitted,

Lori S. Queor
Village Clerk
