Village of Potsdam, New York, 2012-2022 Comprehensive Plan

LU-8 The Village should create Master Plans to encourage “Smart Growth” in some available
and undeveloped R-1 zones.

LU- 9 The Village should continue its policy of not providing village services, such as water
and sewer, for any property located outside the village limits.

LU-10 The Village Board of Trustees should task the Village Planning Board to develop policies
and laws that will appropriately site, size and regulate Big Box, formula or other large retail development.

LU-1I The Village Planning Board should develop a maximum parking ratio for commercial,
residential and institutional uses. Include parking lot design standards that reduce large uninterrupted
expanses of pavement; and, work to improve the appearance of existing parking lots.

LU-12 Since the future of the Town and Village of Potsdam are inextricably linked, the Village

should work collaboratively with the Town to develop a Comprehensive Plan for the Town and Village
of Potsdam.
