Additional Resources for Complete Streets Advocates

Research & Informational Resources

Building Healthy Communities - Are Small Towns Best? (Mark Fenton) - Paper produced my Mark
Fenton for a healthy community’s presentation in Elizabethtown, NY. In it he describes how rural

communities can make themselves healthier communities through design.

Bicyclists Bring Business (Parks and Trails NY) — Economic and infrastructure recommendations for
attracting bicycle tourists to New York communities.

CDC Transportation Recommendations (Centers for Disease Control) ~ Provides transportation policy
recommendations that the CDC believes can have a positive impact on community health.

CDC Recommended Community Strategies to Prevent Obesity (Centers for Disease Control) - Identifies

strategies and measurements that local governments and communities can use to plan, implement and
monitor initiatives to prevent obesity.

Essex County Plan iHi (SUNY Albany - iHi) — Study conducted in Essex County, NY - designed to
facilitate statewide efforts to create physical environments which foster healthy active lifestyles.

Biking in Rural Communities (America Bikes) — A short brochure highlighting the positive effects of
biking. Includes studies of rural communities that have made environmental and policy changes to
promote bike-able environments.

Local Government Actions to Prevent Childhood Obesity (Institute of Medicine) — Provides 15

strategies for local governments to encourage healthy eating and physical activity.

Hidden Health Costs of Transportation (American Public Health Association) - Describes how public
transportation can reduce health costs across a large population.

The Economic Value of Active Transportation (Ryan Snyder Associates, LLC) — Provides data on the
increased value of property and increased business which can come out of designing communities for
active transportation.

AARP Pedestrian Mobility Assessment Audit Guide (American Association of Retired Persons) —
Comprehensive guide to conducting street and sidewalk assessments produced by AARP.

ENACT: Environmental Nutrition and Activity Tool (Prevention Institute) — Short policy and built
environment assessments and an interactive menu of strategies designed to help communities enhance
food and activity environments on a local level. It provides communities with "how-to" resources to
prioritize and implement strategies for change.

Bicycle Friendiy Community Program (League of American Bicyclists) - Website with assessments and
a sample action plan for moving the community toward in meeting the criteria for becoming a Bicycle
Friendly Community

Selecting Pedestrian Safety Improvements (Federal Highway Administration) — Provides a complete
matrix of pedestrian related accidents and what design changes could be used to avoid that type of
accident. Gives complete descriptions of many possible design changes.
