Village of Potsdam, New York, 2012-2022 Comprehensive Plan

PFS-13 The Village Board of Trustees should begin planning for major renovations to the

PFS-14 The Department of Public Works should upgrade all sewer lift stations.

Storm Water Management Recommendations

PFS-15 The Village Board of Trustees should encourage the Department of Public Works to
develop a maintenance and improvement program to eliminate known blockages in the storm water
system, including renovation as necessary to meet state and federal standards for the Potsdam or Cross-

Town Canal.

PFS — 16 The Department of Public Works should continue to reduce infiltration and inflow of
storm water so as to reduce the load on the waste water treatment plant.

a.. Consider changes to building lot coverage allowances in the Village zoning law to reduce storm
water run-off.

Street and Sidewalk Recommendations:
PFS-17 Consider requiring the burying of overhead utilities during renovation or construction.

PFS-18 Develop procedures and facilities to improve the cleanliness and appearance of
downtown area sidewalks and alleyways.

Village Police Department Recommendations:

PFS-19 The Police and Fire Departments should evaluate the potential cost/benefit of sharing
Police Department and Fire Department dispatch services.

PFS-20 Encourage the Police Department to continue to strengthen cooperative relationships with
the SUNY Potsdam University Police and the Clarkson University Campus Safety Officers.

PFS- 21 The Village Board of Trustees should encourage the Police Department to continue to
pursue grant opportunities for new equipment.

PFS-22 The Village Board of Trustees should encourage the Police Department to complete the
requirements to gain New York State accreditation and pursue national accreditation as part of an effort
to control the department’s insurance costs.

Potsdam Fire Department Recommendations:

PFS-23 The Village Board of Trustees should encourage the Fire Department to complete a
comprehensive capital expense plan.
