An Executive Session of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday,
September 15, 2014 at 7:42 P.M., in the Board Room, Civic Center, Park Street, Potsdam, New
York.

PRESENT: Mayor Yurgartis
Trustees Warr & Hopke
ALSO PRESENT: David H. Fenton, Jr., Village Administrator

The Board of Trustees, upon a resolution offered by Trustee Warr and seconded by Trustee
Hopke went into Executive Session for the discussion of the employment history of a particular
person at 7:42 p.m.

Discussion of same was had; no formal vote or action was taken in Execution Session.

Upon a resolution offered by Trustee Warr and seconded by Trustee Hopke the Executive
Session was adjourned at 8:12 P.M.

The undersigned was not in attendance at this Executive Session. Information was provided by
Village Administrator David Fenton.

Respectfully submitted,

Lori S$. Queor
Village Clerk
