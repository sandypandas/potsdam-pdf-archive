A Public Hearing of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday, April 6,
2020 at 5:55 P.M., via Zoom Meeting due to the Coronavirus Pandemic.

PRESENT: Mayor Tischler
Trustees McKenna, Jacobs-Wilke & Lee

ALSO PRESENT: Lori S. Queor, Clerk/Treasurer
Fred Hanss, Planning Director
Greg Thompson, Administrator
Mayor Tischler opened the hearing at 5:55 p.m. He stated that this public hearing is to receive
public comments on a proposed Local Law to amend Chapter 153, Taxation Article IX Tax Cap
Override. He took the roll stating that all members were present except Trustee Warr.

Village Clerk, Lori Queor, then read the Legal Notice as published.

Public Comments: There were no public comments and Clerk Queor stated that she had not
received any public comment via email, regular mail or telephone call.

Hearing no public comments, the Mayor then closed the hearing at 5:57 P.M.

Respectfully submitted,

Lori S. Queor
Village Clerk
