Village of Potsdam, New York, 2012-2022 Comprehensive Plan

Land Use

For the last decade, the Village of Potsdam has based its land use decision-making including zoning, site
plan, special use and variance applications on a “well considered” comprehensive plan that offers a
rational basis for actions taken by the Board of Trustees, the Village Planning Board and the Zoning
Board of Appeals. The St. Lawrence County Planning Board considers the Village’s comprehensive plan
when undertaking project reviews under General Municipal Law §239-m. The NYS Planning Federation
also notes that other government agencies such as the Town of Potsdam, St. Lawrence County, the state
or federal governments must consider the Village’s comprehensive plan when making plans for their
respective capital projects (such as highway reconstruction).’

Land use in the Village is regulated by zoning codes whose primary function is to protect the public’s
health, safety and welfare as well as its financial investment in real property. The Village presently has 13
zoning districts. Of these 4 are residential zoning districts, including: R-1 which delineates areas where
single-family housing is recommended as the predominant use. The R-2 zoning district delineates areas
_ where single and 2 family housing is appropriate. The R-3 zoning district encourages a wide range of
housing options including single-family, 2 family and multiple family residential uses. The R-4 zoning
district is designed to accommodate group dwellings and multiple family residential developments.

There are a number of commercial and industrial zoning districts within the Village, including: The B-]
zoning district which is intended support and encourage retail and commercial development. The B-2
_ zoning district is designed to encourage less intense commercial uses such as professional office space or
personal service businesses. The BLI zoning district delineates areas appropriate for business and light
industrial or assembly compatible with surrounding land uses. The BLI-2 zoning district delineates those
areas of the community that are most appropriate for light industrial, manufacturing and assembly land
uses. The industrial zoning district delineates those areas of the community best suited for heavy and
enclosed manufacturing. A Planned Unit Development District is designed to provide a flexible zoning
district that may accommodate innovative land uses.

In 2009, the Village Board adopted a local law creating the Health Care 1 and Health Care 2 zoning
districts to plan for the future growth and expansion of the Canton-Potsdam Hospital’s campus located at
50 Leroy Street. The H-1 zoning district permits the intensive development of the campus to address the
anticipated growing importance of the health care industry both as a necessary community facility and
also as an engine for local economic growth and development. The H-2 zoning district delineates an area
surrounding the hospital campus that essentially buffers and minimizes potentially negative impacts on
the adjacent residential neighborhood.
