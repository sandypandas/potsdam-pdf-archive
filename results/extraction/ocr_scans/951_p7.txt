Why not expand at the Lawrence Avenue Campus?

Brent stated that it would be too large a capital outlay of funds, it would be four to five times the
cost of the current project. He stated that there would be concerns about transporting of patients
between facilities and as the demands of finance in health care go up, the bottom line shrinks.

He stated that these decisions have not been made overnight, that there have been years of design
and development, feasibility cost estimating and planning and building elsewhere would cost
four times the amount.

How will you ensure a good buffer between the hospital and the neighborhood?

Brent stated that there are a number of details that will need to be worked out over the next six
months including the buffer. He stated that they would get experts involved and would ask for
comments/thoughts from the neighbors and they will do the best job they can do so that it is done
right and the noise, light, etc. concerns are addressed.

Is the long term goal to expand out to Market Street?

Brent stated that, no not to his knowledge, this specific expansion has been talked about for years
and it will be 2024 before it is done. He stated that the hospital’s pockets are only so deep and
there is no plan for the long term to expand out to Market Street. He stated that is far from truth.

Trustee McKenna suggest that Brent work with the creative people in the community to solve
some of these issues.

Brent stated that the hospital would be sure to get input from the community.
