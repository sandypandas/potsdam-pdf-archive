An Executive Session of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday,
November 16, 2020 at 6:32 p.m., via Zoom meeting.

PRESENT: Mayor Tischler
Trustees McKenna, Jacobs-Wilke & Lee
Administrator Greg Thompson

The Board of Trustees, upon a resolution offered by Trustee Jacobs-Wilke and seconded by
Trustee McKenna went into Executive Session for the discussion of the employment history of
particular persons and contract negotiations at 6:32 p.m.

Discussion of same was had; no formal vote or action was taken in Execution Session.

Upon a resolution offered by Trustee Lee and seconded by Trustee Jacobs-Wilke the Executive
Session was adjourned at 7:03 p.m.

The undersigned was not in attendance at this meeting and information was provided to her by
Mayor Tischler.

Respectfully submitted,

Lori S. Queor
Village Clerk
