A Public Hearing of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday, September
15, 2014 at 6:45 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, New York.

PRESENT: Mayor Yurgartis
Trustees Warr & Hopke
ALSO PRESENT: David Fenton, Village Administrator

Mayor Yurgartis opened the hearing at 6:45 P.M. He stated that this public hearing is to receive
public comments on a proposed Local Law to amend Chapter 168, Vehicles & Traffic to prohibit
parking at all times on the South Side of Cottage Street from Waverly Street to Leroy Street.

The Mayor then read the Legal Notice as published.

The Mayor then asked for public comments.

There was no public comment.

The Mayor declared the hearing closed at 6:47 P.M.

Respectfully submitted,

Lori S. Queor
Village Clerk
