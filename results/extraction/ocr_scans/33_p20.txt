With respect to land-use, the Village faces several challenges, including:

It has become clear that the Village’s zoning and sub-division regulations are becoming increasingly
unwieldy and in some cases out-dated. For the Village to remain competitive in the attraction and
retention of commercial enterprises, as a location where real estate development is encouraged and
community values are upheld, the Village Board should consider a complete review of §180 and §A186
of the Village Code.

There are properties situated within the Village that are zoned B-1 that have varying degrees of
environmental contamination. The Village should take steps to identify parcels that might be most
desirable for re-development and work with the property owners to undertake remediation and market the
property for new development.

With respect to new opportunities, the 2001-2011 Comprehensive Plan recommended that the Village
develop and implement a waterfront revitalization strategy. Using NYS Local Waterfront Revitalization
Program grant funds and in-kind assistance from Village staff and resident volunteers, a plan entitled the
- 2012 Downtown and Waterfront Revitalization Plan was developed and subsequently was adopted by the
Village Board of Trustees. With a formal plan in hand, the Village will be able to target local, state and
federal funds on a wide range of downtown revitalization and waterfront development projects that
together will result in Potsdam being an more attractive place to live, work, study and play. These
activities will change the face of the community in ways that respect the community’s past and position it
for the future.

Land Use Recommendations
LU-1 The Village Board of Trustees should task the Village Planning Board to undertake a
complete review of the Village’s zoning, sub-division and site plan laws to ensure that they are not

conflicting, are up-to-date and consistent with Smart Growth principles.

LU-2 The Village Board of Trustees should encourage annexation of property into the Village
limits so Jong as the annexation is in the best public interest of the Village and the Town of Potsdam.
