AGENDA - REGULAR MEETING 5
VILLAGE OF POTSDAM BOARD OF TRUSTEES
JANUARY 5, 2015 ~ 7:00 P.M.

Trustee __.. Moved and Trustee __.. Seconded the following resolution:

Whereas, various Federal or State aided transportation programs, as authorized by actions
of the U.S. Congress or the New York State Legislature, require several local municipal
approvals, applications, and agreements for projects receiving funding; and,

Whereas, the New York State Department of Transportation’s Locally Administered
Federal Aid Procedures (LAFA) manual requires that of these documents, Non-Standard
Feature Approval, Design Approval, Right-of-Way Certificates and Contract Document
Approval must be given by the Mayor; and,

Whereas, Approvals of other required documents, including but not limited to SEQR
determinations, Right-of-Way Certificates, State and Federal permits, utility relocation
and/or railroad agreements, Contract Document Approval, and reimbursement requests
may be delegated by the Mayor.

Now Therefore, Be It Resolved That: the Mayor is hereby authorized to approve any
documents required to progress these Federal and State projects that have been authorized
by the Village of Potsdam; and, be it further:

Resolved, that the Mayor may delegate this authority to the extent permitted by the LAFA
guidelines to the appropriate Village officials.

Ayes Nays

Trustee Moved and Trustee Seconded a resolution as follows:
Whereas, a Project for the Village of Potsdam-Downtown Riverwalk, (“the Project” is
eligible under the Transportation Alternatives Program, P.I.N. 7806.03 for funding under
Title 23 U.S. Code, as amended, that calls for the apportionment of costs such program to
borne at the ratio of 80 percent Federal funds and 20 percent non-Federal funds; and,

Whereas, the Village of Potsdam desires to advance the Project by making a commitment
of 100 percent of the non-federal share of costs of the of the Preliminary and Final Design
costs and Construction/Construction Support /Construction Inspection costs for the
Project or portion thereof, with the Federal share of such costs to be applied directly by
the New York State Department of Transportation (“NYSDOT”) pursuant to Agreement;
and,

Whereas, the estimated cost of the Preliminary and Final Design costs and Construction/
Construction Support/Construction Inspection costs for the Project is estimated as
$503,000.00.
