An Executive Session of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday, January
24, 2022 at 3:00 p.m., Civic Center, Park Street, Potsdam, New York.

PRESENT: Mayor Tischler
Trustees Warr, Tirion & Jacobs-Wilke
Administrator Thompson
The Board of Trustees, upon a resolution offered by Trustee Jacobs-Wilke and seconded by
Trustee Tirion went into Executive Session for the discussion of the employment history of
particular persons at 3:02 p.m.

Discussion of same was had; no formal vote or action was taken in Execution Session.

Upon a resolution offered by Trustee Jacobs-Wilke and seconded by Trustee Tirion the
Executive Session was adjourned at 3:38 p.m.

The undersigned was not in attendance at the Executive Session. Information is provided by
Mayor Tischler.

Respectfully submitted,

Lori S. Queor
Village Clerk
