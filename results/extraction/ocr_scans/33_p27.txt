The Village Hydro-Electric Plants

PES-37 Develop a project and financing schedule for the following long-term capital
improvement needs at the East Dam hydro-electric plant:

b. Rebuilding the retaining wall on Fall Island.
c. Repairing at the East Dam.

d. Updating and automating control systems.
e. Replacing of the plant roof.

PFS-38 Use net revenues over and above the East and West Dam hydro-electric operating costs to
supplement General Fund revenues.

PFS-39 The Village Board of Trustees should consider the development of a hydro-electric power
interpretive center as part of its tourism infrastructure.

Urban and Community Forestry

PFS-40 Revise, update and continue to implement the short and long-term recommendations of
the 1999 Village of Potsdam Urban and Community Forest Master Plan to include:

a. Continue to plant at least 20 to 40 new trees annually along public rights-of-way and on Village-
owned properties; and, develop a short-term program to replace street and parkland trees lost
during the July 2012 straight-line storm and future storm events.

b. Emphasize improved landscaping on Outer Market Street.

c. Emphasize new plantings on Pierrepont Avenue. and Bay Street.

d. Continue the existing long-term tree maintenance program.
