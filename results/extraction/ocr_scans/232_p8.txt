A Public Hearing of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday, April 6,
2015 at 6:30 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, New York.

PRESENT: Mayor Yurgartis
Trustees Hopke, Tischler & Garner

ALSO PRESENT: Lori S. Queor, Clerk/Treasurer
Everett Basford, Village Administrator
Fred Hanss, Planning Director
Greg Thompson, Code Officer
Kevin Bates, Police Chief

Mayor Yurgartis opened the hearing at 6:30 P.M. He stated that this public hearing is to receive
public comments on the tentative Village budget for Fiscal Year 2015-2016.

Village Clerk, Lori Queor, then read the Legal Notice as published.

The Mayor then asked for public comments.

Michael Ells of 15 Walnut Street addressed the board. He spoke regarding his increasing taxes
due to his new assessment. He stated that he felt Clarkson University should help out more and
that the village people of Potsdam cannot continue to pay 100% of the taxes for 40-50% of
taxable property.

Dean Laubscher of 55 Lower Pine Street addressed the board. He seconded Mr. Ells sediments.
He stated that he is on a fixed income and between the water and sewer rates and taxes he is not
sure how long he can continue to live here. He stated his assessment went up $20,000 and that

Clarkson needs to chip in.

Hearing no further public comment, the Mayor declared the hearing closed at 6:49 P.M.

Respectfully submitted,

Lori S. Queor
Village Clerk
