LOCAL LAW #1-2015

Section 1. Purpose.
The Purpose of this Local Law is to Amend Chapter 180, Zoning, Article [ll, Section 180-6 Zoning
Map of the Village of Potsdam Municipal Code.

Section 2. Amendment.

The zoning map shall be amended to change the zone designation from R-2 to BLI for the property located
at 6 Prospect Place, Potsdam, NY Tax Map #64.067-3-21.3 and for the property located at 71 Main Street,
Potsdam, NY Tax Map #64.067-3-22.

Section 3. Knactment.
This Local Law shall become effective immediately upon the notification of the filing of same
with the Secretary of State.
