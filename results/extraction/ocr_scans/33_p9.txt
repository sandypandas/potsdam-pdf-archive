Given that Potsdam is home to 2 universities, SUNY Potsdam and Clarkson University, it is anticipated
that much of its population is comprised of students. In 2000, 58.4 percent of the Village’s population
was reported to be between the ages of 15 and 24. With the differences in the presentation of data
between the Census of Population and Housing and the American Community Survey, it is difficult to
exactly match the data, however the American Community Survey reported that 37 percent of the
Village’s population were between the ages of 20 and 24. The American Community Survey also
reported that the median age in the Village of Potsdam is 20.7 years compared to 36.8 years for St.
Lawrence County and 36.5 for the United States overall.
