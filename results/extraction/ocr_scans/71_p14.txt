A Special Meeting of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday, September
22, 2014 at 9:00 A.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, New York.

PRESENT: Mayor Steve Yurgartis
Trustees Warr, Hopke & Tischler

ALSO PRESENT: David H. Fenton, Jr., Administrator
Lori 8. Queor, Clerk/Treasurer

Mayor Yurgartis opened the meeting at 9:00 A.M., and took the roll and stated that all members
were present with the exception of Trustee Garner.

NEW BUSINESS:

Trustee Tischler Moved and Trustee Warr Seconded a resolution accepting the Grant
Offer for Airport Improvement Program (AIP) Project No. 3-36-0099-030-2014 from the
Federal Aviation Administration for the Potsdam Airport Damon Field Runway 6-24
Rehabilitation (Design Only); Replace Runway 6-24 Medium Intensity Runway Lighting
~ MIRL (Design Only). Further authorizing the Mayor to execute said grant agreement.

AYES 4 (Mayor Yurgartis) NAYS 0
(Trustee Warr, Hopke & Tischler )

Trustee Tischler Moved and Trustee Warr Seconded a resolution to adjourn the Special
Meeting of the Village Board of Trustees held on September 22, 2014 at 9:01 a.m.

AYES 4 (Mayor Yurgartis) NAYS 0
(Trustees Warr, Hopke & Tischler)

Respectfully submitted,

Lori S. Queor
