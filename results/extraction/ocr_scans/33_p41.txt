Village of Potsdam, New York, 2012-2022 Comprehensive Plan

Recreation

The Village of Potsdam. is fortunate to have approximately 15 acres of municipally -owned parkland
situated along or adjacent to the Raquette River waterfront. The development of waterfront parkland is a
high priority in the Village’s 2012 Downtown and Waterfront Revitalization Plan, including passive
recreation facilities at Ives Park and more active recreation proposed for Fall Island Park. In addition,
both SUNY Potsdam and Clarkson University have waterfront recreation areas that are open for use by
the general public.

The Potsdam Joint Recreation Committee is a volunteer advisory group charged with working with
Recreation Department staff to plan for and implement a community-wide recreation program. The
Sandstoner Park and Pine Street Arena are situated on Pine Street and offers a wide range of seasonal
recreational activities including swimming, swimming instructions, boating, and field sports during the
spring, summer and autumn seasons and winter sports such as figure skating and ice hockey. The park
also provides a play ground facility for children and a picnic facility. Youth athletic associations as well
as school-based sports teams and sports clubs take advantage of the excellent facilities available at
Sandstoner Park.

In addition to Sandstoner Park, the Recreation Program offers swimming lessons and open public
swimming available at Postwood Park located in nearby Hannawa Falls. Postwood Park offers arguably
the best swimming beach in St. Lawrence County. St. Lawrence County maintains hiking and cross
country skiing trails in the County forest adjacent to Postwood Park creating year-round active recreation
opportunities.

SUNY Potsdam and Clarkson University also provide recreational facilities to the community including
the use of Lehman Park, Clarkson University’s Munter Trails along the western shoreline of this river and
multi-use trail system on Clarkson Hill. Both universities offer memberships for use of their athletic
facilities and fitness centers.

Brookfield Renewable Energy and the Adirondack Mountain Club maintain the popular Red Sandstone
Trail that stretches from Hannawa Falls to Potsdam along the Raquette River. This 3 4 mile long hiking
trail offers a wide range of wooded terrain and an up close view of the process of generating hydro-
electric power at the Sugar Island Dam.

Finally, The Village of Potsdam has taken the lead in the development of canoe trails along the Raquette
with the development of the “Log Driver’s Trail” that is proposed to link communities from Piercefield to
Akwesasne with a Blueway trail. The first portion of the trail linking Potsdam and Norwood will be
completed in 2013.
