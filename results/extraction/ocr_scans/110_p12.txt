A Regular Meeting of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday, November
3, 2014 at 7:00 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, New York.

PRESENT: Mayor Yurgartis
Trustees Warr, Hopke & Tischler

ALSO PRESENT: — David H. Fenton, Jr., Village Administrator
Lori S. Queor, Village Clerk/Treasurer
Bob Henninger, WTP/WWTP Chief Operator
Fred Hanss, Planning Director

Mayor Yurgartis opened the meeting at 7:00 P.M., and stated that all members were present with
the exception of Trustee Garner.

Mayor Yurgartis then stated that the following sets of minutes had been submitted for Board
approval:

October 20, 2014 Regular Meeting 7:00 p.m.
October 20, 2014 Executive Session 7:23 p.m.

A Motion was made by Trustee Tischler and Seconded by Trustee Warr to approve the minutes
as submitted. All were in favor, none opposed, motion carried.

Mayor’s comments: None

Trustees’ comments: Trustee Warr stated that Old Main has been refurbished and it looks very
nice. Trustee Hopke had no comments. Trustee Tischler encouraged everyone to get out and
vote tomorrow.

Administrator’s Comments: Administrator Fenton stated that work is continuing on the hangar
door at the airport, work will be done on the back side of the hangar as well and the door is
scheduled to be delivered on Wednesday. He stated that the East Dam Hydro has been
dewatered, scaffolding has been put up and the gear box will be opened from the bottom to see
what is wrong with it. The Administrator also encouraged everyone to get out and vote
tomorrow, Election Day.

Recreation Director’s Report: Recreation Director Tim Carey was working so was not available
to report. He provided a written report which is on file with the Village Clerk.

Public Comment: Susan Sharlow of 46 Waverly Street asked to the board to make no parking
on the side of Waverly street where they hospital owns property because cars are parking on both
sides of the street making it hard to get down the street. She stated it is mostly between Cottage
and Grove Streets. Mayor Yurgartis stated that he would bring the issue to the parking
committee for their recommendation.
