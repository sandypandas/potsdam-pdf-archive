AGENDA — REGULAR MEETING 3
VILLAGE OF POTSDAM BOARD OF TRUSTEES
OCTOBER 4, 2021 — 6:00 P.M.

Trustee Moved and Trustee Seconded a resolution to hold a
public hearing on Monday, October 18, 2021 at 5:30 p.m. in the Civic Center Board
Room, Park Street, Potsdam, NY. The purpose of the hearing is to receive public
comments on a proposed local law to amend Chapter 180 Zoning, Attachment 15, H-1

Zoning District to change one and two family dwellings from a Special Use, as it appears
now, to a Standard Use.

Ayes Nays

Trustee Moved and Trustee Seconded a resolution that the
Village Board agrees to being part of a three-way partnership between the Village, the
North Country Children’s Museum and the North Country Arts Council to seek grant
funding for instruments to be placed in Garner Park.

Ayes Nays

Trustee Moved and Trustee Seconded a resolution to go into
Executive Session for the discussion of contractual obligations at p.m.
Ayes Nays

Trustee Moved and Trustee Seconded a resolution to adjourn
the Executive Session at p.m.

Ayes Nays

Trustee Moved and Trustee Seconded a resolution to adjourn
the Regular Meeting of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees of October 4, 2021,
at p.m.

Ayes Nays
