The Annual Meeting of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday,

December 6, 2021 at 6:00 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, New
York.

Prior to the meeting, a swearing in ceremony was held for Trustees Warr and Tirion.

PRESENT: Mayor Tischler
Trustees Warr, Tirion, McKenna,
Jacobs-Wilke & Lee

ALSO PRESENT: Lori S. Queor, Village Clerk/Registrar
Jim Corbett, DPW Superintendent
Cara Adams, Treasurer
Fred Hanss, Planning Director

Mayor Tischler opened the meeting at 6:07 P.M., pointed out the exits on the North and East
Walls and advised that the board room has the capacity for 109 people.

The Mayor then stated that all members were present.

The Mayor then stated that the following sets of minutes had been submitted for Board
acceptance:

November 15, 2021 Public Input Session 5:30 p.m.
November 15, 2021 Regular Meeting 6:00 p.m.
November 15, 2021 Executive Session 6:52 p.m.

Motion was made by Trustee Lee and Seconded by Trustee Jacobs-Wilke to accept the minutes
as submitted. All Were in Favor — None Opposed — Motion Carried.

Mayor’s comments: Welcome to Trustee Monique Tirion and re-welcome Trustee Warr and I
look forward to working with you both on the board. Covid 19 still here and there is the new
Omicron variant so please wear masks, social distance, and avoid large gatherings.

Trustee’s Comments:
Trustee Warr — Welcome Monique Tirion to our board and I look forward to working with you.

Trustee Tirion — Attended the St. Lawrence County Mayor’s dinner last Thursday and found it
very informative and helpful. We have volunteered to hold the next meeting at the Cactus Grill
and I was thinking we could have Sharon Williams do a tour of the Children’s Museum prior to
dinner — the Mayor and Board agreed this was a good idea. Trustee Tirion then stated that she
was happy to be on the board.

Trustee Jacobs-Wilke — Welcome Monique to the board. We are still taking nominations for the
Citizen’s Advisory Committee, just looking for village residents at this time. If there is someone
who has a specific expertise that is not a village resident, we may appointment them due to that
expertise. The hospital will be presenting plans for their expansion to the Planning and Zoning
Boards and some elements of their plan have changed so residents who are interested should go
to those meetings to see what is going on.
