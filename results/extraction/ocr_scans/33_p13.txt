Village of Potsdam, New York, 2012-2022 Comprehensive Plan

With less than 30 percent of its housing stock being owner-occupied, the Village of Potsdam has a
declining number of community stakeholders who typically volunteer for essential community services
such as the fire department and who are engaged in civic affairs, either as an elected official or as board
volunteers. The decline of homeownership continues to be cause for alarm as it was in the 2001-2011
Comprehensive Plan.

The U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) has defined gross housing costs (rent or
mortgage principal and interest) costs in excess of 30 percent of a household’s income as “unaffordable”
Using this definition for housing affordability, 59 percent of renters and 37 percent of homeowners pay in
excess of 30 percent of their annual income on housing costs. Of interest is that in 1990 12 percent of
homeowners paid in excess of 30 percent of their household income on housing expenses, thus over the
decade, homeownership has become increasingly expensive within the Village.
