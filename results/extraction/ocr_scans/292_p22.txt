Councilwoman Rich stated that she is going say no to the annexation as more and more land is
being taken over by the colleges and every dollar we get in taxes counts. Councilwoman Carvil
stated that she too is concerned about taxation and if the Agbotics is profit making being
transparent about paying taxes on it but she would like to see it in writing. Mr. Giordano stated
that he can’t predict the future of the Agbotics but they will be transparent that if it becomes a
business they will pay taxes on it. Councilwoman Carvil stated that one of the criteria for
granting the annexation is that it is in the public’s interest and she asked how it is. Mr. Goldie
stated that other than taking an orphaned parcel that is in the middle of property that is part of the
village and making it part of the village he really couldn’t answer that. Councilwoman Rich
stated that she would feel better if Clarkson could just pay us some money. Mr. Goldie informed
the boards that when they acquired the property they did pay all the back taxes. Mr. Cappello
stated that the previous owner never paid taxes for decades and that the county would not take
the property because it was contaminated making the property virtually useless. Mr. Goldie
stated that Clarkson was aware of this and assumes all risks that come with the property.
Administrator Basford did inform the boards that he would defer to Mr. Cappello but that state
law would not allow the boards to demand any payment in order to complete the annexation
process.

Dean Laubscher stated he lives in the village and he is not opposed to the annexation but as the
footprint of the colleges, hospital and churches expands the tax base gets smaller and the rest of
the structures have to support that. He stated that he is glad to hear that the boards are looking
out for the taxpayers.

Hearing no further public comment, the Mayor declared the hearing closed at 6:38 P.M.
