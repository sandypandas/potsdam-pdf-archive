Members of the Board:

Clarkson University proposes to re-zone two parcels they presently own from existing R-2 to BLI to become the same
zone as the balance of their property on their ‘downtown’ campus.

The intended future use for the Prospect Place parcel is for parking for their increasing use of the existing buildings for
new and expanding academic programs. There is no intended future use for the lot on the comer of Main and State

Streets at this time.

The Owner is requesting the Village consider re-zoning parcel 64.067-3-2 (6 Prospect Place) and parcel 64.067-3-22
(71 Main Street) from R-2 to BLI.

Actual future construction within the proposed re-zone is conceptual at this point, would be accomplished in phases,
and would be based on a site plan design yet to be developed.

. The basis for the request for the re-zone at this time is as follows:

° Prospect Place: The current R-2 use of the Prospect Place Jot and building has been superseded. There is no
plan by Clarkson to repair the existing vacant former fraternity house nor to restore residential use to this
parcel.

° Main St: Clarkson does not intend to develop this lot for an R-2 use nor to sell it to a buyer who might develop
it for this use. .

° . Prospect Place: The current land use needs by Clarkson involve space for adequate parking for their new and
, expanding academic programs in the existing buildings adjacent to this property. This parcel can provide the
needed acreage to further develop adequate downtown campus parking.
. Main St: Clarkson has no plans for development of this lot in any way at this time, however, they wish to have
it zoned as part of their downtown campus rather than as a separate residential} lot.

. Prospect Place and Main St: The current Clarkson downtown campus is zoned BLI. Ownership of the two
subject parcels by Clarkson and intended use of the two parcel by Clarkson as part of their downtown campus
is the basis for requesting that all of the lands of their downtown campus are zoned the same.

. There is no intent to sell these properties now or in the future.

. There is no reasonable chance that these lots would in the future be developed as R-2 uses by Clarkson.
