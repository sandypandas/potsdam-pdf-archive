A Regular Meeting of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday, December
19, 2022, at 6:00 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, New York.

PRESENT: Mayor Tischler
Trustees Tirion, Jacobs- Wilke & Lee

ALSO PRESENT: Lori S. Queor, Village Clerk/Registrar
Mark Murray, Police Chief
Trey Smutz, Recreation Director
Fred Hanss, Planning Director
Greg Thompson, Administrator

Mayor Tischler opened the meeting at 6:03 P.M., pointed out the exits on the North and East
Walls and advised that the board room has the capacity for 109 people.

The Mayor then stated that all members were present except Trustee Warr.

The Mayor then stated that the following sets of minutes had been submitted for Board
acceptance:

December 5, 2022 Annual Meeting 6:00 p.m.

Motion was made by Trustee Lee and Seconded by Trustee Jacobs-Wilke to accept the minutes
as submitted. All Were in Favor — None Opposed — Motion Carried.

Mayor’s comments: I commend the DPW, P.D. and Fire Department for the outstanding job
with the snowstorm Thursday night into Friday. Thank you-to all of them and keep up the good
work. Covid, flu and RSV are out there so be cautious and smart. Happy Hanukkah, Merry
Christmas, and Happy Kwanza.

Trustee’s Comments:

Trustee Tirion — Attended two zoom meetings. The first one was regarding the Extreme Heat
Action Plan and what issues we should be worried about. The second one was the Eco Adapt
Climate Change and what should we prepare for. There are five topics, and one will need to be
cut out: Agriculture & Food, Water & Infrastructure, Transportation, Utilities & Housing. The
Town and Village of Potsdam and the Town and Village of Canton can each have 10 experts sit
in on meetings, so we need names of 10 people if anyone has any suggestions, please let me
know. DPW did a very good job from my perspective with the snowstorm clean up.
Outstanding work by the volunteers at the Rescue Squad and Fire Department also. Holidays
can be a joyous time or a time of loss, and I encourage you to make use of the services that are
available.

Trustee Jacobs-Wilke — Greg has done more research regarding the trash contract, and he will
report on that later, but I want to say I was swayed by the comments at the last board meeting to
keep the system as it is. I think it is time to adopt the Snow Removal Policy. World University
Games are coming and SUNY Potsdam, Clarkson University and SUNY Canton have done a lot
of preparations for these games. Tickets are available so please support this great opportunity.
