A Regular Meeting of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday, August
17, 2015 at 7:00 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, New York.

PRESENT: Deputy Mayor Tischler
Trustees Warr & Hopke

ALSO PRESENT: _ Everett Basford, Village Administrator
Lori S. Queor, Village Clerk/Treasurer
Greg Thompson, Code Officer
Kevin Bates, Police Chief
Bob Henninger, WTP/WW'1P Chief Operator

Deputy Mayor Tischler opened the meeting at 7:00 P.M., and stated that all members were
present with the exception of Mayor Yurgartis and Trustee Garner. He then pointed out the
location of the exits on the north and east walls of the Board Room and advised that the Board
Room has a capacity for 109 people.

Deputy Mayor Tischler then stated that the following sets of minutes had been submitted for
Board approval:

August 3, 2015 Regular Meeting 3:00 p.m.

A Motion was made by Trustee Hopke and Seconded by Trustee Warr to approve the minutes as
submitted. All were in favor, none opposed, motion carried.

Mayor’s comments: Deputy Mayor Tischler proclaimed September as Potsdam Sandstone
Festival Month.

Trustees’ comments: None

Administrator’s Comments: Administrator Everett Basford reported to the board on the
following:

- WWTP Digester cleaning is going slower than originally estimated because of a layer of
compacted sandstone type substance but should be completed in a couple more days.

- Report from DANC and Barton & Loguidice on the Waste Water Treatment Plant
upgrade is done and the estimate for a total upgrade is 10.2 million dollars. This estimate
could change a little and there may be a special meeting needed to finalize resolutions for
the grant application.

- East Hydro Dam both gear boxes removed and are at Northern Machining. Mr. Basford
stated that he went to Northern Machining himself and met with the owner to discuss the
gear boxes. Gear Box #2 is not a bearing failure, the gears are eating themselves up. The
gears cannot be rebuilt by Northern Machining. Gear Box #1 popped a bearing and we
have no real idea as to what could have caused it. Mr. Basford said until we know
exactly what is going on with the gear boxes and the root cause of the issues we should
not put any further money into them.
