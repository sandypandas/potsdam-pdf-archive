Downtown Streetscape Renovation and Riverwalk Trail Project
C1001883

RFP Respondent Review Sheet
Respondent: La Bella Associates

Reviewer: Fred Hanss

Responsiveness to the Request for Proposal — 10 points maximum

Reviewer’s Comments & Score: I found LaBella to be highly responsive to the RFP — based in part on
the firm’s prior experience on Village LWRP funded downtown and waterfront revitalization projects.
CLA Sites was a sub-contractor to M.J. Engineering in the landscape design work for the DRI Strategic
Investment Plan. Jade has also worked a sub-contractor to La Bella on all of the Village’s streetlighting
projects at Ives and Garner Park.

Score: 10 Points

Understanding of the Scope of Work — 20 points maximum

Reviewer’s Comments & Score: La Bella has an excellent understanding of the scope of work for the
streetscape and riverwalk trail projects. I appreciate the common-sense approach that LaBella and its
team members are bringing to the project. CLA grasps the landscape design opportunities at the mural
wall and Martin Park. LaBella specifically noted that Jade Engineering would work with National Grid
on the abandonment of existing light poles. No other respondent addressed this.

Score: 19 Points

Proposed Schedule and Budget — maximum 15 points

Reviewer’s Comments & Score: LaBella’s proposed schedule is realistic. Its proposed fee is equal to
7.2 % of project costs. This will free-up grant funding for development and construction costs. The lower
fee is due in large part to the fact the firm is familiar with the proposed project; and, has prepared
topographical survey work and draft designs through a previous LWRP grant.

Score: 15 Points.
