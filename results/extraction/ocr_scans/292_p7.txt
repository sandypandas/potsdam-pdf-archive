AGENDA ~ REGULAR MEETING
VILLAGE OF POTSDAM BOARD OF TRUSTEES
JANUARY 18, 2016 — 7:00 P.M.

Trustee Moved and Trustee Seconded a resolution authorizing
the Village of Potsdam to implement the proposed Downtown Riverwalk. Further
resolved that the Village Board of Trustees authorizes and directs the Mayor or in his
absence the Deputy Mayor to execute a scope of services agreement with Fisher
Associates for engineering design services, right-of-way consulting services and
construction administration; and, further resolved to pay for said services using grant
funding provided by the New York State Department of State Local Waterfront
Revitalization Program C-1000251 and the New York State Department of
Transportation’s Transportation Alternatives Program PIN780603; and, further utilizing
grant funding from the New York State Department of State’s Local Waterfront
Revitalization Program C-1000547 and the NYS Department of Transportation’s
Transportation Alternatives Program PIN 780603 and Village equity.

Ayes Nays

the Executive Session at p.m.
