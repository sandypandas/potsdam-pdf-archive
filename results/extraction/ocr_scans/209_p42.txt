C. Environmental Justice

The new alignment passes through an NYSDEC potential EJ area in St. Lawrence County in
proximity to several potential EJ Areas throughout the study area (see Figure 7), presenting the
potential for disproportionately high and adverse human health or environmental effects on these
communities.

D. Section 4(f) Resources

The new alignment would be located in close proximity to several Section 4(f) resources
identified in Chapter I, Section C-4 of this technical memorandum. Therefore, the new
alignment could result in a direct or indirect use of these resources.

E. Section 6(f) Resources

Use of Section 6(f) resources identified in Chapter I, Section C-5 of this technical memorandum
due to the new alignment is unlikely. In its current conceptual stage, the alignment avoids these
resources and is not expected to affect them.
