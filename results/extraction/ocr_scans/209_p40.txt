In the No-Build scenario, the majority of the traffic travels on highways that have an average
crash rate of 2.68 C/MVM. In population centers, the crash rates tend to be higher if it is
assumed that the urban crash rates apply. The proposed alignment as a four-lane divided
highway would have a significantly lower crash rate than any of the existing highway types in
the Route 11 corridor. Therefore, in the Build scenario, when a portion of the traffic is shifted to
this new highway type, the overall number of crashes is expected to decrease. Based on
generalized estimates of traffic volumes on each roadway type, it is estimated that the annual
number of crashes could be reduced by approximately 15 percent.
