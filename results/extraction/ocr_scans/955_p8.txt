An Executive Session of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday,
September 20, 2021 at 6:30 p.m., Civic Center, Park Street, Potsdam, New York.

PRESENT: Mayor Tischler
Trustees Warr, Jacobs-Wilke & Lee
Administrator Thompson
Police Chief Mark Murray

The Board of Trustees, upon a resolution offered by Trustee Lee and seconded by Trustee
Jacobs-Wilke went into Executive Session for the discussion of the employment history of
particular persons at 6:30 p.m.

Discussion of same was had; no formal vote or action was taken in Execution Session.

Upon a resolution offered by Trustee Lee and seconded by Trustee Jacobs-Wilke the Executive
Session was adjourned at 7:14 p.m.

The undersigned was not in attendance at the Executive Session. Information is provided by
Mayor Tischler.

Respectfully submitted,

Christine Travis
Deputy Village Clerk
