Downtown Streetscape Renovation and Riverwalk Trail Project
C1001883

RFP Respondent Review Sheet
Respondent: Barton and Loguidice (B&L)

Reviewer: Fred Hanss

Responsiveness to the Request for Proposal — 10 points maximum

Reviewer’s Comments & Score: I appreciated B & L’s approach to the project (8 guiding principles for
public spaces) and their approach to public engagement/kick-off meeting. They offered several notable
recommendations for design solutions for Raymond Street. B & L’s proposal was responsive.

Score: 10 Points

Understanding of the Scope of Work — 20 points maximum

Reviewer’s Comments & Score: I felt that B & L has a good understanding of the scope of work included
in the RFP. I appreciated the consideration they offered for potential design solutions for the Riverwalk
Trail on the west shoreline of the Raquette. B & L has a highly process-driven approach that might work
well in a large municipal project (infrastructure), but may be overkill for smaller projects such as this
one, especially given the extremely tight development budget.

Score: 17 Points.

Proposed Schedule and Budget — maximum 15 points

Reviewer’s Comments & Score: B & L’s project schedule is realistic. The firm’s proposed fee is
$652,900 or 14.4% of total project costs. It seems like B & L has many sub-contractors engaged in parts
of the project that may drive up their design fees. Given this, the scope and scale of the project would
need to be reduced significantly impacting the project’s benefit to the community.

Score: 8 Points
