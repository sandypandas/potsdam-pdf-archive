A Special Meeting of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Thursday, July 30,
2015 at 9:15 A.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, New York.

PRESENT: Mayor Steve Yurgartis
Trustees Warr, Hopke, & Garner

ALSO PRESENT: Everett Basford, Administrator
Lori Queor, Clerk/Treasurer
Fred Hanss, Planning Director

Mayor Yurgartis opened the meeting at 9:15 A.M. and took the roll stating that all members were
present with the exception of Trustee Tischler.

NEW BUSINESS:

Trustee Garner Moved and Trustee Warr Seconded a resolution to submit an application
through the 2015 Consolidated Funding Application seeking $507,500.00 in grant
funding through the New York State Small Cities Community Development Block Grant
Program and $145,000.00 in grant funding through the New York State Economic
Development Grant Program to be used for the proposed Cross Town Canal
Rehabilitation Project; and, further resolved that the Village of Potsdam hereby commits
$72,500 in cash from its General Fund as an equity investment for this project; and,
further resolved that the Mayor or in his absence, the Deputy Mayor be authorized to
execute the application document, any required certifications and any grant agreements
that may result from said application.

Ayes 4 (Mayor Yurgartis) Nays 0
, (Trustee Warr, Hopke & Garner

Trustee Garner Moved and Trustee Warr Seconded a resolution classifying the Cross
Town Canal Rehabilitation Project as a Type II action under SEQRA and further
authorizing the Mayor or in his absence the Deputy Mayor to execute a Certification of
SEQRA Classification.

Ayes 4 (Mayor Yurgartis) Nays 0
: (Trustee Warr, Hopke & Garner

Although there was a resolution on the agenda to hold an executive session for the discussion of
the employment of a particular person, the Board decided that they did not need the executive
session at this time.
