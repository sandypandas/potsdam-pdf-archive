PES -3 The Village Board of Trustees should develop and implement a Capital Improvement
Plan (CIP) for all Village funded facilities and services. The CIP should identify the capital need, the
estimated costs and timelines. The plan should be in place by budget time and look ahead 5 years; and,
identify sources of funding for improvements.

PFS-4 The Town and the Village of Potsdam should jointly fund facilities and services that
benefit both the Town and the Village (such as the Airport, Museum, Recreation, Rescue Squad and
Chamber of Commerce).

PFS -5 The Village Board of Trustees should continue to pursue state, federal, and private grant
opportunities. These funds should be used to pay for capital improvements as prioritized in the adopted
CIP.

PFS-6 The Village should install information kiosks and develop other resources to provide
way-finding and information on community activities,

PFS-7 The Village Administrator should implement an annual employee performance review
process.
PFS-8 The Village Board of Trustees should periodically reconvene the Comprehensive

Planning Committee to review progress of the plan and repeat the community survey to gauge how
public opinion is evolving.

Municipal Water Recommendations
PFS-9 The Department of Public Works should work to reduce open-ended water loops.

PFS- 10 The Department of Public Works should assess infrastructure improvements,
including fire hydrant volumes.

PFS-11 The Department of Public Works should upgrade the water plant pumping system.
Municipal Sewer Recommendations:

PFS — 12 The Village Board of Trustees should assess the need for the renovation of the solids
handling facilities and secure grants to implement recommendations.
