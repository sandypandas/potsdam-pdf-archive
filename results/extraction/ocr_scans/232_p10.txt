A Regular Meeting of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday, April 6,
2015 at 7:00 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, New York.

PRESENT: Mayor Yurgartis
Trustees Hopke, Tischler & Garner

ALSO PRESENT: _ Everett Basford, Village Administrator
Lori S. Queor, Clerk/Treasurer
Kevin Bates, Police Chief
Greg Thompson, Code Officer
Bob Henninger, Chief WTP/WWTP Operator
Fred Hanss, Planning Director
Mimi VanDeusen, Museum Director

Mayor Yurgartis opened the meeting at 7:00 P.M., and stated that all members were present with
the exception of Trustee Warr.

Mayor Yurgartis then stated that the following sets of minutes had been submitted for Board
approval:

March 16, 2015 Regular Meeting 7:00 p.m.
March 16, 2015 Executive Session 8:09 p.m.
March 23, 2015 Special Meeting 2:30 p.m.
March 26, 2015 Special Meeting 2:15 p.m.
March. 30, 2015 Special Meeting 2:30 p.m.

A Motion was made by Trustee Tischler and Seconded by Trustee Garner to approve the minutes
as submitted. All were in favor, none opposed, motion carried.

Mayor’s comments: The Mayor Proclaimed April as Occupation Therapy Month.

Trustees’ comments: Trustee Hopke stated that the Chamber of Commerce is organizing a
clean up day on April 18" from 10-12. Trustee Tischler and Garner had no comments.

Administrator’s Comments: Administrator Everett Basford stated that it has been a busy period
since the last board meeting. He stated that he and the Treasurer completed the Revenue
Anticipation Note for the $500,000 that we borrowed and that they continue to monitor the
budget and cash flows. He informed the board that the Jet-A project is complete at the airport
and we are just awaiting the seal certification from St. Lawrence County Weights and
Measurements which should be completed this week. Mr. Basford stated that there are several
grants that C & S and Passero are finishing up and closing out at the airport. He also stated that
the DPW continues to chase water breaks and actually had to come in on Saturday and plow.

Trustee Tischler asked about the water running, if that was still in effect. Administrator Basford
stated that the DPW is keeping an eye on the frost which is working it’s way out of the ground
and that Bruce prefers to have the water running continue until he is sure that it is safe for
residents to turn it off.
