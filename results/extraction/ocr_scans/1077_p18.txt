Downtown Streetscape Renovation and Riverwalk Trail Project
C1001883

RFP Respondent Review Sheet
Respondent: Fisher Associates

Reviewer: Fred Hanss

Responsiveness to the Request for Proposal — 10 points maximum

Reviewer’s Comments & Score: Fisher seemed to have a good understanding of the project. I was
confused about the firm’s previous experience working on Village projects — I have no recollection of
working with Fisher in the past. They cited their DOT design work for the recent crosswalk work
downtown. Fisher clearly relied on boiler plate language in their response which led to misstatements
(referring to the Village as the Town, Raquette River as the canal, etc.) as well as map labelling errors
and typos.

Score: 7 Points

Understanding of the Scope of Work — 20 points maximum

Reviewer’s Comments & Score: Fisher is an experienced transportation engineering firm that has
accomplished similar projects for municipalities. I questioned their understanding of the scope of work.
Fisher proposed undertaking a National Environmental Policy Act (NEPA) review of the project even
though there are no federal funds being used for the project — thereby duplicating the SEQRA
environmental review process. They also proposed to use NYSDOT design guidelines and contract
process that may have the effect of increasing project costs.

Score: 15 Points

Proposed Schedule and Budget — maximum 15 points

Reviewer’s Comments & Score: Fisher’s schedule is realistic. Their proposed fee is $558,000 or 12%
of total project costs.

Score 9 Points.
