RESOLUTION BY THE VILLAGE BOARD
VILLAGE OF POTSDAM
TO MAINTAIN HIGHWAY LIGHTING

RESOLUTION NO.

Resolution of the Village Board of the Village of Potsdam Agreeing to Maintain, Repair and Energize
a Highway Lighting System on or along a State Highway within the geographical jurisdiction of the Village, such
highway identified as NY Route 9710, Village of Potsdam, PIN 7780.08, County of St. Lawrence.

WHEREAS, the State Of New York Department Of Transportation Proposes To Reconstruct A State
Highway Identified As NY Route 971U, Village of Potsdam, PIN 7780.08, County of St. Lawrence, within the
geographical jurisdiction of the Village of Potsdam.

WHEREAS, the Village of Potsdam approves of such project and desires to have a highway lighting
system on such highway within its geographical jurisdiction, and

WHEREAS, the State of New York has agreed to provide as part of the project the following items in
connection with a street lighting system.

1. Underground duct system, including conduit, pull boxes, hand holes and drainage pockets.
2. Foundation for light standards.
3. Light poles.

4. Luminaries, wiring, switches, ballasts, light meter cabinets and all other components necessary
to complete the lighting system.

Provided that the Village of Potsdam agrees to maintain, repair and energize such highway lighting
system for a period of 20 years or until such time as the COMMISSIONER, in her discretion, determines that
such lighting and / or the maintenance of such lighting system is no longer necessary for such Highway.

NOW, THEREFORE, the Village Board, duly convened, does hereby

RESOLVE, the Village of Potsdam approves of the above subject project; and it is hereby further

RESOLVED, that the Village of Potsdam shall maintain, repair and energize such highway lighting
system, and it is hereby further

RESOLVED, that the Village Board hereby authorizes the Mayor of the Village of Potsdam to enter
into and execute any Agreement with the State of New York by an through the Commissioner of Transportation
to commit the Village of Potsdam to mainiain, at its own expense, the lighting system on the above identified
project, such agreement to provide that the maintenance shall include the repair and replacement of equipment
and the furnishing of electric current for the lighting system, an it is hereby further

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED: that the clerk of this Board is hereby directed to transmit four (4) certified
copies of the foregoing resolution to:

New York State Department of Transportation
317 Washington Street

Watertown, NY 13601
