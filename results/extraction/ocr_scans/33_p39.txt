Village of Potsdam, New York, 2012-2022 Comprehensive Plan

The Planning and Development Office should aggressively continue the rehabilitation of
substandard housing units occupied by low-to-moderate income people and families.

Promoting the Real Property Tax Law Section 421-f property tax exemption and other similar
exemptions to encourage residential property improvements.

The provision of assistance to parties wishing to purchase, rehabilitate and occupy two-family
homes.

Adopting “Smart Growth” development policies (an outline which is attached as Appendix 2).
Encourage the construction and renovation of housing in the Village by:

Developing and implementing a “Village of Potsdam Housing Incentives” program.

Make infrastructure improvements that will encourage new home construction.

Encourage private/public development of new moderately priced, single family residences in the
Village. Such housing is particularly in demand for first-time homebuyers as is rental properties

for new families arriving in Potsdam.

Encourage private/public partnerships to develop middle and upper income rental housing units
within the Village.

Encourage development of owner-occupied and rental housing suitable for active retirees.

The Planning and Development Office should work to encourage homeownership by partnering
with local banks and participating in related public sector-funded programs.

Review the Village’s zoning and sub-division regulations and revise them as necessary to
encourage residential development that serve a broad range of housing needs and affordability.

Encourage the development of employer assisted housing programs by the Village’s two

universities, the hospital and the Village’s other major employers.

H-5

The Village Code Enforcement Office should continue vigorous regulation of rental housing to

protect the public health, safety and welfare of Village residents.

H-6

The Village Code Enforcement Office should increase enforcement of the NYS Property

Maintenance Code so as to maintain property values for all residents and to enhance the desirability of
living in the Village of Potsdam.
