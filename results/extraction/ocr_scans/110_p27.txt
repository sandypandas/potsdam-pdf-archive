Dear Mr. Yurgartis: Re: Project Resolutions and Agreement
NY Route 971U over Raquette River, Potsdam
PIN 7780.08, St. Lawrence County

This project begins on NY Route 971U (Maple Street) at a point approximately 115’
west of the west structure over the Raquette River and proceeds easterly along Route
971U for a distance of approximately 1000’, terminating on the easterly side of the east
structure crossing the Raquette River at the intersection of Route 971U (Maple Street),
NY Route 56 (Market Street) and Main Street in the Village of Potsdam.

The tentative letting date for the project is February 5, 2015.

Construction on the project will consist of rehabilitating the existing three-span and five-
span concrete arch bridges to improve the overall condition and repiace the sidewalk
facilities. The rehabilitation will provide a reconstructed roadway over the bridges and
repairs to various superstructure and substructure components. Between the bridges,
the pavement will be milled, and the top course will be replaced. Reconstruction of
deteriorated sidewalk and curb will be replaced along the island section between the
bridges as needed. The approach roadway will be reconstructed for approximately 50
feet to the west and 50 feet to the east of each bridge to tie into the existing roadway.

New concrete sidewalk, curbing and striping as well as other miscellaneous
construction will also take place throughout the project.

Because of the work scheduled to be done on this project, which will include replacing
street lighting, concrete sidewalk and curbing, the following enclosed resolutions and
agreement should be acted on by the Village Board at this time,

1. A Sidewalk Maintenance Resolution pertaining to maintenance of sidewalks which
will be reconstructed as a part of the construction of this project.

2. A Highway Lighting Agreement pertaining to energizing and maintenance of street
lights.
