effective for the remainder of this agreement and all extensions

3. The County will annually solicit bids for sand mixed with

Municipality will purchase the salt and sand for use on State
roads from the vendor awarded the bid by the County.

4, In lieu of making three interim payments of 25% each of
the estimated expenditure, as specified in Section 7 of the
aforementioned agreement, the County will make one advance payment
in October. The cost will be calculated at the prices resulting
from bids let by the County.

5. The Municipality will submit Monthly Claim Vouchers for
the actual expenditures for labor, equipment and materials on or
after the 15th day of the month following the incurrence of such
expenditures. The amount of the advance payment will be used as
payment of the incurred expenses. When such amount has been used,
the County will reimburse the Municipality for further expenses.
