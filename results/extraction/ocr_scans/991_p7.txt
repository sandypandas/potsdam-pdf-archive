A Regular Meeting of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday, January
31, 2022, at 6:00 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, New York.

PRESENT: Mayor Tischler
Trustees Warr, Tirion, Jacobs-Wilke & Lee

ALSO PRESENT: Greg Thompson, Administrator
Mayor Tischler opened the meeting at 6:51 P.M., pointed out the exits on the North and East
Walls and advised that the board room has the capacity for 109 people.
The Mayor then stated that all members were present.

The Mayor then stated that the following sets of minutes had been submitted for Board
acceptance:

January 3, 2022 Regular Meeting 6:00 p.m.
January 3, 2022 Executive Session 6:22 p.m.

Motion was made by Trustee Lee and Seconded by Trustee Jacobs-Wilke to accept the minutes
as submitted. All Were in Favor — None Opposed — Motion Carried.

Mayor’s comments: Covid still with us so please social distance, wear a mask and get
vaccinated.

Trustee’s Comments:

Trustee Warr — None

Trustee Tirion — None

Trustee Jacobs- Wilke — Last call for Citizen Advisory Committee Members. If you are a
resident or property owner in the Village of Potsdam you can be on this committee. Nomination
forms are on the village’s website. SUNY Potsdam is doing covid testing which is free and done
with an oral swab.

Trustee Lee - Welcome back SUNY Potsdam students. Thank you to the DPW for their work
on the water break near the school. Winter season, please check parking regulations and help

your neighbors with snow if they need it.

Administrator’s Comments: Administrator Thompson provided the Board with a written copy of
his report which he went over with the board.

Public Comment: None
