A Regular Meeting of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday, July 6,
2015 at 3:00 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, New York.

PRESENT: Mayor Yurgartis
Trustees Warr & Hopke

ALSO PRESENT: _ Everett Basford, Village Administrator
Greg Thompson, Code Officer
Kevin Bates, Police Chief
Fred Hanss, Planning Director
Bruce Henderson, DPW Superintendent

Mayor Yurgartis opened the meeting at 3:00 P.M., and stated that all members were present with
the exception of Trustee Garner & Tischler

Mayor Yurgartis then stated that the following sets of minutes had been submitted for Board
approval:

June 15, 2015 Regular Meeting 7:00 p.m.
June 15, 2015 Executive Session 8:02 p.m.

A Motion was made by Trustee Warr and Seconded by Trustee Hopke to approve the minutes as
submitted. All were in favor, none opposed, motion carried.

Mayor’s comments: None

Trustees’ comments: Trustee Warr had no comments. Trustee Hopke reminded everyone that
Summer Festival is this coming weekend.

Administrator’s Comments: Administrator Everett Basford reported to the board on the
following: .

- Mid summer — vacations, lots of people off so it cuts into productivity.

- Met with Village Auditor, Tom Peet, Lori is finishing up the adjustments for the end of
the year last year. Some last minute items need to be worked out. Difficulties will need
to be worked through.

- Work on the digester cleaning needs to be addressed.

- Chemical bulk storage tank needs to be addressed.

- Report from Barton & Loguidice on the water tower. Everyone got a copy of the report.

- Repayment of the Revenue Anticipation Note will be made on July 15, 2015.

Public Comment: None
