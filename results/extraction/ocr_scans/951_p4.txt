A Public Hearing of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Tuesday, September
7, 2021 at 5:30 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, NY.

PRESENT: Mayor Tischler
Trustees Warr, McKenna, Jacobs-Wilke & Lee

ALSO PRESENT: Lori Queor, Village Clerk/Registrar
Cara Adams, Village Treasurer
Greg Thompson, Administrator
Fred Hanss, Planning Director
Jim Corbett, DPW Superintendent
Mark Murray, Police Chief
Trey Smutz, Recreation Director

Mayor Tischler opened the hearing at 5:37 p.m. He stated that this public hearing is to receive
public comments on a proposed local law to amend Chapter 180 Zoning to rezone the current H-
2 properties to H-1 and the R-2 properties on Waverly Street per the map to H-1.

He took the roll stating that all members were present.
Village Clerk Queor then read the Legal Notice as published.

Public Comments:

Brent Bishop from St. Lawrence Heath Systems gave the board a brief presentation on the
proposed expansion at Canton-Potsdam Hospital.

Monique Tirion of 8 Circle Drive spoke. She stated that the village is 4.39 square miles, fiscal
appropriations for 2018 were 10.5 million dollars, 69% of the village assessed property is tax
exempt, population is decreasing, and she pays 5.13% tax rate which is insanely high. She stated
that she was concerned about the tax revenue that would be lost and hoped the board will execute
its due diligence.

Tom Baker of 48 Wavery Street spoke. He stated that he supports the hospital expansion and
modernization but wants to see the character of Waverly Street maintained. He asked how many
parking spaces are needed for employees and suggesting shuttling them would solve some
problems. He also stated that the design including a park would help mitigate the feel of the
nature of the hospital.

Doyle Dean of 14 Broad Street spoke. He stated that he agreed the hospital needs modernization
but to remember that this is an ask of the Village Board and the hospital is a business. He stated
that the board should ask what is in it for us. He also stated that there has been a broken ashtray
in the area of the hospital that has been there for 9-10 months and he has taken photos of it. He
stated that we should judge their neighborliness by our experience with them.
