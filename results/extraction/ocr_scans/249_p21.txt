§180-54 Scope.

This local law enacts a moratorium on the development of photo-voltaic electricity generating
facilities except for those installed at one (1) and two (2) family dwellings within the Village
of Potsdam.

The Village of Potsdam enacts a moratorium on the development of commercial scaled
photo-voltaic generating facilities within the Village boundaries for a period of six (6)
months from the date of the Village Board of Trustees adoption of the local law and its filing
with the New York State Secretary of State.

Photo-voltaic electricity generating facilities installed at one (1) and two (2) family dwellings
as an accessory use are exempt from the moratorium subject to §180 Zoning of the Village

Code and the New York State Fire Prevention and Building Code.

Section 3. Enactment.

This Local Law shall become effective immediately upon the notification of the filing of
same with the Secretary of State.
