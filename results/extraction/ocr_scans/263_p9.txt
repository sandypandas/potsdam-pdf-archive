Trustee Warr Moved and Trustee Hopke Seconded a Resolution as follows:

To authorize submittal of a NYS Water Infrastructure Improvement Act
Clean Water Grant Application

Whereas, the Village of Potsdam completed a Preliminary Engineering Report to
determine improvements needed at the Village’s Water Pollution Control Facility and
Wastewater Collection System (Barton & Loguidice, August 2015), and

Whereas, the Village of Potsdam is acting as the lead municipality for purposes of the
State Environmental Quality Review (SEQR) and project funding, and

Whereas, the Village is pursuing federal and state funding to maximize the affordability
of the project for system users, and

Whereas, the New York State Infrastructure Improvement Act of 2015 provides the New
York State Environmental Facilities Corporation (EFC) authority to provide grants to
assist municipalities in funding clean water projects that protect the environment and
public health.

Now, therefore be it resolved that the Village of Potsdam, at their meeting held August
17, 2015, does hereby authorize the submittal of an application to the NYS Water
Infrastructure Improvement Act Clean Water Grant program, due September 4, 2015, and
does further authorize the Mayor to execute any and all documents associated with the
application.
