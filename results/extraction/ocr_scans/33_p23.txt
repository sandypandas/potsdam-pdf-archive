Village of Potsdam, New York, 2012-2022 Comprehensive Plan

Public Facilities and Services

Over the coming decade, the Village of Potsdam will continue to provide essential services that protect
the public’s health, safety and welfare while supporting the Village’s economic development efforts. The
Village will provide safe drinking water, an environmentally responsible waste and storm water sewer
collection and treatment system, a high quality network of roadways, bicycle and pedestrian facilities,
and responsive police, fire and rescue services.

With respect to its infrastructure and services, the Village faces many challenges related to the increasing
demand for services, a regulatory environment that mandates costly compliance and the long-term
problem of providing municipal services in a community where the majority of the taxable value is
exempt from the payment of property taxes. Against this backdrop, there are reasons for optimism:
Potsdam continues to be a community that attracts new commercial development and has the potential to
attract new residential development as well. Much of this potential is related to the care that the Village
Board of Trustees and Village departments have planned for future capital needs, carefully allocated
resources and efficiently used the resources at their disposal. The Village should at all costs avoid
returning to the days when deferred maintenance was viewed as a cost containment strategy and
recognize that delaying maintenance and upkeep only shift costs from the near to Jong-term, often at
greater cost.

After 15 years of facility development and upgrading, Potsdam Damon Filed Airport is beginning to
become a facility that attracts general aviation and commercial air traffic. With the leasing of an area of
the SRE Building and the commercial hanger to Air Methods, a national air ambulance service and T-
hangar bays to commercial based-aircrafi, the airport is a new center for economic growth and
development.
