3. Travel Time and Traffic Operations

Travel time along the entire Route 11 corridor from Watertown to Champlain is approximately
3 hours with some seasonal variation due to inclement weather conditions such as heavy snow
accumulation typical to the area and increased travel demand during peak tourist seasons. In the
2008 study’, travel time is calculated as both free flow travel time based on free flow speed and
actual travel time’ based on GPS data from NYSDOT. Along Route 11, free flow speed is
assumed to be 60 miles per hour (mph) along rural segments and 40 mph along urbanized
segments. Table 4 shows free flow travel time along Route 11 segments from Watertown to
