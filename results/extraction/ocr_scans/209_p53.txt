VII. COST ESTIMATE

A conceptual cost estimate (see Appendix C) has been developed for the four-lane interstate
freeway mainline alternative based upon the proposed design criteria and typical section. On the
summary sheet, four different corridor components are estimated separately. The four corridor
components include a 1-mile-long regular roadway section, a diamond-shaped interchange,
structures for grade separation without interchange, and structures over water. -

The costs of the 1-mile-long regular roadway section and the diamond interchange are broken
down to details in following pages. Asphalt concrete pavement was used in the estimate. The
unit prices from I-781 construction bid results were adopted for similar items in this estimate.
I-781 is a newly constructed similar type of roadway in the same region, which provides highly
comparable unit costs. Other unit prices were adopted from NYSDOT Pay Item Catalog
historical data.

Some additional assumptions on the diamond-shaped interchange cost estimate are:

1. All mainline interchanges are configured as diamond-shaped interchanges with
connecting ramps in all four quadrants.

2. An interchange is about 1 mile long and 600 feet wide.

3. Mainline is carried by dual structures over the crossing road. A structure is about 102 feet
long and 41 feet wide ,

4. This interchange cost estimate includes I mile of mainline, four ramps, and the dual
structures.

5. Structures carrying mainline through the interchange are estimated by using the
Preliminary Cost Estimate Worksheet (New and Replacement Bridges) from NYSDOT.
A bridge is generally assumed to be 102 feet long and 41 feet wide.

6. Ramps and auxiliary lanes are 2,500 feet long and 15 feet wide.
7. Cross road under the new structures will need milling and resurfacing

At grade separation locations without interchanges, there are two types of structures, either
carrying mainline over local roads or vise versa. Each of these two types is assumed to have
50 percent of the total number of grade separations. Structures spanning mainline over local
roads are assumed to have the same costs as structures within an interchange. Structures carrying
local roads over mainline are assumed to be two-span, approximately 180 feet long and 40 feet
wide,

Structures carrying mainline over water are assumed to have same costs as structures within an
interchange as well. .
