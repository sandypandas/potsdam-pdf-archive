Village of Potsdam Parking Restrictions
Effective 11/01/18

Village of Potsdam residents are reminded that per 8168-22 of the Village
Code, parking on Village streets is prohibited between the hours of 2 AM to 6
AM. Vehicles parked on street between these hours will be ticketed and
towed at the owners expense. Also attached is schedule XXIX which
identifies parking restrictions in village owned lots to facilitate snow
removal. Vehicles in violation of these rules will also be ticketed and towed.

Thank you for your cooperation. Please contact the Dept. of Public Works at
315-265-4620 with any questions.
