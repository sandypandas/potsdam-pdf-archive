Village of Potsdam, New York, 2012-2022 Comprehensive Plan

I. Introduction

In 2001, the Village Board of Trustees adopted the 2001-2011 Comprehensive Plan as its official 10 year
strategic policy and planning guide. The plan was revisited in 2006 for a mid-term review when revisions
were made to acknowledge the completion of a number of priority projects and initiatives and to
recognize new challenges and opportunities. The purpose of the 2001-2011 Comprehensive Plan was to:

1. Serve as an expression of what the community wants, namely a statement of goals and objectives
for the future of the community.

2. Guide the Village Board of Trustees and other Village government agencies in future decision-
making

3. Act as a policy document to support the Village Board of Trustee’s decisions and legislative
actions. The policies were recognized not to be laws, but rather to provide a rational and well
considered plan to guide future decisions of the Board and to prioritize the activities of Village
government agencies.

In 2011, the Village Board of Trustees convened a committee comprised of Village and Town residents,
representatives of stakeholder organizations including Canton-Potsdam Hospital, SUNY Potsdam and
Clarkson University and the Potsdam Chamber of Commerce to develop a new plan for the Village of
Potsdam that would serve as a guide for policy and planning between 2012 and 2022. The
