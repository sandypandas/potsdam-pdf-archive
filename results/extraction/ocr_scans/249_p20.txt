Section 1. Purpose.

The Purpose of this Local Law is to Amend Chapter 180 Zoning to Add Article XII
Moratoria of the Village of Potsdam Municipal Code.

Section 2. Amendment.
ADD:

Article XI. Moratoria

§180-52 Title.
Moratorium on the Development of Commercial Scale Photo-Voltaic Electricity Generating
Facilities.

§180-53 Purpose.

To enact a six (6) month moratorium on the development of commercial scale photo-voltaic
electricity generating facilities pending completion of the Village Planning Board’s review
and revision of §180, Zoning of the Village Code, a process presently underway; and, the
Village Board of Trustee’s action on the revised code.
