An Executive Session of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday, April 6,
2015 at 7:21 P.M., Civic Center, Park Street, Potsdam, New York.

PRESENT: Mayor Yurgartis
Trustees Hopke, Tischler & Garner
Village Administrator Everett Basford

The Board of Trustees, upon a resolution offered by Trustee Tischler and seconded by Trustee
Garner went into Executive Session for the discussion of contracts at 7:21 p.m.

Discussion of same was had; no formal vote or action was taken in Execution Session.

Upon a resolution offered by Trustee Tischler and seconded by Trustee Garner the Executive
Session was adjourned at 7:45 P.M.

The undersigned was not in attendance at this Executive Session. Information was provided by
Village Administrator Everett Basford.
