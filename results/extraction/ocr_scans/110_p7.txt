AGENDA - REGULAR MEETING 7
VILLAGE OF POTSDAM BOARD OF TRUSTEES
MONDAY, NOVEMBER 17, 2014 - 7:00 P.M.

WHEREAS, the Village Board having determined (a) the Petitioners are the sole owners
of all of the real property described in the Petition according to the last preceding
assessment role of the Town of Potsdam and the assessor’s certification (b) the
Petitioners are duly qualified to sign the Petition, (c) the Petition substantially complies in
form and content with Article 17 of the General Municipal Law; and (d) the proposed
annexation is in the overall public interest;

NOW, THEREFORE, upon the evidence given and upon all the prior proceedings and
public hearing it is

RESOLVED AND DETERMINED, that the relief requested in the Petition is hereby
granted in all respects and the real property, as described in the Petition, shall be hereby
annexed from the Town of Potsdam into the Village of Potsdam; the relevant portion of
the Official Map of the Town of Potsdam shall be so amended at no expense to the Town
of Potsdam; the Map shall be filed in the Office of the Clerk of the Village of Potsdam
and the Office of the Clerk of St. Lawrence County; and, it is further

RESOLVED AND DETERMINED that such annexation shall be as proposed by the
Petition subject to the provisions, terms, and conditions of this Resolution and the Town
of Potsdam Resolution; and it is further

RESOLVED AND DETERMINED, that at no expense to the Village of Potsdam, the
Village Clerk shall comply with the provisions of §711 of the General Municipal law
with respect to this Resolution and Order.
