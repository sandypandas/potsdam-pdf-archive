A Joint Public Hearing of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees and the Town of Potsdam
Board was held on Tuesday, January 12, 2016 at 6:15 P.M., in the Town Offices, Board Room,
Elm Street, Potsdam, New York.

PRESENT: Mayor Tischler
Trustees Warr, Hopke & Sheehan

ALSO PRESENT: Everett Basford, Village Administrator
Lori Queor, Village Clerk/Treasurer

TOWN OF POTSDAM: Supervisor Beattie
Councilwoman Revezzi
Councilwoman Rich
Councilwoman Carvil
Councilman Grant
Cindy Goliber, Town Clerk
Frank Capello, Attorney

Mayor Tischler opened the hearing at 6:15 P.M. He stated that this public hearing is to receive
any objections which may be presented against a petition to annex territory into the Village of
Potsdam consisting of land owned by Clarkson University.

The Mayor then stated that the Legal Notice was published in the Courier-Observer on December
19, 2015.

The Mayor then asked for public comments.

Scott Goldie, Attorney for Clarkson University, showed the boards drawings explaining that this
piece of property was not part of the annexation they did a few months ago as the property was in
litigation. He stated that Clarkson now owns the property at 152 Maple Street and the property is
assessed at $60,000.

Councilwoman Revezzi asked what Clarkson’s vision for the property was. Ian Hazen from
Clarkson stated that the facilities complex will be on the properties they own and that in the area
of 152 Maple Street it will mainly be landscaping. Councilwoman Rich asked what facilities.
Mr. Hazen stated it would be custodial, maintenance, grounds crew, etc. George Giordano of
Clarkson stated that they wanted to enhance the view of the trail and the river and get rid of the
buildings but that is two to three years off. Councilwoman Revezzi asked about the Agbotics
Greenhouse. Mr. Giordano said there are plans for that in the Spring. Ms. Revezzi stated that
she has no concerns with the annexation, she loves that the property is now in the hands of
someone that will beautify the entrance to the village, but she is concerned about the loss of taxes
from that property for the school, village and town. She stated that she would like to be sure
Clarkson works with the assessor so that if the greenhouse is a for profit venture that they pay
taxes on it. Mr. Giordano stated that he is in constant communication with the assessor and they
will be very transparent that if the Agbotics becomes a business they will tell the assessor and
pay their fair share of taxes for the business.
