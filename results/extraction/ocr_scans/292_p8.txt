A Public Hearing of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday, January 4,
2016 at 6:45 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, New York.

PRESENT: Mayor Tischler
Trustees Warr & Hopke

ALSO PRESENT: Lori S. Queor, Clerk/Treasurer

Everett Basford, Village Administrator

Fred Hanss, Planning Director

Greg Thompson, Code Enforcement Officer
Mayor Tischler opened the hearing at 6:45 P.M. He stated that this public hearing is to receive
public comments on a proposed local law to create Chapter 19, Ethics Code of the Village of
Potsdam Municipal Code.
Village Clerk, Lori Queor, then read the Legal Notice as published.

The Mayor then asked for public comments.

Hearing no public comments, the Mayor declared the hearing closed at 6:49 P.M.

Respectfully submitted,

Lori S. Queor
Village Clerk
