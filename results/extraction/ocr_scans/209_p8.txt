Rural principal arterials in the North Country study area, including Route 11, State Route (SR)
56, and SR 812, carry 7,000 to 20,000 AADT. The greatest traffic volumes along these
roadways occur in the vicinity of the village and city centers of Watertown, Canton, Potsdam,
and Malone.
