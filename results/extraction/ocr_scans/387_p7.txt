Trustee Moved and Trustee Seconded a resolution as follows:

Local Law #6-2016

Section 1, Purpose.
The Purpose of this Local Law is to Amend Chapter 18, Elections to add Section 18-2

Extension of terms of office of the Village of Potsdam Municipal Code.

Section 2. Amendment.
ADD:

§ 18-2 Extension of terms of office.

On April 20, 1987, the Village Board of Trustees passed a resolution to extend the terms
of office of all Village Trustees to four years, effective at the next Village Election in
accordance with § 3-302, Subdivision 5a of the Village Law, subject to permissive
referendum in accordance with Article 9 of the Village Law. This resolution was to take
effect 30 days after official posting of a notice of the passage of said resolution unless 200
of the qualified voters signed a petition objecting to this action within 30 days after the
posting. No petition was filed within the time specified.

Section 3. Enactment.
This Local Law shall become effective immediately upon the notification of the filing of
same with the Secretary of State.
