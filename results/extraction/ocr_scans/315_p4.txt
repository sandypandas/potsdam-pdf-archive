2) Moratorium

Fred Hanss brought it to the Boards attention that a Public Hearing has
been planned to discuss the proposed moratorium on photovoltaic
development within the Village. A 239-M review, as well as a SEQRA, have
been completed on the moratorium as well. Mr. Hanss further went on to
explain that the County Planning Board also suggested that we should
include 2 family homes within our allowances of PV.

Hugo Atteman inquired as to how other local municipalities dealing with PV
development. It was explained by Fred Hanss that because the concept is
so new that there really is not a lot of information to use.

David Trithart questioned the existence of any tax implications. Mr. Hanss
sourced Peggy Russo’s article and county Real Property information which
states that you can get 15 years of tax exemption as long as the projected
has NYSERDA assistance. This is an opt out program, which further means
that everyone is included in the program, unless you specifically opt to not
take part. Mr. Trithart asked if the entire property would at that time be
removed from the tax roles. Mr. Hanss answered that it would.

Bob Josephson posed the question that as good as solar energy seems to
be, could there be hidden concerns or hazards. Fred Hanss replied that the
County Planning Board has already suggested that any contract put in place
with PV Developers should include a “no walk away” clause. This would
prevent anyone from walking away from the structures should they stop
producing power.

No Further Discussion
