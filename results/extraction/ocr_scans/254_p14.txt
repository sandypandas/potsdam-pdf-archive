Public Comment: John Lancaster of 32 Pierrepont Avenue addressed the board regarding the
toilet garden installed at 28 Pierrepont Avenue. He asked the board what they intended to do
about the toilets and when they intend to do it. Mayor Yurgartis stated that Mr. Lancaster had a
fair question, that it is an urkesome situation and that it is expensive to litigate. Mr. Lancaster
informed the board that he actually took measurements and the set backs are not far enough for
the sidewalk or the rental property that is between his house and the toilet garden property.
Mayor Yurgartis suggested Mr. Lancaster speak to the Code Enforcement Officer regarding the
set backs. Mr. Lancaster also suggested that if this property is artwork it should be assessed as
such and perhaps the assessor should reduce the neighboring properties assessments. Mayor
Yurgartis stated that we will continue to look at and talk about this issue.
