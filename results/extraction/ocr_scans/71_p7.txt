A Regular Meeting of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday,
September 15, 2014 at 7:00 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, New
York.

PRESENT: Mayor Yurgartis
Trustees Warr & Hopke

ALSO PRESENT: — David Fenton, Village Administrator
Greg Thompson, Code Enforcement Officer
Bob Henninger, WTP/WWTP Chief Operator
Kevin Bates, Police Chief
Bruce Henderson, DPW Superintendent

Mayor Yurgartis opened the meeting at 7:00 P.M., and stated that all members were present
except for Trustees Garner & Tischler.

Mayor Yurgartis then stated that the following sets of minutes had been submitted for Board
approval:

September 2,2014 Regular Meeting 7:00 p.m.
A Motion was made by Trustee Warr and Seconded by Trustee Hopke to approve the minutes as
submitted. All were in favor, none opposed, motion carried.
Mayor’s comments: None

Trustees’ comments: Trustee Warr had no comments. Trustee Hopke stated that the new
website is nice.

Administrator's Comments: Administrator David Fenton reported on the FAA Grant for the
Airport.

Water/Wastewater Chief Operator’s Report: Chief Operator Bob Henninger provided the board
with a written report which is on file with the Village Clerk. He then went over his report with
the board.

DPW Superintendent’s Report: Superintendent Bruce Henderson reported on: Ives Park
improvements, new truck, street paving, salt bids, water break on Lower Pine Street, sewer break
on Maynard Street and painting at the Civic Center and the Ives Park Gazebo which will be done
next year.

Public Comment: None
