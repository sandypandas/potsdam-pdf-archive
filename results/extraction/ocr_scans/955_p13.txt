Of Potsdam as follows:

Section 1. Purpose.
The Purpose of this Local Law is to Amend Chapter 180, Zoning, Attachment 15, H-1 Zoning District of the
Village of Potsdam Municipal Code.

Section 2. Amendment.

Chapter 180, Zoning, Attachment 15 shall be amended to make one and two family dwellings a Standard Use
in the H-1 Health Care Zone -1.

Section 3. Enactment.

This Local Law shall become effective immediately upon the notification of the filing of same with the Secretary
of State.
