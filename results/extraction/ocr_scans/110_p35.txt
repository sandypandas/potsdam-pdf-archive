AGREEMENT FOR MAINTENANCE, REPAIR AND
ENERGIZING OF HIGHWAY LIGHTING FOR A
STATE HIGHWAY IDENTIFIED AS
NY ROUTE 977U, VILLAGE OF POTSDAM,
PIN 7780.08, ST. LAWRENCE COUNTY

This Agreement made this day of , 20 by and between
the People of the State of New York (hereinafter referred to as “STATE”) acting by and through
the Commissioner of Transportation (hereinafter referred to as “COMMISSIONER”) whose
principal office is at 50 Wolf Road, in the City and County of Albany, State of New York, and the
Village of Potsdam (hereinafter referred to as “VILLAGE”) acting by and through the Mayor
whose principal office is at 2. Park Street - Civic Center, Potsdam, NY 13676.

WITNESSETH:

WHEREAS, the COMMISSIONER proposes to reconstruct a State Highway pursuant to
the New York State Highway Law, such highway being identified as NY Route 971U, Village of
Poisdam, PIN 7780.08, County of St. Lawrence within the geographical jurisdiction of the
Village, and

WHEREAS, it is recognized by the VILLAGE and the COMMISSIONER that the STATE
does not have the funds available to maintain, repair and energize a lighting system for such
highway, and

WHEREAS, the VILLAGE desires to have lighting on such highway within the
geographical jurisdiction of the VILLAGE, and

WHEREAS, it is recognized by the VILLAGE and the COMMISSIONER that if the
VILLAGE desires to have lighting on or along the State Highway within the geographical
jurisdiction of the VILLAGE, the VILLAGE will have to maintain, repair and energize such lighting
at its own expense, and

WHEREAS, the VILLAGE, by Resolution number adopted at a meeting
held on ,20__ approved the above identified project and the terms and
provisions of the Agreement and has further authorized the Mayor of the VILLAGE to execute
this Agreement on behalf of the VILLAGE.

{copy of such Resolution is attached and made a part of this Agreement), and

WHEREAS, the VILLAGE and the COMMISSIONER are desirous of identifying the
respective responsibilities of the parties with regard to the highway lighting system.
