Trustee Moved and Trustee Seconded a resolution as follows:
Whereas, the Village of Potsdam has secured a grant in the amount of $30,000 from the
New York State Environmental Facilities Corporation, Planning Grant # 38948 to be
used for the express purposes of conducting a wastewater treatment planning study;

Now Therefore, Be It Resolved That: the Mayor of the Village of Potsdam is authorized to
execute a Grant Agreement with the New York State Environmental Facilities
Corporation and any and all other contracts, documents and instruments necessary to
bring about the Project and to fulfill the Village of Potsdam’s obligations under the
Engineering Planning Grant Agreement.

Trustee ___ Moved and Trustee Seconded a resolution as follows:

Whereas, 6NYCRRSection 615 (Title 6 of the New York Code of Rules and Regulations)
under the State Environmental Quality Review Act (SEQR) provides certain action
identified in subdivision (c) of that section that are not subject to environmental review
under the Environmental Conservation Law;

Now Therefore, Be It Resolved That: the Village of Potsdam hereby determines that the
proposed Waste Water Treatment Planning Study-Planning Grant #38948 is a Type II
action in accordance with 6NYCRR Section 617.5 (c) (21) which constitutes:
“conducting concurrent environmental, engineering, economic, feasibility and other
studies and preliminary planning and budgetary processes necessary to the formulation of
a proposal for action, provided those activities do not commit the agency to commence,
engage in or approve such action;” and is therefore not subject to review under 6NYCRR

Part 617,
