Use Variance Application #2015-3-30.1 submitted by John Swedick regarding 80 Elm Street

and to this point he has net done so.

Wade Davis read all 4 of the required legal tests for a Use Variance and at that time inquired as to
whether or not Mr. Swedick desired to proceed further with this matter.

Fred Hanss further clarified that Mr. Swedick must show an inability to gain a return on investment for
all acceptable used within the R-1 zone.
