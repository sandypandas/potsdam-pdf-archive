Truck activity in the project area can be categorized into two distinct groups, trucks with an
origin and or destination within the project area and trucks that are passing through. Both groups
of trucks are present in the study area, but the majority of trucks are expected to have an origin or
destination within the region.

Freight generators in the area include the stores and retail establishments in villages such as
Canton, Potsdam and Malone, the 94-bed Canton-Potsdam hospital, St. Lawrence University,
Clarkson University, and the State University of New York. Corning Specialty Materials, a
global producer of advanced optics and material solutions with a plant west of Canton, as well as
agricultural and timber vendors, producers, and processors, such as Poulin Grain in Canton and
Agri Mark dairy processing east of Malone, also generate freight. Other freight generators
located to the north of RT-11 include Maxam, an industrial manufacturer based in Ogdensburg,
the Port of Ogdensburg, the Massena rail terminal and Alcoa aluminum production operations
also in Massena,

The nearby border. crossings are also likely to influence the routing and volume of truck traffic
using RT-11. In 2013, the 12 border crossings located between the Thousand Island Bridge
Crossing and the NY/VT state line accounted for 9.55% of all trucks crossing into the U.S. from
Canada. The vast majority of trucks use the border crossings located on the key interstates I-81
and I-87. These two crossings alone account for 91 percent and 97 percent respectively of the
import and export goods value that is transported through the 12 border crossings by truck.
