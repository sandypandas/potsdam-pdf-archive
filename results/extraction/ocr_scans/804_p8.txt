A Regular Meeting of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday, April 6,
2020 at 6:00 P.M., via Zoom Meeting due to the Coronavirus Pandemic.

PRESENT: Mayor Tischler
Trustees McKenna, Jacobs-Wilke & Lee

ALSO PRESENT: Lori Queor, Clerk/Treasurer
Fred Hanss, Planning Director
Mark Murray, Police Chief
Greg Thompson, Administrator

Mayor Tischler opened the meeting at 6:00 P.M.
The Mayor then stated that all members were present except Trustee Warr.

The Mayor then stated that the following sets of minutes had been submitted for Board
acceptance:

March 16, 2020 Regular Meeting 6:00 p.m.
March 16, 2020 Executive Session 6:58 p.m.

Motion was made by Trustee Lee and Seconded by Trustee Jacobs-Wilke to accept the minutes
as submitted. All were in Favor — None Opposed — Motion Carried.

Mayor’s comments: Corona Virus — we have been following County, State and Federal
guidelines. Please stay home unless essential, go out by yourself, keep social distancing and stay
safe.

Trustee’s Comments:

Trustee McKenna — Thank you for your patience with getting this zoom meeting going. This
meeting is being streamed live to the Village’s Facebook page. Echo what Mayor said, stay safe,
and keep social distancing.

Trustee Jacobs-Wilke — Agree with Mayor and Maggie. Do not get complacent, stay home and
reach out to your neighbors and elderly digitally to offer any help you can give.

Trustee Lee — Echo Mayor and other trustees comments. Thank you to the EMT’s, fire, police,
rescue for all they do.

Administrator’s Comments:

- Corona Virus — mandates, dealing with things as they come up. Mayor issued a state of
emergency.

- Word today from Passero that there may be extra funding for airports which would cover
our portion of the taxiway widening project. More info will be forthcoming.

- East Dam—NYS essential project so it is off hold now. Gear boxes are now in
Pennsylvania to be re-built. Generators are removed and going to Maine to make sure the
are 100% before going back in.
