A Regular Meeting of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday,
September 20, 2021, at 6:00 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street Potsdam, NY.

PRESENT: Mayor Tischler
Trustees Warr, Jacobs-Wilke & Lee

ALSO PRESENT: Christine Travis, Deputy Village Clerk/Registrar
Cara Adams, Treasurer
Greg Thompson, Administrator
Jim Corbett, DPW Superintendent
Fred Hanss, Planning Director

Trey Smutz, Recreation Director
Mark Murray, Police Chief

Mayor Tischler opened the meeting at 6:00 P.M and pointed out the location of the exits on the
north and east walls of the board room. He advised that the board room has a capacity for 109
people.

The Mayor then took the roll and stated that all members were present except for Trustee
McKenna.

The Mayor then stated that the following sets of minutes had been submitted for Board
acceptance:

September 7, 2021 Public Hearing 5:30 p.m.
September 7, 2021 Regular Meeting 6:00 p.m.
September 7, 2021 Executive Session 7:41 p.m.

Motion was made by Trustee Lee and Seconded by Trustee Jacobs-Wilke to accept the minutes
as submitted. All were in Favor — None Opposed — Motion Carried.

Mayor’s comments: Covid is still among us, get vaccinated , avoid large groups, wear masks
even indoors.

Trustee’s Comments:
Trustee Warr — None
Trustee McKenna — Not Present

Trustee Jacobs-Wilke —Thanked volunteers working with Fred trying to get funding for Farmer’s
Market pavilion.

Trustee Lee —Greg and myself got in touch with digital towpath regarding website issues.

Administrator’s Comments: Administrator Thompson provided a written report which is on file
with the Village Clerk. He then went over his report with the board.
