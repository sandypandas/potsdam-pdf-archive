Village of Potsdam, New York, 2012-2022 Comprehensive Plan

IL. Demographics
A map of the Village of Potsdam is attached hereto as Appendix |.

The following demographic information is largely derived from the 1990, 2000, 2010 U.S. Census of
Population and the 2010 American Community Survey a statistical sampling tool employed by the U.S.
Bureau of the Census as an alternative to “long-form” data collection. Where possible to find comparable
data, information from 2000 and 2010 is presented. Student enrollment data was secured from Clarkson
University and SUNY Potsdam. Certain data regarding the estimates for low/moderate income
population were provided by the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development in connection with
the agency’s income-based housing and economic development programs. It should be noted that the
data presented may be inconsistent but this is inherent in the calculation formulas of the Census and
American Community Survey.

In 2000, the Village lost 8 percent of its population, largely due to the outmigration of residents to
surrounding rural communities. With a population below 10,000, the Village is eligible for several
programs offered by the USDA Rural Development that can provide assistance for the development of
infrastructure, necessary community facilities and economic development. Between 1990 and 2000, the
Village’s population rebounded slightly.

Potsdam’s population continues to become more diverse given its prominence as a center for advanced
university degrees and its growing role as a regional health care center. In 2000, 92 percent of the
Village’s population was white compared to 89 percent in 2010. Black/African American and Asian are
the largest racial minority groups residing in the Village constituting 3 percent and 5 percent of the
Village’s population respectively.
