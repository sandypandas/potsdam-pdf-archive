Lori,

The reason that I chose the higher of the two Polymer chemical bids
for the Wastewater Treatment Plant was due to it's ability to achieve
our percent solids requirements for landfill disposal. Bench testing
of each Polymer has been performed to determine the effectiveness
with our current sludge make-up. After bench testing is performed a
true test is performed during operation of our dewatering press. We
are currently approved by the NY State DEC for 4 Polymer products and
of these the one Polymer from Clean Waters has proven to outperform
the other three and allow us to meet our percent solids requirements
for land disposal within New York State.
