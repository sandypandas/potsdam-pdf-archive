Re: Rezone of H-2, and certain R-2 properties on Waverly St

As indicated on the submitted Short Environmental Assessment Form, this narrative describes the
proposed rezoning action as follows:

All existing properties presently zoned as H-2 and certain specific properties along the east side of
Waverly Street between Grove and Cottage Streets presently zoned R-2 are proposed to be rezoned to H-
1, as shown on the attached diagrams.

The intent of the proposed action is to maintain consistent development requirements on the lands owned
by the St Lawrence Health System at the Canton-Potsdam Hospital Leroy St site, and allow for orderly
redevelopment and growth of the existing facility at that location.

There will be no significant change to environmental resources as a result of the rezoning. The use of
existing Village and private utilities will be maintained, and changes to storm water runoff will be
managed on site per applicable regulations. Existing traffic patterns and flows will be maintained. The
ecology of the existing site will be unchanged and remain suburban in nature.
