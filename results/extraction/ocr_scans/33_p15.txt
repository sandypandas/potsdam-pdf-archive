Village Government Structure

The Village of Potsdam operates under a Board of Trustees/Village Administrator form of government.
The Village’s Mayor and its Board of Trustees are elected by the voting public. The powers and
responsibilities of the Mayor, Village Trustees, the Village Justice and other officers are outlined in §64
of the Code of the Village of Potsdam and Article 4 of the Consolidated Laws of New York State. The
Village Justice is also an elected position.

The Mayor and Trustees appoint a professional Village Administrator to manage the day-to-day activities
and operations of the government. The responsibilities of the Village Administrator are set-forth in §4 of
the Code of the Village of Potsdam.

Appointed positions include those department heads and their staff. These positions are filled by the
Village Board upon the recommendation of the Village Administrator. The department heads include:
