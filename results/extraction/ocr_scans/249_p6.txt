A Regular Meeting of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday, June 15,
2015 at 7:00 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, New York.

PRESENT: Mayor Yurgartis
Trustees Warr, Hopke, & Tischler

ALSO PRESENT: — Everett Basford, Village Administrator
, Lori S. Queor, Clerk/Treasurer
Greg Thompson, Code Officer
Kevin Bates, Police Chief
Fred Hanss, Planning Director
Justin Perry, Fire Chief
Tim Jerome, Fire

Mayor Yurgartis opened the meeting at 7:00 P.M., and stated that all members were present with
the exception of Trustee Garner

Mayor Yurgartis then stated that the following sets of minutes had been submitted for Board
approval:

May 29, 2015 Special Meeting 10:00 a.m.
June 1, 2015 Regular Meeting 3:00 p.m.
June 1, 2015 Executive Session 3:17 p.m.

A Motion was made by Trustee Tischler and Seconded by Trustee Warr to approve the minutes
as submitted. All were in favor, none opposed, motion carried.

Mayor’s comments: First Saturday will be September 5" this year. Market Street from Main to
Elm St. will be closed.

Trustees’ comments: Trustee Warr had no comments. Trustee Hopke asked about the travel
allowance for the Administrator. Administrator Basford stated that it is in the code that the
Administrator gets $100 a month for travel. He stated that he could track his mileage but that it
will be cumbersome. The Mayor stated that there is a mileage allowance for personal use of a
vehicle during village business and that is the mechanism he thinks should be used. Trustee
Hopke asked about the IT issues that personnel were having. Mayor Yurgartis stated that he
talked to Russ Nelson and he will work with personnel to retrieve archived emails. Trustee
Hopke asked about the AT & T and Verizon contracts. The Mayor stated that he is still working
on these. Trustee Tischler had no comments.

have the tank that holds sodium hydroxide at the Water Plant refurbished. It did not pass

digester tank needing cleaning. There is approximately five feet of sand and debris in the bottom
