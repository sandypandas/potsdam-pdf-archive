Trustee Warr Moved and Trustee Hopke Seconded a Resolution as follows:

WHEREAS, the Village of Potsdam (Village) is proposing the Water Pollution Control
Facility and Collection System Improvements Project (Project) in the Village of Potsdam,
St. Lawrence County, New York; and

WHEREAS, the Project has been classified as an “Unlisted Action” as determined in
accordance with the State Environmental Quality Review Act (SEQRA) in 6 NYCRR
Parts 617.4 and 617.6; and

WHEREAS, the Village caused a letter and Part 1 of a Full Environmental Assessment
Form (FEAF) to be sent to other potentially “Involved Agencies” (as this term is defined
in the SEQRA Regulations found at 6 NYCRR Part 617), indicating the Village’s desire
to serve as the “Lead Agency” (as this quoted term is defined in the SEQRA
Regulations), to complete a coordinated review of the Project (in accordance with 6
NYCRR Part 617.6); and

WHEREAS, a minimum of 30 calendar days were provided, and each of the potentially
Involved Agencies has agreed to, or raised no objections to, the Village of Potsdam
Village Board serving as Lead Agency for the Project:

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED AND DETERMINED, pursuant to and in
accordance with the coordinated review requirements of SEQRA, that the Village of
Potsdam Village Board establishes itself as Lead Agency for the Project; and it is further

RESOLVED, that the Village Board, as Lead Agency, will continue to progress the
SEQRA process associated with the Project, including the completion of Parts 2 and 3 of
the FEAF and declaring the Project’s Significance Determination by resolution at a future
meeting.
