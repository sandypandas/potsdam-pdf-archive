A Special Meeting of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Tuesday, January
12, 2016 at 51:00 p.m., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, New York.

PRESENT: Mayor Ron Tischler
Trustees Warr, Hopke & Sheehan

ALSO PRESENT: Everett Basford, Administrator
Lori Queor, Clerk/Treasurer
Andrew Silver, Village Attorney

Mayor Yurgartis opened the meeting at 5:00 P.M. and took the roll stating that all members were
present with the exception of Trustee Garner.

NEW BUSINESS:

Trustee Warr Moved and Trustee Hopke Seconded a resolution to go into Executive
Session for the discussion the employment history of a particular person and to see the
advice of counsel on those issues at 5:01 p.m.

Ayes 4 (Mayor Tischler) Nays 0
(Trustee Warr, Hopke & Sheehan)

Trustee Warr Moved and Trustee Hopke Seconded a resolution to adjourn the Executive
Session at 5:57 p.m.

Ayes 4 (Mayor Tischler) Nays 0
(Trustee Warr, Hopke & Sheehan)

The Board back in Special Session considered the following:

Trustee Warr Moved and Trustee Hopke seconded a resolution to accept, with regret, the
retirement of Cindy Vivlamore from the Village of Potsdam Planning & Code Offices
effective January 25, 2016.

Ayes 4 (Mayor Tischler) Nays 0
(Trustee Warr, Hopke & Sheehan)
