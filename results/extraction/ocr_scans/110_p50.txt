Dear Mr. Yurgartis:

| am writing this letter to solicit your interest in being the lead agency for a grant application to
the New York State Archives’ Local Government Records Management Improvement Fund
(LGRMIF) for regional Geographic Information Systems (GIS) implementation. Over the past five
years, the Authority has helped facilitate seven similar projects for 37 communities in St.
Lawrence, Jefferson, and Lewis counties and has obtained over $400,000 of grant funding to
develop GIS in the North Country.

The project we are proposing would involve mapping of water and wastewater infrastructure
for approximately six municipalities in St. Lawrence County. The LGRMIF does not have a co-
share; there is no cost to your community. This project’s objective is to convert hardcopy water
and wastewater records to electronic format and make them available in a secure, internet-
based GIS application. The application would be hosted by a consultant and similar in design to
one used by Development Authority and 37 other municipalities in the North County region;
you can view this application at www.dancgis.org/IMA. Communities we have worked with
have found significant value in developing a GiS with assistance from the Archives’ LGRMIF.
Uses include: improved access to mapping for clerks, DPW, Board members, assessors, and
engineers; ability to overlay infrastructure lines with county property data and aerial imagery;
line/valve/shutoff locating; creating and printing maps; etc.

The due date for the LGRMIF application is February 1, 2015 and several items need to be
completed in advance. Once the lead agency has been identified, letters need to be sent to
other St. Lawrence communities to solicit their interest in a partnership for the project.
Thereafter, hardcopy records at each participating community will be inventoried for a GIS
Needs Assessment, and a competitive RFP solicitation (facilitated by the lead agency) must be
completed to select a vendor to perform the record conversion to GIS. Finally, the grant
application needs to be prepared and submitted. The Authority will assist the lead agency with
records inventory, GIS Needs Assessment, and grant application preparation at no cost. The
lead agency will be requested to review and sign grant forms and select the GIS consultant.
