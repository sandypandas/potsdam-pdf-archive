In Figure 12, flood events were responsible for producing the most property damage in St.

Between the years 2006 and 2018, flooding produced the most damage of the three climate
hazards. Flooding damage produced costs of $5,802,094.54, while ice storms produced costs of
$2,062,438.69 and wind storms costs of $277,597.76. When comparing the frequency of all
three events to the previous time period, all frequency values have increased respectively
compared to the values in Figure 13. This illustrates how these storms over the selected time
period are becoming a larger threat to St. Lawrence County.
