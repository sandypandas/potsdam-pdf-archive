Trustee Lee Moved and Trustee Jacobs-Wilke Seconded a resolution to enter into an
agreement with the St. Lawrence County Health Initiative for $16,000 in grant funding
through its “Creating Healthy Communities” grant program to be used for the express
purpose of repair or replacement of sidewalks identified as substandard in the Village’s
sidewalk inventory; and, further authorizing the mayor or in his absence the deputy
mayor to execute the grant agreement with the Health Initiative.

Ayes 5 (Mayor Tischler) Nays 0
(Trustee Warr, Tirion, Jacobs- Wilke
& Lee)

Trustee Lee Moved and Trustee Jacobs-Wilke Seconded a resolution authorizing the
Village of Potsdam to apply for $50,000 in grant funding through the T-Mobile
Hometown Grant Program to be used for the express purpose of developing and
constructing a new pavilion on Urban Renewal Sub-Parcel III adjacent to Ives Park; and
further authorizing the mayor, or in his absence, the deputy mayor to execute the grant
application and any resulting grant agreements.

Ayes 5 (Mayor Tischler) Nays 0
(Trustee Warr, Tirion, Jacobs- Wilke
& Lee)
