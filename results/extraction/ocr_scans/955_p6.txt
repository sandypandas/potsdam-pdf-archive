LOCAL LAW #5-2021

Section 1. Purpose.
The Purpose of this Local Law is to Amend Chapter 180, Zoning, Article III, Section
180-6 Zoning Map of the Village of Potsdam Municipal Code.

Section 2. Amendment.

The zoning map shall be amended to change the zone designation for the hospital owned
properties on Grove, Cottage and Waverly Streets that are currently H-2 to H-1 and the
following properties on Waverly Street that are currently R-2 to H-1: 51 Waverly Street
(Tax Map #64.051-5-1), 49 Wavery Street (Tax Map #64.051-5-24), 47 Waverly Street
(Tax Map #64.051-5-25) and 45 Waverly Street (Tax Map #64.051-5-22), Potsdam, NY.

Section 3. Enactment.
This Local Law shall become effective immediately upon the notification of the filing of
same with the Secretary of State.
