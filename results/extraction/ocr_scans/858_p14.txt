Dear Board Member,

North Country This Week is excited to announce a new paid St. Lawrence County edition
and we expect to soon have a second class mailing permit that will allow us to accept
legal notice ads in 2021. We are asking you to consider designating North Country
This Week as one of your official newspapers.

Your legal notice published in the North Country This Week paid and free editions will
be seen over 21,000 times — many more than is now possible. The ads will also be
posted on NorthCountryNow.com! Please see attached flier for more details.

We suggest a daily newspaper also be designated as a second official paper, for two
reasons:

+ Should there occasionally not be enough time to wait for our weekly deadline to meet
publication date requirements, the daily would be available.

* We expect to obtain our U.S. Postal Service Second Class mailing permit by Jan. 1, but
if it is delayed, the daily paper would be available. For many types of legal ads, New
York State requires publications to have a Second Class permit.

The improved readership and receivership of your legal ads placed with North
Country This Week will now reach significantly more St. Lawrence County residents.
Legal notices will have an extended shelf life through our print editions, 24/7 access on
NCNOW and will keep your investment in St. Lawrence County. The revenue will help
ensure current level and potential future growth of local news reporting for the county.
With 21 employees, we are St. Lawrence County’s only locally-owned news outlet!

Please contact us should you have any questions. We would be glad to attend your
organizational meeting, in person or remotely. We look forward to helping you inform
county residents.

Thank you for your consideration,
