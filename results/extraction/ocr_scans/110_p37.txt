The COMMISSIONER or her representative may periodically inspect the highway
lighting system provided and installed under the above identified project nurnmber
to ascertain that the lighting system is being maintained in accordance with the
terms of this Agreement and in condition satisfactory to the COMMISSIONER.
The COMMISSIONER shall, in writing, notify the VILLAGE of any observed
deficiencies, listing such deficiencies, within thirty (80) days of receipt of such
notification, the COMMISSIONER or her representative shall arrange for a
meeting to be held with the authorized representative of the VILLAGE. At such
meeting the Commissioner or her representative and the authorized
representative of the VILLAGE shall discuss the means required to remedy the
noted deficiencies. Based on the discussion, and based on the nature of the
required remedial action, a reasonable time limit shall be mutually established by
the COMMISSIONER or her representative and the authorized representative of
the VILLAGE for the satisfactory completion of remedial action by the VILLAGE.

it is recognized by the parties hereto that failure of the VILLAGE to complete the
required remediai actions within the agreed upon time limit may subject the
VILLAGE to certain penalties. If ihe equipment supplied and installed by the
STATE for the above subject lighting system was done pursuant to a Federally-
aided and Federally-reimbursable contract, and the VILLAGE fails to make the
remedial actions within the agreed upon time limit, no further Federaily-aided
project for which the VILLAGE would have maintenance responsibility shall be
approved until such time as the lighting system is restored to the level and
conditions of maintenance required by the Agreement. in addition, failure of the
VILLAGE to make such remedial actions may subject the VILLAGE to loss of
State aid for other VILLAGE projects.

The VILLAGE agrees not to assign, transfer, convey, sublet or otherwise dispose

power to execute such agreement to any person, company, or corporation without
previous consent in writing of the COMMISSIONER, except as herein provided
and by Resolution attached hereto.

Prior to the expiration of the Agreement, the VILLAGE shall review the Agreement
and determine whether it desires to continue maintaining said lighting system for
an additional VILLAGE year period.

the expiration of the Agreement the VILLAGE Board of the VILLAGE shall, by
resolution, authorize the Mayor of the VILLAGE to enter into and execute an
Agreement with the STATE by and through the COMMISSIONER committing the
VILLAGE to maintain, at its own expense, said lighting system for a second
period of 20 years. The Mayor of the VILLAGE shall enter into such an
Agreement with the State prior to the termination of this Agreement.
