A Public Hearing of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Thursday, July 30,
2015 at 9:00 A.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, New York.

PRESENT: Mayor Yurgartis
Trustees Warr, Hopke & Garner

ALSO PRESENT: Everett Basford, Village Administrator
Lori Queor, Village Clerk/Treasurer
Fred Hanss, Planning Director
Mayor Yurgartis opened the hearing at 9:00 A.M. He stated that this public hearing is to receive
public comments on a proposed application for grant funding through the NYS Small Cities
CDBG Program for the renovations of the cross drain.
Village Clerk Lori Queor then read the Legal Notice as published.

The Mayor then asked for public comments.

Planning Director Fred Hanns gave the board an overview of the proposed application and
provided them with a project description which is on file with the Village Clerk.

Hearing no further public comment, the Mayor declared the hearing closed at 9:05 A.M.

Respectfully submitted,

Lori 8. Queor
Village Clerk
