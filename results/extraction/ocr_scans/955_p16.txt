Notes:

All permitted and specially permitted use require site plan review by the Village Planning Board.

Front setback: 25 feet or 15 feet with a buffer approved by the Village Planning Board. Frontage is the measured distance from one foot inside the sidewalk to a structure or facility.

Side setback: 20 feet one side, two sides 40 feet for structures that are adjacent to privately owned property.

Side setback: zero feet for adjacent properties that are owned by the same party.

Rear setback: 20 feet for structures or facilities that are adjacent to privately owned property.

Rear setback: zero feet for adjacent properties owned by the same party.

A maximum lot coverage percentage of 80% shall be allowed for all standard and special uses in the Health Care Zone - 1 if adequate stormwater prevention plan and snow storage areas are provided per the site plan review process.

All on-premise parking facilities shall comply with applicable sections of § 180-22.
