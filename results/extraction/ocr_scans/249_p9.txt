law to enact a six month moratorium on the development of commercial scaled photo
voltaic electric generating facilities within the Village of Potsdam pending the

Village of Potsdam Municipal Code.

Trustee Tischler Moved and Trustee Warr Seconded a resolution as follows:

Whereas, the Village of Potsdam, New York as lead agency in a Full Environmental
Assessment of a SEQRA review for the Runway 6/24 Obstruction Mitigation Project

environmental impacts and therefore there is no need to prepare an environmental impact
statement; and,

Whereas, the project will have no adverse impact, a negative declaration is issued by the
lead agency.

Now Therefore, Be it resolved that, the Village Board of Trustees has completed the
environmental review process and directs C & S Companies to file the SEQR Notice of
Negative Declaration with the New York State Department of Environmental
Conservation for publication in the Environmental Notice Bulletin.
