Trustee Moved and Trustee Seconded a resolution as follows:

Whereas, Clarkson University has submitted a petition for annexation of a real estate
parcel — 152 Maple Street - to the Village of Potsdam from the Town of Potsdam; and,
rezoning to the Village of Potsdam SCH Zoning district; and,

Whereas, Clarkson University has submitted as a part of its petition a State
Environmental Quality Review Act (SEQRA) Short Environmental Assessment Form;
and,

Whereas, the Village of Potsdam in its capacity as lead agency in the SEQRA review has
completed Parts 2 and 3 of the SEQRA Short Environmental Assessment Form and
determined that the proposed action is an Unlisted Action and that the annexation of the
subject parcels will not have any adverse impact on the Village’s natural or cultural
resources,

Now therefore be it Resolved, that the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees hereby
adopts a negative declaration for the SEQRA action, namely the annexation and rezoning

of the subject parcels.

AYES NAYS
