A Regular Meeting of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday, November
16, 2020 at 6:00 P.M., via Zoom Meeting due to the Coronavirus Pandemic.

PRESENT: Mayor Tischler
Trustees McKenna, Jacobs-Wilke & Lee

ALSO PRESENT: Lori Queor, Clerk/Treasurer
Trey Smutz, Recreation Director
Fred Hanss, Planning Director
Greg Thompson, Administrator

Mayor Tischler opened the meeting at 6:00 P.M.
The Mayor then stated that all members were present except for Trustee Warr.

The Mayor then stated that the following sets of minutes had been submitted for Board
acceptance:

November 2, 2020 Regular Meeting 6:00 p.m.
November 2, 2020 Executive Session 6:24 p.m.

Motion was made by Trustee Lee and Seconded by Trustee Jacobs-Wilke to accept the minutes
as submitted. All were in Favor — None Opposed — Motion Carried.

Mayor’s comments: Meeting via Zoom because of rise in COVID-19 19 cases. Remind public
to adhere to the guidelines — mask, social distance, limit large group gatherings.

Trustee’s Comments:

Trustee McKenna — Glad we are able to do the meeting via Zoom as her kids are quarantined
due to possible exposure at school. Take every precaution to keep our community safe and
follow the guidelines.

Trustee Jacobs-Wilke — Stay safe. Working through the Complete Streets process there are new
guidelines that will need to be added to our policy. Will be working through this. Glad new
fluoride system is up and running, glad we can support public health in that way.

Trustee Lee — None

Administrator’s Comments: Administrator Thompson provided the board with a written report
which is on file with the Village Clerk. He then went over his report with the board.

Recreation Director’s Report: Director Smutz provided the board with a written report which is
on file with the Village Clerk. He then touched on some of the highlights of the report and asked
if there were any questions. The Board had no questions. The Mayor and Administrator both
thanked Trey for the exception job he is doing at the arena especially under such trying times.
