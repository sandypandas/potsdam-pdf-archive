Flooding poses a threat to the Potsdam residents and economy. $340 million worth of edifices in
Potsdam are in danger from flooding with 27% of Potsdam’s structure value within the hazard
zone. Wind storms have caused major damage to property with wind speeds reaching 100MPH?
. In 2012 Potsdam experienced a damaging microburst with extensive damage to residential,
commercial, and institutional property. More data is needed for predictions of timing and risk
mitigation for microbursts in Upstate New York. Finally, ice storms are considered the most
hazardous and economically devastating climate event facing Potsdam. The Ice Storm of 1998
caused over $4 million worth of property damage. This event, as described in the
Potsdam-made documentary, Resilience, is why Potsdam is particularly attuned to climate
hazards in the North Country’. All three hazards will be discussed in depth concerning physical
and economic damages.

This document also outlines Potsdam’s climate and economic profile. The Village and Town of
Potsdam’s assets and ability to address these climate hazards are discussed at the conclusion
of this paper.
