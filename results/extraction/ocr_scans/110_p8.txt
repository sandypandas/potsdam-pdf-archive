AGENDA - REGULAR MEETING 8
VILLAGE OF POTSDAM BOARD OF TRUSTEES
MONDAY, NOVEMBER 17, 2014 — 7:00 P.M.

WHEREAS, a duly notice public hearing thereon was held in joint session by the Board of
Trustees of the village of Potsdam, New York and the Town Board of the Town of Potsdam,
New York at 6:15 p.m., Thursday, November 6, 2014 at the Town Offices, Elm Street, Potsdam,
New York, and

WHEREAS, no one appeared in opposition thereto and no one filed written objections to the
annexation by the said Village of Potsdam of such territory, and

WHEREAS, the Board of Trustees of the Village of Potsdam, New York duly adopted a
resolution finding the petition in compliance with the provisions of Article 17, General
Municipal Law, that the annexation of such territory was in the over-all public interest, and
approving the annexation as requested in said petition, a copy of which said resolution is attached
hereto and made a part hereof, it is hereby

Ordered that the annexation of the property hereinafter described to the Village of Potsdam, New
York, be and it hereby is approved:

Clarkson University Property:

Lawrence, State of New York lying southeasterly of Hatch Road being described as follows:

conveyed to the Village of Potsdam by Clarkson University by deed dated May 1, 2007 and recorded
