RE: Recommendation for Landscape Designer for the DRI Downtown Streetscape and Riverwalk Trail
Project

As you are aware, the Village received three responses to its Request for Proposal (RFP) for the DRI -
funded Downtown Streetscape and Riverwalk Trail Project in late October. Responses were submitted by:
Barton and Loguidice, Fisher Associates and LaBella Associates and were emailed to you on November 3,
2022. Over the last few weeks, Greg and I have carefully reviewed each of the proposals. The
respondent’s proposals were reviewed using the scoring criteria that was included in the RFP. Attached
for your review and use are the scoring sheets that Greg and I prepared for each of the respondents.

Based on our review of the proposals and using the RFP’s scoring criteria, Greg and I strongly recommend
that the Village Board of Trustees select LaBella Associates as its project designer. We believe that
LaBella Associates provides a common-sense approach to project design and construction administration.
LaBella was also the landscape designer for several successful Village projects including: Ives
Park/Veteran’s Memorial, Garner Park and design development documents for the streetscape projects
funded through previous Local Waterfront Revitalization Program grants for the Village.

LaBella will bring a strong team of sub-consultants to the project including:

e CLA Sites (Minority-Owned Business Enterprise) who developed landscape plans for the riverwalk
trail as part of the DRI strategic investment planning process,

e Jade-Stone Engineering (Woman-Owned Business Enterprise) who has prepared streetlighting
plans for Ives and Garner Park and the streetscape enhancement project designs as a sub-consultant
to LaBella.

e Aubertine and Currier, Land Surveyors, Architects and Engineers (Woman-Owned Business
Enterprise).
