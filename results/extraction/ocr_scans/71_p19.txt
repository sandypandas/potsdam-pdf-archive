WITNESSETH:

That in consideration of the sum of One Dollar, ($1.00) and other good and valuable
- consideration, the parties hereto hereby agree as follows:

1. Term - The Village hereby agrees to lease to Lessee, land space at the Potsdam Airport,
(Damon Field), Potsdam, New York, for the period from October 1, 2014 through May 31, 2015
to be occupied by Lessee’s airplane hangar structure used to park single or twin engine airplanes.

Zz. Premises and Rate

a. Lessee agrees to pay rental at the rate of $0.40 per square foot of ground space, based
on Six Thousand Six Hundred Twenty-Four (6,624) square feet for the period of this contract in
an amount of $2,649.60 per year, in advance.

b. Village shall have the right to designate the metes and bounds location of said ground rental
area and in its sole discretion may designate other areas at the end of said term in the event of a
renewal of this lease. As in the Site Plan Approved by the Planning Board on 9/4/14.

c. During the rental period the Lessee shall keep the hangar and premises in good
condition, fit for its designated purposes and shall repair same as necessary. Lessce shall not
allow the hangar or environs to become dilapidated and shall keep hangar neat and clean.
Hangar shall be constructed and maintained with such materials as will either be maintenance
free or of materials which shall be kept well painted in standard Kelly Green and maintained in a
uniform manner.

d. Unless the Lessee is in default on a material term of the lease, Lessee shall have the

right to renew on such terms as the parties may agree.
