AGENDA — REGULAR MEETING 5
VILLAGE OF POTSDAM BOARD OF TRUSTEES
MARCH 15, 2021 — 6:00 P.M.

Trustee Moved and Trustee Seconded a resolution proclaiming
April 2021 as Fair Housing Month in the Village of Potsdam, NY. The Village of
Potsdam is an equal opportunity community and does not discriminate against people
based upon their race, color, religion, national origin, age, gender, familial status,
orientation or veteran status. It encourages all engaged in the local housing industry,
including lenders, realtors, appraisers, property management agents and contractors to
adhere to federal, state and local Fair Housing Laws; and, encourages tenants and
homeowners to understand their Fair Housing rights and responsibilities.

Ayes Nays
