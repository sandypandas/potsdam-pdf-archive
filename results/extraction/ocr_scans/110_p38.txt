lf the VILLAGE, in its discretion, determines that it does not desire to maintain

Where the VILLAGE has no desire to maintain the lighting system for an
additional 20 years, said lighting fixtures will be removed by the STATE at the
VILLAGE’s expense, unless the STATE has funds available to maintain, repair
and energize said lighting system and the COMMISSIONER, in her discretion,
determines that such lighting is necessary for such State Highway. Upon
notification by the COMMISSIONER of the removal cost, the VILLAGE shail
reimburse the STATE the amount specified. The VILLAGE’s obligation to pay the
removal costs continues beyond the expiration of this Agreement.

The cost of removal includes but is not limited to review and upgrading of
roadway delineation features, including pavement markings, and any and all
penalties, fees, and/or other costs for unamortized fixtures which the STATE is
required to pay the Federal government.
