AGENDA - REGULAR MEETING 5
VILLAGE OF POTSDAM BOARD OF TRUSTEES
MONDAY, NOVEMBER 17, 2014 — 7:00 P.M.

Trustee _ Moved and Trustee Seconded a resolution authorizing
the Mayor to sign the New York State Department of Transportation Utility Work
Agreement for the NY Route 971U over Raquette River, PIN 7780.08 Project.

AYES NAYS

Trustee _ Moved and Trustee Seconded a resolution as follows:

WHEREAS, the State of New York Department of Transportation proposes to reconstruct
a State highway identified as NY Route 971U, Village of Potsdam, PIN 7780.08, County
of St. Lawrence, within the geographical jurisdiction of the Village of Potsdam

WHEREAS, the Village of Potsdam approved of such project and desires to have a
highway lighting system within its geographical jurisdiction, and

WHEREAS, the State of New York has agreed to provide as part of the project the
following items in connection with a street lighting system:

1, Underground duct system, including conduit, pull boxes, hand holes and drainage
pockets.
2. Foundation for light standards.

3. Light poles.

4. Luminaries, wiring, switches, ballasts, light meter cabinets and all other
components necessary to complete the lighting system.

Provided that the Village of Potsdam agrees to maintain, repair and energize such
highway lighting system for a period of 20 years or until such time as the Commissioner,
in her discretion, determines that such lighting and/or maintenance of such lighting
system is no longer necessary for such Highway.

NOW, THEREFORE, The Village Board, duly convened, does hereby

RESOLVE, that the Village of Potsdam approves of the above subject project; and it is
hereby further
