Village of Potsdam, New York, 2012-2022 Comprehensive Plan

As incredible as it may seem, the Village’s median household income declined by 19 percent between
2000 and 2010 while per capita income increased by 9 percent but saw a decline in households earning
less than $10,000; and, an increase in those earning between $15,000 and $24,999. This may be explained
by shifts in employment opportunities away from education to sectors of the economy that do not
typically pay high wages, increase in university populations and the fact that many low/moderate income
people reside in the Village. Presently, the Village offers 436 subsidized rental housing units (e.g. Mid-
Town Apartments, Evergreen Park, and 51-57 Market Street).
