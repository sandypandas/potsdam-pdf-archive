A Public Hearing of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday, July 6,
2015 at 2:45 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, New York.

PRESENT: Mayor Yurgartis
Trustees Hopke & Warr
ALSO PRESENT: Everett Basford, Village Administrator

Fred Hanss, Planning Director

Greg Thompson, Code Officer

Kevin Bates, Police Chief

Bruce Henderson, DPW Superintendent

Mayor Yurgartis opened the hearing at 2:45 P.M. He stated that this public hearing is to receive
public comments on a proposed Local Law to amend Chapter 180 Zoning to enact a six month
moratorium on the development of commercial scale photo-voltaic electricity generating
facilities. He then read the Legal Notice as published.

The Mayor then asked for public comments.

Planning Director Fred Hanns stated that he had received a couple comments in his office
regarding this issue. He said Alice McClure who just purchased 59 Market Street, which is
McDuff’s, was in our office a couple weeks ago and expressed an interest in putting a small p.v.
on the roof of her building. He stated that he also had some communication with Affinity
apartment developers and one of the things they had hoped to do is put in a small p.v. as an
accessory use to their apartments to generate power. He stated that under the moratorium you
have before you, both of those accessory uses would not be permitted. He stated that one of the
things the board may want to discuss today is that they may want to include language that would
not necessarily preclude those small commercial uses.

The board then had a brief discussion.
Hearing no further public comment, the Mayor declared the hearing closed at 2:55 P.M.

The undersigned was not in attendance at this meeting. Information was provided by Deputy
Village Clerk, Maureen Gaffney.
