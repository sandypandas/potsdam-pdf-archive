The Regular Meeting of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday, January
4, 2016 at 7:00 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, New York.

PRESENT: Mayor Tischler
Trustees Warr & Hopke

ALSO PRESENT: — Everett Basford, Village Administrator
Lori S. Queor, Village Clerk/Treasurer
Fred Hanss, Planning Director
Bob Henninger, WTP/WWTP Chief Operator
Kevin Bates, Police Chief
Greg Thompson, Code Enforcement Officer

Mayor Tischler opened the meeting at 7:00 P.M., and stated that all members were present with
the exception of Trustee Garner & Trustee Sheehan.

Mayor Tischler then stated that the following sets of minutes had been submitted for Board
acceptance:

December 21, 2015 Regular Meeting 7:00 p.m.
December 21, 2015 Executive Session 8:18 p.m.

Motion was made by Trustee Warr and Seconded by Trustee Hopke to accept the minutes as
submitted. All were in favor, none opposed, motion carried.

Mayor’s comments: None.
Trustees’ comments:

Trustee Warr had no comments. Trustee Hopke stated that the Sparks Project meeting is January
18, 2016 and that Fred’s office will be sending out a press release on this. She stated that the
agenda item on the I.T. proposals she would like more information about so she would like to
discuss that when we get to that item. She reported that the winter swim program under the
recreation department is going well and there is a plan to have lawnmower races at Pine Street
Arena during Summer Festival. Trustee Hopke said we will need to make sure everything is
ready regarding insurance, etc. before the Summer Festival.

Administrator’s Comments: Administrator Everett Basford reported to the board on the
following:

- West Dam Hydro #2 wehd/down because of a turbine issue. The plant was dewatered
and it was found that there was debris between the turbine blade and the housing. It
was cleared and the plant went back on line.

- Passero & Sheehan have firm dates for the closing of the airport ~ May 9, 2016
through July 13, 2016.
