Dear Ron:

This letter is to notify you, and the Board of my intent to retire from my position as Superintendent of
Public Works. My retirement will be effective at the close of business on 14 January 2022. It has been
my privilege to serve the residents of the Village for over 36 years. I wish to thank you and the trustees
for the support and cooperation I have received over the years.

I have been very fortunate to have worked with many fine people. I leave knowing the DPW will be in
good hands.

Thank you all again.

Sincerely,
