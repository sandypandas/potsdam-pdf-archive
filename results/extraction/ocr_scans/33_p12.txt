Unlike communities across New York State, the Village of Potsdam is a community comprised of tenants
and landlords. According to the Census and the American Community Survey, nearly three-quarters of
the Village’s existing housing stock is renter-occupied. Unlike many communities in St. Lawrence
County, the Village of Potsdam has pro-actively provided decent and affordable rental housing for
families and the elderly in rental projects such as Evergreen Park and the Mid-Town Apartments. The
Village contains 6 affordable housing projects containing 436 rental units, or 27 percent of the total
reported in the American Community Survey. The balance of rental units can be found in market-rate
rental housing projects such as the Meadow East Apartments or units that are principally leased to
university students.
