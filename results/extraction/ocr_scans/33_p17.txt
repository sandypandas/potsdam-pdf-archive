The Village General Fund is directly supported by Village tax revenues and helps pay the costs associated
with the majority of Village government including for example: police, public works, planning and
recreation. The Water, Sewer and Trash Funds are supported by the receipt of use fees and revenues are
restricted to offsetting expenses attributable to the provision of water, sanitary and trash removal. When
revenues generated by the Village’s hydro-electric plant are high, the Village Board may allocate excess
revenue from the Hydro-Electric Fund to offset capital costs in the General Fund, Water Fund or Sewer
Fund.

Citizen Participation

A critical element in the success or failure of Village government is the extent to which Village residents
regularly participate in the governmental process. Attendance at planning board meetings, attending a
public hearing or Village Board of Trustees’ meeting are all critical to the successful operation of Village
government in a transparent and accountable manner. Volunteering for other advisory groups interested
in beautification or recreation helps to make Potsdam an outstanding place to live, work, study and play.

Recommendations Municipal Government and Finance

MGF- 1 The Village Board of Trustees should continue its effort to improve the budget
format to include all costs under each budget category to provide the public with a clear picture of the true
costs for village services.

MGF -2 The Village Board of Trustees should continue efforts to minimize Village tax
increases through the following means:

a. Making capital investments with the greatest potential for long-term impact (such as reducing
high short-term maintenance and repair costs on public facilities by making necessary long-term
replacement investments).

b. Working with employees and insurers to control employee benefit costs.
c. Negotiating with the Town government to obtain equitable sharing of costs and services.

MGF-3 Continue to petition the Potsdam Town Council to relieve Village taxpayers from
payment of Sections 3 and 4 of the Town of Potsdam Highway Budget.

MGE - 4 The Village Board of Trustees should pursue opportunities to increase public awareness
of and participation in local government affairs. Possible strategies could include: .
a. Increasing the use of the Village website (www.vi.potsdam.ny.us) for disseminating useful
information.

b. Implementing a promotional campaign to increase public participation in local government
affairs.
