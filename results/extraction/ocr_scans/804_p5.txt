AGENDA — REGULAR MEETING

VILLAGE OF POTSDAM BOARD OF TRUSTEES

APRIL 20, 2020 — 6:00 P.M.

VIA ZOOM MEETING DUE TO COVID-19 EPIDEMIC

Trustee Moved and Trustee Seconded a resolution approving
the taxi rates for inside the Village of Potsdam limits of North Star Taxi (Ghulam Sabir)
for the period of May 1, 2020 through April 30, 2021 as follows:

From Canton, NY - $10.00 plus $2.30 per mile within the Village.

For distances longer than Canton or if in Potsdam already - $20.00 to sit in the car plus
$2.30 per mile.

Every stop costs $10.00 plus $1.00 a minute for waiting time.
More than two people in the car — 50% more charged.
If someone calls but fails to appear, they must pay 50% additional fare the next time.

Rates are from 7:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. After that, the charge is 50% extra.

Ayes Nays

Trustee Moved and Trustee Seconded a resolution to adjourn
the Regular Meeting of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees of April 6, 2020

at p.m.

Ayes Nays
