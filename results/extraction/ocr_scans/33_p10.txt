In 2000, nearly half of the Village’s population was employed in the education, health care and social
service sector however by 2010 the percentage share of residents employed in this sector declined to only
35 percent. In 2010, the majority of Village residents were employed in the retail sector or in the art,
entertainment, recreation, accommodations and food service sector. Despite the decline in the education,
health and social service jobs held by residents, this sector continues to be the Village’s top employment
sector. With the anticipated growth at Canton-Potsdam Hospital it is likely that employment in the health
care sector will remain stable or trend up.
