OTHER:

Jim Corbett inquired as to why there was a proposal to change the
currently owned BLI-2 property, owned by Chazen, to an R-1. He would like
explanation as to why we would not just leave it as it is. Further, he
mentioned that if we rezone this location to R-1 then no solar development
can take place.

Ted Prahl replied that he believed there was no malice in this move. There
is already too many vacancies among the properties within the R-3 zone
and that in changing this location to R-3 we would only increase an already
obvious problem.

Hugo Atteman reminded everyone of the upcoming moratorium vote. He
felt that perhaps we should delay any zoning changes until such time as the
moratorium has expired.
