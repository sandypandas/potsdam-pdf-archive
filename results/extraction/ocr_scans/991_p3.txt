A Special Meeting of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday, January
24, 2022 at 3:00 p.m., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, New York.

PRESENT: Mayor Reinhold Tischler
Trustees Warr, Tirion & Jacobs- Wilke

ALSO PRESENT: Lori S. Queor, Village Clerk/Registrar
Greg Thompson, Village Administrator

Mayor Tischler opened the meeting at 3:00 p.m., took the roll and stated that all members
were present except for Trustee Lee. Trustee Lee joined the meeting by phone, but trustees
cannot vote by phone, you have to be able to physically see the person. The Administrator
could not get the ZOOM platform to work.

NEW BUSINESS:

Trustee Jacobs- Wilke Moved and Trustee Tirion Seconded a resolution to go into
Executive Session for the discussion of the employment history of particular persons at
3:02 p.m.

Ayes 4 (Mayor Tischler) Nays 0
(Trustee Warr, Tirion & Jacobs-Wilke)

Trustee Wilke Moved and Trustee Tirion Seconded a resolution to adjourn the Executive
Session at 3:38 p.m.

Ayes 4 (Mayor Tischler) Nays 0
(Trustee Warr, Tirion & Jacobs-Wilke)

The Board back in Regular Session considered the following:
Note: The Village Clerk was not present and this information is being provided to her by
the Mayor. Resolutions were prepared by the Village Administrator.

Trustee Jacobs-Wilke Moved and Trustee Warr Seconded a resolution to promote Audie
Jerome to the position of Public Works Crewleader 1 effective 01/24/2022 at an hourly
rate of $30.65 as per CSEA Union Contract.

Yes 4 (Mayor Tischler) No 0
