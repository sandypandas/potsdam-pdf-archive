AGENDA — REGULAR MEETING 4
VILLAGE OF POTSDAM BOARD OF TRUSTEES
JANUARY 18, 2016 — 7:00 P.M.

Trustee Moved and Trustee seconded a resolution as follows:

WHEREAS, Clarkson University submitted an Annexation Petition with an attached
annexation map dated November 18, 2015 to the Clerk of the Town of Potsdam and the
Clerk of the Village of Potsdam; and

WHEREAS, said application is for the annexation of a parcel of land owned
by Clarkson University, identified by St. Lawrence County Tax Identification No. 64.065-
5-1 (152 Maple Street) from the Town of Potsdam into the Village of Potsdam; and

WHEREAS, in conjunction with the submitted annexation petition, the petitioners also
submitted Part I of the New York State Environmental Assessment Form (SEAF); and

WHEREAS, the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees determined that there would be
two involved agencies in this action, the Village of Potsdam and the Town of Potsdam:
and

WHEREAS, the Potsdam Town Council has no objection to the Village of Potsdam
Board of Trustees being lead agency in this action; and

WHEREAS, on January 12, 2016 the Town and Village of Potsdam held a Joint Public
Hearing and took public testimony, comment or objections to the proposed annexation;
and

WHEREAS, the Village Board having determined (a) the Petitioner is the sole owner
of all of the real property described in the Petition according to the last preceding
assessment role of the Town of Potsdam and the assessor’s certification (b) the
Petitioner is duly qualified to sign the Petition, (c) the Petition substantially complies in
form and content with Article 17 of the General Municipal Law; and (d) the proposed
annexation is in the overall public interest;

NOW, THEREFORE, upon the evidence given and upon all the prior proceedings and
public hearing it is

RESOLVED AND DETERMINED, that the relief requested in the Petition is hereby
granted in all respects and the real property, as described in the Petition, shall be hereby
annexed from the Town of Potsdam into the Village of Potsdam; the relevant portion of
the Official Map of the Town of Potsdam shall be so amended at no expense to the Town
of Potsdam; the Map shall be filed in the Office of the Clerk of the Village of Potsdam
