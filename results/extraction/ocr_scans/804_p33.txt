The Crosstown Canal in its current state remains a liability for the Village of Potsdam and a
major danger in the case of a flooding storm event. It has been recommended that the Village
and Town of Potsdam strongly consider installing stormwater storage areas and that the Village
replace the roof of the canal. This project will cost approximately $15,400,000, but will help
protect and improve the lives of Potsdam residents. (EDR Engineers Report on Crosstown
Canal)
