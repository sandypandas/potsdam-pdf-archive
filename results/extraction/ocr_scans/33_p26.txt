Village of Potsdam, New York, 2012-2022 Comprehensive Plan

PFS-24 In conjunction with the Police Department, establish a Local Emergency Planning
Committee to prepare for, and coordinate services during local catastrophic emergencies. The committee
should include representatives of the universities, hospital, the Potsdam Town Council and Town outside
emergency services representatives. Grant money for emergency services should be sought.

PES-25 The Village Board of Trustees should encourage the Fire Department to work
cooperatively with other fire departments in the region to establish cost effective methods for attracting
and retaining volunteer firefighters.

PES-26 The Fire Department and the Planning and Development Office should develop a plan
and financing strategy to relieve the Fire Department’s building space limits.

PFS-27 The Village Board of Trustees should encourage the Potsdam Fire Department to
optimize coordination of services where ever possible with other fire departments in the Town.

PFS-28 The. Village Administrator and Clerk/Treasurer should study having the Town of
Potsdam contribute to and use the services of the Fire Department’s paid drivers; and, submit the study

to the Village Board of Trustees.

PFS-29 Encourage the Fire Department and the Town and Village of Potsdam to investigate the
implications of forming a Fire District.

Potsdam Damon Field Airport Recommendations

PFS-30 Concentrate additional capital improvements on maintaining and improving existing
facilities.
PFS-31 The Village Board of Trustees should carefully review and adopt a Master Plan guiding

future airport development and utilization. Expansion of the airport facilities should occur only after a
careful cost/benefit analysis.

The Village Civic Center Recommendations

PFS-32 The Department of Public Works should continue the capital improvements and
maintenance plan, in particular to improve storm water drainage and grounds maintenance.

PFS-33 The Planning and Development Office should submit an application to have the Village
Civic Center listed on the National Register of Historic Places and thereby qualify the Village to secure

state and federal support for building renovations.

PFS-34 Install an improved ventilation system to handle summer heat, particularly in the area
used by the library.
