A Regular Meeting of the Village of Potsdam Board of Trustees was held on Monday, July 20,
2015 at 7:00 P.M., in the Civic Center Board Room, Park Street, Potsdam, New York.

PRESENT: Mayor Yurgartis
Trustees Hopke & Tischler

ALSO PRESENT: — Everett Basford, Village Administrator
Lori S. Queor, Clerk/Treasurer
Greg Thompson, Code Officer
Fred Hanss, Planning Director
Bob Henninger, WITP/WWTP Chief Operator
Tim Carey, Recreation Director
Mimi VanDeusen, Museum Director

Mayor Yurgartis opened the meeting at 7:00 P.M., and stated that all members were present with
the exception of Trustees Warr & Garner.

No minutes were available for approval.
Mayor’s comments: None

Trustees’ comments: Trustee Hopke wants an audit committee. Trustee Tischler had no
comments.

Administrator’s Comments: Administrator Everett Basford reported to the board on the
following items:

- Bill Swift is retiring from the Sewer Plant after 40 years effective July 31°. He stated
that we wish him the best in his retirement. He stated that we will need to canvass to fill
the position. He stated that unofficially another employee from the sewer plant will be
retiring next month and we will need to fill that position also.

- Tom Peet of Pinto’s office is a little ahead this year on the audit. He stated that the
preliminary numbers do not look good with a deficit in a couple funds, especially the
hydro with the issues we have been having and it looks like the general fund will break
even but the fund balance will be reduced.

- July 15" the $500,000 Revenue Anticipation Note payment was made.

- Comparing last year’s cash position to this year’s we are a little behind so he will be
tracking that regularly throughout the year and we may have to borrow next spring.

- Working with computer consultants to get a handle on our overall computer system. This
may take some time as they need to learn the way our system is set up.

- Environmental Assessment grant at the airport is waiting for the Blanding Turtle survey.

- Runway project grant has been reviewed by the FAA and the comments are favorable.
We are just waiting for the Federal Government to distribute the funds.
