IV. FUTURE TRAFFIC AND SAFETY ASSESSMENTS

The future year traffic analysis includes three major elements: Traffic Forecasts; Traffic
Operations, and Traffic Safety. For each, the No-Build and Build conditions are compared for
2034 (estimated time of completion [ETC]) and 2064 (ETC+30 years). The No-Build scenario
assumes no significant corridor level improvements are made over the planning horizon, while
the Build scenario assumes the construction of a new four-lane divided freeway facility. The
interchanges for the Build scenario are presented in Table 12.

The traffic forecasts were completed using a spreadsheet-based model and diversion estimates
for parallel routes. The operational evaluation compares high-level operational characteristics,
including an estimate of annual travel time saved for all vehicles and heavy vehicles only. The
safety assessment compares the safety characteristics of the two scenarios based on roadway type

and vehicle exposure (vehicle miles traveled).
A. Traffic Projections

Figure 9 shows a possible conceptual alignment for a new Watertown to Plattsburgh freeway
with potential interchange locations as listed in Table 12. The figure also highlights a number of
origin-destination points across the North Country that would be served by this new facility.

The traffic volumes along the corridors that run parallel to the new highway were examined. For
each general section, a cut line was established between the two sequential origin-destination
points. This cut line was placed where the traffic volume was lowest so as to estimate the
maximum through flow between the two points. Traffic along the proposed alignment includes
diverted traffic from intermediate roads and developments in addition to traffic diverted from
Route 11. As shown in Figures 10 and 11, these cut lines were established both along the

Route 11 corridor and along the SR 37 corridor.
