I. INTRODUCTION
A. Corridor Location

The North Country Access Improvements (the study) is a transportation scoping study for the
New York State Route 11 corridor (Route 11). This technical memorandum is prepared for a
proposed new roadway alternative built to Interstate standards on a new alignment between the
City of Watertown, located in Jefferson County, New York, and the City of Plattsburgh, located
in Clinton County, New York. .

B. Evolution of Current Study

The North Country Transportation Study (2002) recommended an east-west highway based
solution as the first priority strategy for improving transportation and economy of the region.
The Northern Tier Expressway: Route 11 Corridor Study (2008) proposed a 20 year schedule for
phased implementation of the highway strategy. The current North Country Access
Improvements Study will develop a scoping report for the highway segment between Canton and
Potsdam based on this recommendation.

This technical memorandum was developed to confirm the findings of the prior reports for the
Route 11 corridor and to ensure that the development of Canton Potsdam segment of this
corridor is advanced within the context of the larger Route 11 corridor.

C. Purpose and Need

The following areas of concern demonstrate the need for the study:

e Lack of major east-west connections: There are no major east-west connections to
accommodate greater freight mobility between ports and border crossings located north
and east of the study corridor and market destinations west of the study corridor.

e Safety: Under existing conditions, trucks traversing Route 11 are forced to pass through
village centers, resulting in conflicts with other transportation modes and roadway users,
including school buses, pedestrians, bicyclists, residents, commuters and shoppers.

© Quality of Life: Large trucks generate noise and degrade ambient air quality, adversely
affecting the quality of life of village residents.

¢ Operational Concerns: The existing Route 11 alignment does not provide opportunities
for passing vehicles, which results in delays and increased travel time. In addition, no
redundant capacity is available during winter storms and other emergencies.

Based on the need established above, the purpose of the study is to:

e Improve Safety, Quality of Life, Roadway Operations, and Freight Mobility: Develop

roadway alternatives that avoid conflicts with other transportation modes and roadway
users in village centers, and improve freight mobility.

e Economic Development: Evaluate the economic development potential and benefits of
the proposed alternatives to inform the decision-making process.
