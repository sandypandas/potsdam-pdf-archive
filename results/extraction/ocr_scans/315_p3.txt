Other Buisness:

1) CPH

Jon Huckle and Brooks Washburn were present for preliminary discussion

of a proposed parking lot at 19 Cottage Street.

- The concept is to demolish the structure at 19 Cottage and create an
extension of the existing parking lot.

Brooks Washburn explained that this is land owned by the hospital and is
currently zoned H-1. Greg Thompson further explained that onsite parking
is a standard use in the H-1 zone.

Brooks Washburn explained that at this time CPH feels this is the best use
for the property, given the pre-existing parking problems which exist. Fred
Hanss gave a brief background in regards to the parking issue currently
being faced on Waverly Street.

David Trithart inquired as to whether or not the Village has taken any
official action on the parking issue. Fred Hanss explained that a draft local
law has been created, however it has not had action taken on it.

Hugo Atteman asked whether a second curb cut would be needed or if the
hospital would use the pre-existing curb cut. Brooks explained that the
hospital has at this time proposed using the just the pre-existing curb cut.
He further explained that all storm water would be handled on site per
requirements.

Will Siegfried asked about buffering between the parking area and the
parcel which adjoins 19 Cottage. Mr. Washburn confirmed that there would
be vegetative buffering put in place and local set backs will be followed as
well.

Fred Hanss explained the intended purpose of the H-1 zone.
