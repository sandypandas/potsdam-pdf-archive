AGENDA — REGULAR MEETING 5
VILLAGE OF POTSDAM BOARD OF TRUSTEES
MONDAY ~ AUGUST 22, 2016 — 7:00 P.M.

Trustee Moved and Trustee Seconded a resolution to classify
the proposed Cross Town Canal Rehabilitation Project, NYSCDBG Project # 947PR72-
15 as a Categorically Excluded as defined in 24 CFR Part 58 (a).

Ayes Nays

Trustee Moved and Trustee Seconded a resolution authorizing
the Village Clerk-Treasurer to Publish a Notice of Intent to Request Release of Funds in
the Village of Potsdam’s Newspaper of Record upon the conclusion of the agency
comment period and completion of the Environmental Review Record for the Cross
Town Canal Project NYSCDBG # 947PR72-15.

Ayes Nays

Trustee Moved and Trustee Seconded a resolution authorizing
the Village of Potsdam to submit an application for funding through the Restore New
York Communities Program on behalf of Clarkson University, grant funds to be applied
toward the adaptive re-use and rehabilitation of Congdon Hall.

Ayes Nays

Trustee Moved and Trustee Seconded a resolution authorizing
the Director of Planning and Development to attend the New York State Rural Housing
Coalition Annual Conference scheduled for September 14-16, 2016 including conference
fees, travel, lodging and meals. Funds to come from the Planning and Development
Office travel & training line.

Ayes Nays

Trustee Moved and Trustee Seconded a resolution authorizing
the Director of Planning and Development to attend the Local Government Conference at
SUNY Potsdam on October 11, 2016. Funds to come from the Planning and

Development Office travel & training line.

Ayes Nays
